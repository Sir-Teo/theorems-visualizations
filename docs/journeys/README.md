## Concrete reader tasks

**Water:** distinguish height, capacity and nominal stress; separate a plotted count from a population rate; explain why a falling curve does not alone establish an intervention's effect; identify a planning goal and an unheard group in a fictional proposal. The [EPA reading](https://www.epa.gov/green-infrastructure/community-planning-and-green-infrastructure) is scoped to contemporary US green-infrastructure planning, not evidence about historical London or a legal compliance determination.

**Cloth:** test a non-square rectangle's symmetry, explain what an over-under matrix records, and identify the additional evidence needed to move from one soil observation or a two-good trade model to a particular textile. The [earlier Asare brief](../pilots/sequences/p07-03-kente.md) retains the named provider's perspective and its limits. No new image, course, pattern, loom or physical sample was inspected. The legacy loom article's broader historical/material claims have not been newly audited here.

**Account:** attach a claim to a passage, distinguish copied reports from independent observations, and name the unobserved alternative in a causal question. [NARA's orientation](https://www.archives.gov/education/lessons/worksheets) and [Reuters' stated sourcing practice](https://reutersagency.com/about/standards-values/) are external reading routes. The [legal follow-up brief](../pilots/sequences/p04-02-evidence.md) and [professional preparation](../pilots/professional-review/law.md) identify work still required; neither is linked as an accepted lesson or a legal answer key.

## Connections, scope and rights

Every transition has a reason and a limit. Two are **analogies**; nine are **question shifts**. There are no new prerequisite or evidential edges. Source support for a bounded statement is different from a connection between lessons. The v1 validator rejects attempts to relabel sequence order as evidence or prerequisite; a future contract must validate the actual claim/locator or dependency before allowing those edges.

Entry explanations support the small conceptual tasks. They do not replace full prerequisite study for derivations. The target table retains each lesson's declared prerequisites; formal/deep routes must preserve that closure and cannot be advertised as beginner-complete. Proposed checks are editorial rubrics, not observed learner results.

Only links and original editorial prose are added. The source material and reviewed lesson payloads stay in their existing locations and under their existing terms. This preparation neither copies external media nor grants public distribution. An accepted legacy roster entry is not a fresh specialist source audit. A locally accepted exploration is not a published lesson.

The UN-Water indicator page and SPJ ethics-code page returned **403**. They are recorded as stopped candidates, excluded from the source claims and not retried through alternate copies. Search snippets do not substitute for body reads. EPA and Reuters are different works, with their own narrower scope; neither is a recovered copy of a stopped page.

A release output may omit locally accepted editions; unavailable or withdrawn targets must not produce a promise or content leak. No source lesson, note store or existing bridge needs replacement.
3. Keep an external reading visibly distinct from an atlas lesson. Render the law gap without a lesson URL or completion checkbox. Do not use P07/P08 to fill it or silently count any research stop among the 24 pilot lessons.
4. Verify root and project-prefix links, no-JavaScript reading, keyboard order, narrow layouts and gap/withdrawal behavior on the composed candidate. Test native navigation only within the shared browser limit. Record actual human observations only when performed.
5. Accept a route only against its final content and output. Resolve its release decision separately. Extend the missing situated/practical/professional work under the original pilot contracts and draft limit; this planning package does not waive them.
