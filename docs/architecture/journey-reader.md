# Cross-field journey reader

The reader composes after [domain navigation](domain-navigation.md). Its source is the unchanged [research contract](../journeys/README.md), not a new collection or exploration. Preview exposes three routes with ten accepted lesson links, three external-reading stops and one legal gap. It records no lesson completion, notes or mastery.

## Delivered paths and tasks

`journeys/` lists available routes. `journeys/water-city/`, `journeys/woven-cloth/` and `journeys/account-evidence/` contain questions, bounded tasks, entry explanations, native reasoning disclosures, transition limits and source scopes. `journeys/about/` explains the distinctions; `journeys/catalog.json` exposes the same availability and typed stops. One self-contained stylesheet supports system light/dark themes, phone layouts, keyboard focus and print. No new JavaScript is delivered to the browser.

One guarded Asare instruction adaptation replaces the plan’s authoring-brief reference with the retained source descriptions actually delivered below the task; its question and substantive distinctions stay unchanged. An unexpected source-instruction revision fails closed.

The legal gap shows its question and limit without the planning document's internal authoring task/rubric. It has no lesson destination or completion control. Available field orientations remain optional links. Exact reviewed source dates, locators, findings, limitations and reuse boundaries remain inspectable on each available route; external media/texts are not copied.

## Availability and withdrawal

Preparation revalidates the accepted legacy projection and retained exploration bundles/runtime/reviews. Assembly then resolves targets against the actual output's accepted static snapshot, files and edition catalog. A newer published exploration never substitutes for the pinned edition. Unknown state/path contracts fail closed.

If any required target is missing or withdrawn, the whole route becomes an identity-only unavailable page. Tasks, title, outcomes, sources, transitions and target links are withheld from the page and catalog, because a narrative could paraphrase its target outside the target's own stop. Independently withdrawn journeys similarly retain only identity/version/reason. Available neighbors remain listed; absent or withdrawn field orientations supply no active field link.

This policy operates on a fresh output. It does not erase an old deployment, cache, downloaded page or user's browser history. Production withdrawal requires actual rollout/cache work. The legal gap is explicitly authored as a gap and does not trigger the required-target rule.

## Release and review bindings

The real `journeys/releases.json` is empty. Preview has no canonical URLs; an empty release emits no journey paths, assets or navigation links. Synthetic tests exercise partial publication, target unavailability and withdrawal; their receipts are not real authorizations.

A future publication decision binds selected route revisions, all plan/source/target bytes, the complete composed delivery inputs and canonical destination. Exact per-material scope, territories and independent evidence are required for selected external readings, original prose and software; separate scoped content, access and engineering evidence and an explicit open-check disposition are required. Shape validation checks recorded evidence and scope, not credentials or whether observations actually occurred. An actual decision must settle the existing substantive review conditions honestly. It cannot grant permission to the linked lessons, which have their own delivery decisions.

Withdrawal requires a prior publication. A withdrawn route revision cannot be republished by reusing its event; a new reviewed revision needs explicit ledger migration. The current contract is scoped to the three frozen route plans, not an immutable archive for every future revision.

Research and delivery bindings include the prior navigation/edition inputs, route planning/retained-source records, original planning review receipts and reader code/style. Release evidence is hash-checked separately to avoid self-reference. Assembly rechecks bindings and decisions before success.

## Preservation and integration

The builder requires a fresh output outside the checkout through the existing predecessor builder. It adds seven files and five guarded entry links in preview: root, reading index, exploration index, fields index and notebook manager. Existing lesson pages, content/data, note stores and original source files remain unchanged. A partial release adapts only entry pages actually present; canonical sitemap additions cover available released journeys only.

The [13-file additive package](../integration/journey-reader.md) and its archive rehearsal preserve the whole committed predecessor. Runtime/rendered assertions remain separate from actual reader/screen-reader sessions and from route acceptance. The master-plan goal remains broader than this candidate.
