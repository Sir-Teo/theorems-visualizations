# Law and health preparation packets

Version 0.1.0, 22 October 2026. These are concrete review inputs for P04/P06, **not authored lessons, runtime contracts or qualified approvals**. They leave P07/P08 in the two existing unaccepted slots. No person has been recruited, contacted or observed.

- [Law packet](law.md): ten original fact/branch cards, a statutory authority map, nine calendar fixtures and a precise outstanding-effects/commencement review request.
- [Health packet](health.md): a bounded phase/claim map, two original synthetic traces, nine boundary-counting fixtures and a proposed observation/transfer rubric for clinician review.
- [Source receipts](sources.json): eleven scoped readings, with exact local hashes for eight official statutory pages; fresh OGL 403 and OpenStax login stops are recorded separately. Previously stopped nursing/spirometry sources remain closed.
- Independent AI desk reviews: [law](review-law.md), [health/numeric](review-health.md), [structure/arithmetic](review-structure.md). These do not fill any qualified role.

Run `python3 docs/pilots/professional-review/audit.py` from the repository. It checks the supplied arithmetic, source references and unchanged preparation status. It does not determine rights, clinical status, actual source currency, credentials or release readiness. The inputs contain only fictional cases and authored times; there is no personal-case/patient input or publishing code.

## Required decision record before authoring

The responsible qualified reviewer must identify their actual role/qualification, date, conflicts, the exact packet/file hashes inspected and the limits of their review. They must answer every packet item as approved, changed, outside competence or unresolved, with reasons and precise authority. Missing or unresolved answers remain open; an AI report cannot populate this record as a qualified approval.

For law, approve one common legal-effective snapshot and complete branch scope. For health, approve the phase model, counting terminology and proposed rubric, including every inference it withholds. Changes require a new packet revision and renewed affected review. After that, wait for a draft slot, author the actual lesson and perform exact-artifact acceptance and applicable human checks. A preparation decision is not final lesson acceptance.

The older pilot briefs and sequence receipts are historical inputs, unchanged by this package. These new packets refine preparation; they do not silently rebind those documents' independent reviews. [Integration scope](../../integration/professional-preparation.md) and [verification](../../verification/universal-professional-preparation.md) record the additive boundary and actual checks.
