# P08-L03 · A play, an acting manual and a performance event

Research brief, 2026-10-22. Parent: P08 performance. Audience: readers learning to distinguish evidence about a situated theatre practice. Planned only; no performance reconstruction, translated manual or gesture instruction. Sources `seq-ammannur-history` and `seq-ammannur-plays` are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).

## Question and evidence

Does identifying a play tell us what happened in a particular performance? The [Ammannur Chachu Chakyar Smaraka Gurukulam's institutional history](https://www.ammannurgurukulam.com/about.htm) describes family teaching, subsequent students and a training/performing institution. Its [homepage](https://www.ammannurgurukulam.com/) dates the organisation's beginning to 27 October 1982. These are the institution's historical accounts. Undated prose, old festival listings and incomplete biography text elsewhere on the site prevent treating its staff or training claims as verified current information.

The [plays page](https://www.ammannurgurukulam.com/plays.htm) distinguishes named plays/acts and attributed acting manuals or direction. It lists **Asokavanikamkam**, fifth act of **Ashcharyachoodamani**, attributed there to Mahakavi Sakthibhadra, with a sixteen-day description. That listing is not an inspected event record or a universal runtime rule. The page separately distinguishes a stage manual from an acting manual in some later entries. Those labels help frame a source question but do not substitute for reading the manuals themselves.

The proposed context is this institution's Ammannur-school account and its Asokavanikamkam entry. English spellings are retained as source labels pending specialist checking. No Sanskrit/Malayalam text, manual passage, performance recording or live performance was read or watched. The training page supplied no curriculum text; a workshop PDF timed out. The UNESCO Kutiyattam page presented CAPTCHA and a separate artist biography PDF timed out. No blocked source was recovered or counted as evidence.

## Planned exploration

Build an evidence chain with distinct cards for the dramatic work/act, a particular manual, a documented performance event and a viewer's interpretation. At present only the institutional listing is available; other cards are explicitly missing. Readers identify what new source would answer a question about duration, an enacted passage, a movement or an interpretation, instead of transferring a website's general statement to every performance.

Before authoring the second stage, select one authorised event segment with a practitioner or knowledgeable participant, its date/place and relevant textual/manual locator. Confirm the relation between that specific segment and the chosen act; do not assume every recording labelled Kutiyattam supplies it. Provide reviewed language/transliteration choices, credits, contextual notes, transcript and descriptions with uncertainties retained. A short educational excerpt is not a replacement for the whole event or training tradition.

State: source type, selected claim, locator, attribution and missing link. Offer a linear evidence list as well as any sequence map. The reader's task is to justify the scope of a claim, not imitate a gesture, grade religious authenticity or infer a fixed emotion from an isolated face/hand position. Comparison with P08-L02 concerns evidence types; it does not equate the traditions' performance categories.

## Preparation gates and release

Practitioner/context and Sanskrit/Malayalam-capable review, current institutional scope, a usable event/text selection and explicit media/translation permissions remain open. The site states all rights reserved; public access does not permit copying photographs, manuals or recordings. Links and original commentary are the present release scope. No contact, endorsement or consent is claimed.

Actual performance, learner/access observations and final asset review must be recorded when conducted. Separate English-source AI review can check the present research boundary; it cannot supply those roles or inherit the removed P01/P02 first-lesson human requirements.
