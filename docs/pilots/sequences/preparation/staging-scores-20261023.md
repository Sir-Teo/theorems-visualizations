## Observation and analysis, when evidence exists

The packet offers both presentation orders, facing→offset and offset→facing, while leaving assignment null. Record actual order, viewpoint and access mode. Different orders can expose an order issue; counterbalancing alone does not remove familiarity or carryover. A small comparison cannot establish population effects or universal gesture meanings.
