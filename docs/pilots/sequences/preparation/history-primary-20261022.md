## What the preparation resolves

Document 39 supplies a contemporary Mexican diplomatic communication, which the earlier retrospective Mendoza account could not supply. It proposes a peace invitation or mediation, followed by collective restrictions on supplies and commerce if peace cannot be restored. It also presents neutral cooperation as a means for Mexico to act internationally. These are statements of policy and diplomatic self-presentation; they establish neither implementation nor a response to the German alliance proposal.

Document 300 makes a particularly useful distinction between reported speech and the reporter's inference. Fletcher reports Carranza's assertion of nonreceipt, then characterizes his answer to a hypothetical alliance question as avoiding direct rejection. Fletcher subsequently offers his own prediction and explanation of Carranza's motive. Those three kinds of claim must remain separate. Fletcher's views are not an independently documented Mexican decision. The packet does not settle what Carranza actually received, intended or decided.

The records also expose dependence: document 59 responds to the initiative transmitted in document 39, and document 300 discusses that initiative. They are not three independent Mexican voices. The German draft, transmitted telegram, presidential use and Mendoza's later interpretation remain separate witnesses under the original brief's limits. This packet does not repair or replace their source records.

## Concrete next exploration

The dossier specifies five activities with evidence IDs, expected distinctions and unsupported answers to reject:

1. Trace Carranza → Aguilar → De Negri → unnamed translation → the 1931 edition, without assigning an unverified translation date or translator.
2. Separate the proposed sequence of neutral action from any claim that it was implemented or answered Germany.
3. Label reported nonreceipt, a reported conditional answer and Fletcher's explicit inference; explain why their evidentiary force differs.
4. Identify the direction and subject of Lansing's response.
5. Identify dependent testimony and propose an authenticated missing witness that would change a particular claim.
