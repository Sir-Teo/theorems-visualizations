## Question and evidence

What makes silence permissible, or a form of concealment? Read [Cicero, De Officiis](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/47001/pg47001-images.html), Walter Miller's 1913 English translation, book III, chapters XII–XIII, §§50–57. The grain merchant example contrasts arguments attributed to Diogenes and Antipater. Cicero then gives his own judgement. Preserve those three speaking roles rather than presenting a list of equally endorsed rules.

The passage concerns an apparent conflict between advantage and moral rightness. Cicero does not endorse the premise that wrongdoing becomes right merely because it is profitable. Distinguish his reported debate, his resolution and the reader's reconstruction. Miller's legal vocabulary is not current consumer law; no modern jurisdiction or legal duty follows from this text. The English translation was read, not independently checked against Latin manuscripts.
