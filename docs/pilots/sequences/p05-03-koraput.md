# P05-L03 · Whose evidence describes a farming system?

Research brief, 2026-10-22. Parent: P05 agriculture. Audience: general readers; this broadens the sequence from a measured sample and field model to a situated food system. Planned only. Sources `seq-fao-koraput`, `seq-koraput-plan` and `seq-mssrf-seeds` are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).

## Question and evidence

What can a programme description, a conservation plan and a published farmer account each establish? The [FAO GIAHS profile](https://www.fao.org/giahs/giahs-around-the-world/india-koraput/en) describes Koraput's farming/forest context and identifies MSSRF as the requesting agency. Its diversity counts have mixed historical reference periods; they are not a current census. The [MSSRF action plan hosted by FAO](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/templates/giahs/Action_plan_Koraput.pdf), especially printed p. 2, defines proposed outputs. A listed output is not evidence that an activity succeeded.

The [MSSRF feature dated 19 May 2026](https://www.mssrf.org/stories-of-change/seed-keepers-odishas-community-seed-banks) presents Dutika Mathpadia's seed choices and reported experience through an institutional writer. It names Nuaguda at the start and Kumbharaguda near the end. That unresolved location mismatch must be disclosed; do not silently select one village. Reported preferences and benefits do not establish average effects or a causal evaluation. The original interview language, complete transcript and participant reuse terms were not inspected.

## Planned exploration

Build an evidence matrix with separate rows for seed access, diversity, labour, food preferences and control over decisions. Columns identify source, actor, place/time, evidence type and what is still unknown. Readers place an attributed observation, a programme proposal and an editorial inference into different cells. A system map may show proposed relationships between seed storage, access, cultivation and household decisions, but every edge must be labelled reported, hypothesised or unestablished.

State: selected claim, source/locator, evidence category, uncertainty note and proposed next inquiry. The transfer task asks whether an increase in crop varieties proves less work or better nutrition; neither follows without its own evidence. Do not reduce the system to a biodiversity score or rank it against the Wisconsin sample using incomparable units. Do not extract medicinal, ritual, seed-storage or harvesting instructions into a practice simulation.

Use a keyboard-operated table and ordered text map, source dates visible throughout, and a clear distinction between a participant's reported account and the institution's generalisations. No map pin for the disputed village. Success means selecting evidence at the right scale, recognising an incomplete outcome claim and formulating a question that an appropriate local source could answer.

## Preparation gates and release

Before authoring, resolve the feature's village discrepancy or choose a different inspected, clearly located account. Establish a specific community/practitioner context and a contemporary source for any claim presented as current. Obtain appropriate review and permissions for participant quotations, images, translations or practice descriptions selected for the final lesson. Public hosting does not mean that this project has community participation or authority to generalise across Koraput's peoples.

No broad licence for these pages, photos or reports is assumed. Current use is links and original bounded commentary. The plan's legal/policy discussion and reported medicinal claims are not adopted as current law or medical evidence. The FAO profile, underlying MSSRF plan and MSSRF feature are related institutional sources, not three independent confirmations. Qualified agronomy/community-context review and real learning/access evidence remain open; no contact or consent has been invented.
