# P08 performer, access and learning session packet

2026-10-22. **Unperformed.** This packet is preparation for the generated phrase pilot, not evidence that a performer or learner has heard it. No participants are assigned and no outreach is authorized by the packet.

## Freeze the stimulus

Record exploration ID/revision, content and artifact digests, browser/device/output route, playback volume and the exact downloaded WAV hash. Retain the WAV's embedded JSON provenance: nine event offsets, tone gates, tempo, renderer/formula, sample format, region bounds and pending release terms. Check that any editing/export tool retained it. No human recording is part of the present stimulus.

Begin with versions A/B at 90 BPM, the full eight-beat span. Confirm the selected tone gates and both event tables before playback. The native browser engineering checks establish playback/transport behavior only; they do not establish audibility, comfort or expressive usefulness for a particular listener. Let participants choose a comfortable volume, pause, stop, or use the text route without a timed response.

## Performer or educator review

A real reviewer records their relevant experience and permission to retain the review. Compare short/sustained tones while keeping tempo fixed, then compare tempos while aligning by beat offset. Record whether the duration contrast is perceptible and pedagogically useful, including any failure or ambiguity. Do not use a preferred emotion as the answer key. Ask for one located observation and at least two possible expressive readings with reasons. Record which differences arise from tone duration, speed or their interactions; equal peak gain is not equal perceived loudness or timbre.

A proposed gesture/tapping or human-performance extension is a separate future activity with its own evidence and contributor permissions. It is not accepted by inspecting this generated tone sequence.

## Access and learner observation

Record the actual modality and assistive technology used. Ask the reader to choose a version/tempo/replay region, play/pause/stop if desired, jump to a named event, compare values, create a private note, share only the selection, and export the stimulus. In the no-audio route, use the full event tables and their verbal timing descriptions. Record assistance, failures, confusing labels and any inability to complete a step.

Use the transfer prompt from the exact pilot. Preserve the participant's observation separately from the facilitator's interpretation. A correct event-table answer demonstrates a structural comparison in that session; it does not demonstrate equivalent hearing, general music understanding or learning efficacy. Record unsuccessful attempts too.
