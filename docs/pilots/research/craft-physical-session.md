# P07 real-sample and reproduction packet

Status: prepared, **no physical session performed**. Applies to `x-craft-0001` revision 0.1.0 and the corresponding schematic model. It does not establish that the proposed paper setup or repair works. The independent [source/design review](craft-source-review.md) explains the evidence limits.

## Proposed specimen to review before making

Use twelve pre-cut paper strips, six warp and six weft, with a flat support and removable edge support. A starting proposal is strips about 2 cm wide and 20 cm long, leaving accessible ends around the six-by-six crossing area. These dimensions and paper stiffness are not physically tested; the practitioner must record or revise them before freezing the session instructions.

Use large, high-contrast sets distinguished by written W/F labels as well as color/pattern. Put W1–W6 and F1–F6 labels on both sides near the ends so canonical identities remain readable after a flip. Add a top marker and a distinct front marker. Record actual strip dimensions, thickness/type, spacing, lighting, edge support and any assistance. Keep the weft ends unsealed; any temporary support must be described and removable for the demonstrated extraction. Do not infer stability from the schematic.

The declared front sequence for F1 is over/under/over/under/over/under across W1–W6. F2 has the opposite phase; subsequent rows alternate these phases. Back view turns about a vertical axis: top stays top, W6 becomes the leftmost visible warp label, and which strip is exposed at a crossing reverses. An in-plane rotation is not this back view.

## Independent reproduction

After a practitioner checks the actual demonstrated instructions, a second person attempts the frozen task using the supplied materials and instructions. Obtain consent and use a nonidentifying participant code; no contact or recruitment is authorized by this packet alone. Record the route (independent/partner-assisted/digital conceptual), assistance actually given, misunderstood steps, structural errors, front/back explanation and attempted repair. A demonstrator must not silently correct the second specimen.

Evaluate three outcomes separately: structural plan consistency, physical sequence completion, and observed handling. Digital clicking can support only the structural task. Do not grade inferred dexterity, tension control, drape or knowledge of an entire textile tradition. Ask the participant to transfer the label/flip reasoning to a new four-by-four diagram and explain what the diagram cannot establish.
