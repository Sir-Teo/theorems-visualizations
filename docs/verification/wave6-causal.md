## Models and mathematical limits

1. Binary instrument: exact enumeration of the declared four-node model, with optional exclusion and exchangeability violations. Observed relevance cannot certify the graph's assumptions.
2. Homogeneous Wald: exact population contrasts versus actual samples with fixed, equal instrument arms. The sufficient constant individual effect is distinguished from weaker ATT restrictions. Ratios are summarized by empirical quantiles, not potentially nonexistent means.
3. 2SLS: both stages use intercepts and actual projection; a zero first stage has no second-stage coefficient. Algebra and causal validity remain separate.
4. Weak instruments: exact inversion of the first sample's quadratic AR acceptance inequality, retaining two rays, unbounded, empty and degenerate sets. Exact 95% coverage is limited to valid iid Gaussian structural residuals. Skewed/heavy-tailed modes and invalid exclusion are experiments. Coverage SE is calculated from independent Bernoulli indicators.
5. Principal strata: four known latent masses versus observable mixtures; all-zero weights define no population.
6. LATE: positive complier mass and no defiers identify CACE, while the ATE also depends on other strata. Signed defier contributions and zero net relevance remain visible.
7. Manski: actual counterfactual completions attain every point in the sharp binary interval. Monotone outcome response is an additional assumption, distinct from treatment monotonicity.
8. Balke–Pearl: exact rational LPs over 16 response types, with every witness checked against all eight cell constraints. Both extrema and their mixtures are actual feasible populations. Undefined conditional rows, infeasibility and solver-budget status are distinct. No sampling-confidence claim.
9. Instrumental inequality: the event-inclusion proof applies to finite variables; the widget uses binary population tables. Passing the necessary restriction does not certify a real instrument.
10. DiD: two independent groups, four repeated observations per unit, a shared unit intercept and independent innovations. Exact bias is trend difference plus post shock minus anticipation; exact SD is `2/sqrt(n)`. Flat pretrends need not rule out a later confounding shock.
11. RD: two actual triangular-weight local fits on fixed midpoint inputs, exact conditional mean/variance and explicit rank failure. Unequal density alone is distinguished from an untreated outcome jump. Finite-bandwidth curvature bias is not a violation of continuity.
12. MR: five conditionally independent binomial genotypes, explicit ancestry covariance, actual sampled marginal associations and repeated ratios. Genetic labels, association agreement and synthetic examples do not establish medical intervention effects.

Normal, standardized lognormal (log-SD 0.7) and standardized t3 noise are sampled through the existing distribution library. Repetition choices execute 1, 100 or 10,000 independent samples. Histograms retain their full denominator, count excluded tails and unavailable estimates, and do not invent moments for ratio distributions. Gaussian sampling curves are displayed only where the supplied model makes them exact. Continuous controls retain unsnapped values; integer controls represent actual counts or seeds.
