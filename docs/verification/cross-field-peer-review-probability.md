# Independent peer review of climate cross-field dependencies

Reviewed **26 initial semantic pairs across all eight collections**, plus **two follow-up corrections**. All **96 final ledger edges** independently pass written-endpoint, different-field, existing-dependency and syllabus-mirror checks. Current counts match the ledger. The full written-plus-planned graph has **13661 nodes and zero cycles**. No browser or primary-source closure work was performed.

Two coordinated corrections were applied by the owning reviewer:

- **climate-8.13:** replaced periodic-series `sig-4.1` with Fourier-transform definition `rca-9.1`. A finite-window proxy record or covariance can be integrable without having a fundamental period. This link does not assert a particular historical spectral estimator.
- **climate-4.6:** added `stat-2.1` for conditioning on the stable branch, separate from `pma-6.19` for its Jacobian substitution. With B=1(F<1), the conditional density divides the joint law by Z=P(B=1); the CDF also divides by Z, giving the stated median equation. There was **no alternate prerequisite path** supplying this conditioning rule. Climate therefore reaches **25** qualifying edges for a mathematical reason.

Independent full-array mirror checks also found nine pre-existing syllabus omissions/substitutions among touched items: astro-4.12, geo-4.1, climate-1.9, geog-1.10, popgen-1.10, popgen-1.14, popgen-2.1, popgen-3.5 and neuro-1.7. The owner synchronized these complete dependency lists before release; all new edge targets had already been mirrored.

The remaining sampled links are supported. Three qualifications matter: the seawater Taylor target also supports the source’s curvature/cabbeling discussion; the chemical-equilibrium target is used for its general Gibbs criterion, not its ideal-gas quotient as an aqueous binding formula; and the population-genetics Poisson model is established by its actual branch-count/uniform-time construction, not by constant mean rate alone. Source flags remain untouched.

Alternate paths were independently enumerated for all additions (13 final edges have another path). Each of these 13 alternate paths was inspected against the local operation and retained with a per-edge reason in the JSON. Such a path does not disqualify an explicitly invoked local operation.
