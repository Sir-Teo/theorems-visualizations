## Corrections and derivations

**4.7:** Replaced density-only and line-only generalizations by optically thick thermal emission and optically thin line-plus-continuum qualifications. With constant sourceS, integrating dI/dτ=−I+S yields I=I0 exp(−τ)+S(1−exp(−τ)); zero opacity is transparent, equal temperatures give no contrast, and the emergent intensity is a convex combination of the two inputs. A stable expm1 evaluation preserves thin-slab emission. Added actual zero-opacity and equal-temperature controls. Normalization uses the visible-domain Planck maximum, including both temperature endpoints; previously some extreme spectra were clipped. The colour strip is an illustrative display, not a calibrated detector.

**4.8:** Corrected N2/N1 at10000K from a claimed few percent to approximately3×10⁻⁵. At5772K the model gives4.977744×10⁻⁹. The state count Σ(l=0..n−1)2(2l+1)=2n² supplies the factor4 in N2/N1; common nuclear spin factors cancel. An explicit highest-retained-level control exposes the potentially strong high-temperature partition dependence. The infinite isolated-atom partition diverges since n² exp[−χ(1−1/n²)/kT] grows asymptotically as a positive constant timesn². The finite cutoff is a chosen toy parameter, not plasma level dissolution. Unit-weight toggle is counterfactual. The first eight population bars now show actual fractions with adaptive lower limits rather than silently censoring values at10⁻¹⁴.

**4.9:** General Saha balance uses internal partition functions Ui(T), rather than ground-state degeneracies without qualification. Summing the Boltzmann weights within each stage gives the partition-function extension of the source's level-state derivation. The shown hydrogen approximation explicitly setsUI=2,UII=1, so the electron spin factor2 cancels the neutral ground degeneracy2. The ratio's complete raw extent now fits the log plot. Imposed electron pressure is separate from a solved atmosphere. Charge neutrality gives ne=Pe/kT, nH=ne/x; the readout displays the implied total hydrogen density and flags closely packed atoms where the isolated-atom ideal-gas model is suspect. No pressure-ionization calculation or physical validity across every input is claimed.
