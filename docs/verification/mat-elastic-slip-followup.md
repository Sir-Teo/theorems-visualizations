## Corrections and mathematics

**3.1:** Kept the explicitly assumed sinusoidal rigid-slip model: matching its derivative at zero toG/a givesτmax=Gb/(2πa). Removed unsupported blanket empirical stress ranges and unneeded original-priority claims. The comparison slider is an assumed stress, rather than a measurement or named copper calibration.

The previous PN bar used2G/(1−ν)exp[−2πa/((1−ν)b)], whose exponent is twice the corrected sampled-interface decay. Witha′=b and isotropic pure-edgeK=G/(1−ν), matchingτmax as above givesζ/b=a/[2(1−ν)b]. ForΓ=ζ/b andC=cosh(2πΓ), the misfit energy isW=Kb²/(4π)sinh(2πΓ)/(C−cos(2πu/b)). Its derivative divided byb is maximized atcos(2πu/b)=4/[C+sqrt(C²+8)]. The helper evaluates that exact model maximum, not a wide-core approximation on narrow inputs. ValuesτP/G are0.1599604377814,0.03614189206332,0.002667821848144 ata/b=.5,.82,1.4. Real interface potentials, core relaxation, row periods, sources and thermal effects are not solved. The correct wide-core asymptote isG/(1−ν)exp(−2πζ/b) under these assumptions.

The comparison plot now includes the smallest actual stress instead of flooring its bar at10⁻⁶. The atom-row labels and full vertical extent fit the maximum spacing. Actual rigid and dislocation handles remain functional.
