## Corrections

**3.9:** The old picture equated Poisson substitution events with the number of differing sequence positions, even at rates generating counts larger than the sequence length. It now explicitly plots synthetic unobserved **event counts**, whose mean is2rLt, and separately plots observable Jukes–Cantor expected mismatches `3L/4*(1−exp(−8rt/3))`. The rate per unit time is a separate clock assumption, not an automatic consequence of neutrality.

The rate multiplier is gamma-distributed independently per pair and shared by that pair’s two branches. Its coefficient of variation is therefore a **pair-shared** rate CV, not a CV of two independently varied branches. Conditional moments give variance `lambda+c²lambda²`. Finite resamples are not promised a monotone scatter increase.

The old nominal95% band used a symmetric normal approximation even for tiny Poisson means. The band now uses actual .025/.975 discrete quantiles, drawn on161 time nodes; the readout computes the exact discrete tail probability at each actual sample time, which can be much less than5%. The event sampler now uses the library’s actual Poisson inversion/low-count product sampler, rather than the chapter helper’s rounded-normal approximation above40. No shared helper was altered. Fitted-rate, strict expectation, band and every dot determine the left plot range. The residual histogram includes its entire sample rather than clamping tails into endpoint bins. Its mean squared Pearson residual uses **P**, with expectation1 against the known strict rate; it is not called a pooled dispersion index of unequal-mean observations. The normal overlay is expressly only a large-count reference. Zero estimated rates are displayed safely.

**3.12:** The original compilation supports positive ecological/range associations, so a sweeping assertion of no abundance correlation was removed. The historical167-species range and all three species values were checked against the full original and its dataset. They are explicitly2012 observations/rough historical census estimates, not current conservation counts or confidence intervals. The human Kong mutation-rate attribution was removed; the slider is an illustrative shared model parameter.

The mathematical model is explicitly a neutral constant-size panmictic coalescent with symmetric four-state mutation. Its full exponential-time integral is shown. The approximation `pi≈4Ne mu` does not apply at theta40; finite-state expectation remains below.75. An assumed effective-size ceiling changes the model, and matching a band does not explain its cause or resolve the paradox. The old lower viewport of1e−5 clipped valid low-count model points near4e−8; the range now includes the full curve and selected point.
