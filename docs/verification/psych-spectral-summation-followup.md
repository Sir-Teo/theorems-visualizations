The plot retains every published CIE sample. It calls these standardized whole-observer efficiency functions, not individual pigment curves.

The authored ideal monochromatic pair uses integer wavelengths 380–780 nm and radiant powers `P_A=1`, `P_B=V(A)/V(B)`. Equal photopic products follow exactly. Scotopic A/B is `(V′(A)V(B))/(V′(B)V(A))`. The independently normalized shares use each condition's own pair sum. All source coefficients on this closed domain are positive; no division by zero, false numerical zero in tail readouts, or missing-support extrapolation occurs. Equal wavelengths give shares 1/2 and ratio 1, and swapping wavelengths reciprocates the ratio. Common attenuation cancels, so neither a calibrated mesopic transition nor an absolute cross-condition brightness prediction follows. Scientific readouts retain small positive tails. Diagram colors are not a screen appearance prediction, and radiometric power is not identified with equal photon counts.

## 4.5 model and repairs

Replaced unsupported universal receptor presets and hidden unsaved preset memory with two separate declared authored cuts. With `r=x/x_c`, `H=I_th(x)/I_th(x_c)`, the rule is `r^-1` below the cut and `r^-beta` above it, `beta∈[0,.9]`. Thus `H*r` is 1 below and `r^(1-beta)` above. The two log segments meet exactly at `(log10(x_c),0)`; slopes are −1/−beta for threshold and 0/(1−beta) for the normalized product. At beta=0 the post-critical threshold is flat; positive offered beta gives partial summation. This is not a joint area-time factorization or a fit to source data.

Continuous controls set log10 critical area .3–3.9, log10 critical duration .3–3, and beta 0–.9, using `step='any'`, explicit formatting and exact finite closed-domain validation. Area is arcmin², time ms; the curves' vertical values are logarithms of dimensionless quantities. The products are not labeled energy. Barlow's actual two-observer, peripheral 6°30′ field, Ilford603/497-nm peak, 507-nm-equivalent-quanta, 2-mm pupil and approximately 80%-seen criterion are stated. Figures 2–3 are one observer's curves. The public scope distinguishes these conditions from 1.1 and the authored cut values.

Barlow pp. 340–343 and summary 349 support conditional spatial/temporal limit variation. His p. 345 explicitly rejects identifying measured limits with unique background-noise integration area/time. The public item preserves that inferential limitation. No universal foveal/rod critical value, clinical inference, sharp measured transition or historical priority remains.
