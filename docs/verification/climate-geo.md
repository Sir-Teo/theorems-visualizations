# Atmosphere and ocean dynamics: remaining source checks

Reviewed 2026-10-18. Closed `geo-4.7`; `geo-3.9` remains flagged for the inaccessible Holland primary text. Independently checked root's corrected `geo-4.5` spin-down convention. These are source, equation, computation and browser checks, not a claim that every chapter was independently reviewed.

## Primary text inspected

- Ekman (1905), *On the influence of the earth's rotation on ocean-currents*, [complete author paper scan](https://empslocal.ex.ac.uk/people/staff/gv219/classics.d/Ekman05.pdf), SHA256 `9e33e2788d4462f4668ce7bd2133c786f129cb6f33b7fc434bcc8d1f6f384646`. Read the introduction p.2, the complete relevant deep-water derivation §I pp.7–10 eqs.(5)–(6), and finite-depth extension pp.12–14 eqs.(8)–(10), using single-thread OCR checked against the scan. The original explicitly reports Nansen's 20°–40° ice deflection, 45° deep-water surface flow, exponential weakening, and depth-integrated transverse transport. Finite bottom stress changes the latter. Removed the uninspected Bjerknes/student story and unsupported modern observational range. Corrected the near-deep limit label to h≫D: at h=D, finite-depth transport is not exactly the deep-water value.
- Greenspan & Howard (1963), [*On a time-dependent motion of a rotating fluid*](https://hannahlab.org/papers/Greenspan_Howard_1963.pdf), §3 p.387 (plate separation 2L), p.392 eq.(3.21), and §4 pp.392–394 read independently. Their L/√(νΩ) uses half-depth: for full depth H and two rigid lids this is H/(2√(νΩ)). The independent pumping-flux check also gives H/√(νΩ) for one rigid lid and a free top. Corrected the scope's erroneous description as nonlinear spin-up. The contracting particles are an illustration of near-bottom transport, not a sediment force model, and R²/ν is labeled a radial diffusion scale.
- Bister & Emanuel (1998), [*Dissipative heating and hurricane intensity*](https://texmex.mit.edu/ftp/ftp/ftp/pub/emanuel/PAPERS/bisteremanuel98.pdf), SHA256 `1ff501b075fa041501912927908f10283411d929c5c1914d56c8311daa6f9c78`. Read the complete relevant §3.1 pp.234–235 eqs.(7)–(22) and entropy derivation §3.2 pp.236–237. Eq.(21) uses the denominator To when atmospheric dissipation is recycled as heat. The ordinary Carnot efficiency is (Ts−To)/Ts; the intensity coefficient is ε/(1−ε)=(Ts−To)/To. The item now makes this distinction and credits the inspected 1998 source.

## Hurricane corrections and retained blocker

`geo-3.9` uses actual saturation **specific humidity**, εe/[p−(1−ε)e], and computes ambient specific humidity from RH times vapour pressure before converting to q. It formerly used the mixing-ratio denominator and RH times saturated q. A continuous radial maximum now determines pressure deficit and the readout maximum; the cyclostrophic radius parameter is distinguished from the gradient-wind maximum, which shifts inward for nonzero f. Category thresholds and the universal 26°C guide were removed: the illustrated gradient wind above the boundary layer does not predict the surface sustained wind that defines hurricane categories. The fixed outflow temperature/near-surface humidity assumptions are explicit, and the maximum-SST handle label points inward to stay visible.

The full Holland (1980) original remains uninspected: AMS denied PDF access (403), known course/mirror paths returned404/503, and the official UCAR record for the later Holland et al.(2010) paper presented a connection challenge. No challenge was bypassed. Metadata, abstract and later secondary equations were insufficient to clear the source flag. The displayed pressure/gradient-wind equations have independent mathematical tests, but those tests do not substitute for the missing source inspection. Removed the verification-process parenthetical from product references; this report records the blocker.
