## Corrections and proof scope

**4.2:** signed and neutral selection, the fixed boundaries and asymptotic fixation are explicit. The exact continuous interpolation has rate `ln(1+s)`, not `s`. At s=.01, .01 to .99 takes 923.6115 continuous generations and first crosses at integer generation 924. Wright–Fisher histories start from the actual rounded integer count, including zero; no phantom initial copy is invented. Absorbed endpoints are excluded from finite log-odds fits; insufficient interior states give an unavailable fit. Log-odds plot extents include the actual finite sample, and deterministic agreement is measured separately.

**4.4:** nonnegative constant genotype fitness, positive mean fitness and boundary qualifications are explicit. Wright’s gradient identity gives the local direction; its sign alone is not the proof of finite-step fitness ascent. The multi-allele metric includes `1/(2 mean fitness)`.

**4.5:** positive diagonal entries are a sufficient global positive-mean condition. Mean fitness is a strict Lyapunov function, with equality at every fixed point including unstable and boundary fixed points; the conclusion concerns the equilibrium set, not the globally highest peak. The proof defines `a_ij=W_ij p_i p_j/mean` and uses Jensen to show `ln(mean_next/mean) >= 2 KL(p_next || p) >= 0`, with zero-weight terms omitted. Equality forces a fixed point; continuity and compactness give fixed accumulation points. The nonsymmetric optional map is now explicitly a separate haploid frequency-dependent game, with its actual signed drops displayed. The preset with a stable vertex and stable edge no longer claims an interior peak. Roundoff is reported without flooring negative changes to a fabricated positive value.

**4.7:** `0<s,t<1` ensures the stated positive viabilities. The exact displacement factor is `1−(s+t)p(1−p)/mean`, strictly between zero and one because `mean−(s+t)p(1−p)=(1−s)p+(1−t)(1−p)>0`. This proves no overshoot and global monotone convergence from interior starts; boundaries remain fixed. The local multiplier is independently differentiated. The finite trajectories report their actual largest remaining error, with unavailable measured ratios when deviations are too small. An unsupported universal finite-population fixation-time claim was removed.

**4.12:** the theorem assumes `E|ln W|<infinity`, and separately finite arithmetic mean for Jensen. The normalized log-odds limit is valid also when its limit is zero; zero has no directional fixation conclusion. Forty finite histories, their sample median, exact expected log-odds and the asymptotic prediction are distinguished. Variance alone does not order arbitrary distributions. Temporal dependence is left outside the stated independent-environment theorem. Expected-line and logarithm-curve extents are included in the actual views.

Adjacent **4.1** now restricts fertility equivalence to parent-factorable contributions: general mating-pair fertility does not reduce to constant genotype viability weights. Adjacent **4.3** calls the change a rational expression with a cubic-or-lower numerator and explicitly represents the neutral continuum when all three viabilities agree. These are supported by the same primary treatment and add no source-queue closures.
