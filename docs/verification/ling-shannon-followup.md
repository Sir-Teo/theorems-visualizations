## Material corrections

4.8 used the lower-bound expression on arbitrary human and held-out machine guess frequencies and called it a lower bound on H. The actual existing held-out q vectors are nonmonotonic at all four context settings (first increases at ranks6/8/16/10), so they do not give the nonnegative rectangular mixture required by the ideal lower proof. In general q=[0,1,0,…] makes that expression2 while its marginal entropy is0, a direct counterexample. Also, a finite-context lower bound on F_N does not lower-bound H merely because F_N decreases toward H. These are repaired in both prose and computations.

The replacement preserves a real-text guessing exercise, with five consecutive100-symbol Alice windows, all27 choices, wrong-symbol histories, all27 histogram bins, cursor/window controls, repeated-letter seeking and explicit reset. Every choice and opaque record is saved and replayed. Practice uses text also in the finite-source corpus and is honestly labeled practice, not a held-out experiment or an entropy-rate estimator. The recorded histogram is never passed through the ideal lower-bound formula.

Both items compare a fully specified finite joint source: choose one position uniformly from the normalized embedded Alice symbols after the first three, then observe that next symbol and the preceding k symbols, k0–3. Every k uses the same135,400 positions, yielding nested context partitions and comparable entropies. Normalize to lowercase a–z and space, collapsing all other runs to one space. The complete embedded input and official Gutenberg comparison are documented in `ling-language-models-followup.md`; no shared text was modified. This is a corpus-derived authored finite source, not certification of natural-language entropy or a stationary language process.

Within every context, rank actual counts; pooled rank probabilities q are nonincreasing by construction. Direct conditional entropy is computed independently of the displayed lower/upper formulas. For width i, weight w_i=i(q_i−q_{i+1}) is nonnegative, weights sum to1 and their uniform mixtures reconstruct every q_j. The lower expression is the mixture's average component entropy; the upper is marginal rank entropy. Both contain the directly computed F. The paper's right panel draws every one of the27 ranks and every nonzero rectangle layer, instead of truncating to13 ranks or discarding negative layers from a nonideal histogram. Highlighting a width with zero weight correctly reports zero.

Practice-state validation checks integer controls before setters, all27 integer count bins and total≤1,000,000, distinct legal wrong-symbol indices, exclusion of the actual correct symbol, complete-cursor empty-history rules and agreement between the record's cursor/window and restored controls. A full record disables Guess until reset; it cannot increment into an invalid state. Validation and restoration copy arrays. Invalid saved records reject visibly, rather than snapping inputs, silently substituting data or losing wrong guesses.
