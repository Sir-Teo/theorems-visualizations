## Sources and estimands

- [CDC Principles of Epidemiology, Lesson 1 §7](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/csels/dsepd/ss1978/lesson1/section7.html): cohort, case-control and cross-sectional designs. Cohort simulation records actual event/censor/administrative minimum person-time. Confounding and deliberately informative censoring are optional; the hazard parameter is not labeled the crude causal rate ratio.
- [Vandenbroucke and Pearce (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dys147) and [incidence-density sampling methods, IJE 48 (2019)](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/48/6/1981/5519661): cumulative case-control sampling preserves the disease odds ratio under exposure-independent sampling; risk-set sampling uses the actual eligible survivors and conditional likelihood. The latter targets a proportional hazard ratio without a rare-disease assumption, rather than promising finite-sample equality. Controls can later become cases and can be reused at different times.
- [Prentice (1986)](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/73.1.1), [Kim (2015)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4374014/): nested case-control and baseline case-cohort designs. The visualization estimates a fixed cohort's exposure prevalence using explicitly calculated inclusion probabilities and Horvitz–Thompson weights. It does not misuse these simple weights as a survival-regression estimator. All cases are selected; unique assay counts are actual, with no assumed percentage saving.
- [Mansournia, Jewell and Greenland (2018)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9188848/): matching needs appropriate analysis and does not automatically eliminate confounding. The authored paired population has a common conditional odds ratio, heterogeneous stratum exposure prevalences, and a separately calculated marginal odds ratio. Matching directly on exposure leaves zero discordant-pair information.
- [Maclure (1991)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1985444/): case-crossover design for transient exposure and acute events. The explicitly authored hazard-weighted/rare-event sampling model has expected discordance ratio equal to trigger ratio times temporal exposure-odds trend. Fixed attributes are controlled by self-comparison; trend, carryover and time-varying confounding remain limitations.
- [Robinson (1950)](https://doi.org/10.2307/2087176): aggregation logic only. The five-region numerical counterexample is authored, not a transcription of census evidence.

Some publisher/PMC full-text pages challenged automated access. Indexed primary text, original abstracts and accessible original-paper scans were used; the historical numerical inputs below were checked directly in rendered original tables.
