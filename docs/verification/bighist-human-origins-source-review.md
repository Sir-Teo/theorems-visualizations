## Primary evidence read

- [Aiello & Wheeler1995, The Expensive-Tissue Hypothesis, Current Anthropology36:199–221, complete original23-page scan](https://gwern.net/doc/algernon/1995-aiello.pdf), [independent full university-course scan](https://prod-edxapp.edx-cdn.org/assets/courseware/v1/67e06bd5c8a3c7b568a55bf3ee1933ee/c4x/WellesleyX/ANTH_207x/asset/Aiello95_expensivetissue_.pdf). Read complete relevant main-article metabolic analysis, brain/gut argument, evolutionary discussion and conclusions pp199–211; visually checked original p201 Table1/Eq3. The later invited comments/reply are not claimed as fully reviewed. Eq3 is3.39 M^0.75 W; Table1 gives11.2W/kg of brain,1.3kg/14.6W in its65kg example. Its BMR source table is90.6W, while the separately predicted Kleiber BMR is about77.6W; those denominators are distinct. The paper itself warns that the comparative analysis is not an individual organ/metabolic assessment.
- [Hays, Imbrie & Shackleton1976, Variations in the Earth's Orbit: Pacemaker of the Ice Ages, Science194:1121–1132, complete original scan](https://www.whoi.edu/cms/files/hays76sci_268464.pdf). Read the complete main text, methods, chronological models, frequency/time-domain analyses, nonlinear interpretation and summary pp1121–1131 by OCR, with a visual check of p1126/Fig5 and source statistics. The paper uses two Southern Indian Ocean cores,450kyr of record, alternate SIMPLEX/ELBOW/TUNE-UP chronologies and spectral/phase tests. Its raw power estimates, confidence statements and chronology are not the synthetic curves drawn here. It explicitly notes eccentricity's small effect on annual total insolation; therefore “only modulates precession” was too strong.
- [Imbrie & Imbrie1980, Modeling the Climatic Response to Orbital Variations, Science207:943–953, complete original scan](https://www.whoi.edu/cms/files/imbrie80sci_268884.pdf). Read complete relevant model construction, Eqs1–2, Fig4 and tuning discussion pp948–950, with introductory model context. Visually checked p949/Eq2. The original fitted example has mean17kyr, growth42.5kyr and melt10.6kyr (ratio4), a tuned orbital combination and explicit limitations. The present toy orbit and freely chosen time constants do not reproduce that fitted experiment.
- [Vidal et al.2022, Age of the oldest known Homo sapiens from eastern Africa, Nature601:579–583, complete open-access original and Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04275-8.pdf). Read complete relevant main text, tephra-correlation/dating discussion and full40Ar/39Ar method; visually checked Figs1–2, including original p581. The preferred pooled ash age is233±22kyr at2σ after filtering to71 accepted grains. The KHS ash overlies Omo I: this is a **minimum fossil age**, not a direct measurement of the fossil's age. The paper explicitly leaves a robust maximum age unresolved. Its separate Herto implications were not transferred to the map.

Temporary originals/text/page images are `/tmp/bighist-primary`. The publisher's Aiello PDF returned403, but both public complete mirrors above succeeded. The IA Current Anthropology item listed only an encrypted PDF; no restriction was bypassed or represented as a reading. Hays's PDF lacks useful body text, so each original page was rendered and OCRed serially with OMP_THREAD_LIMIT=1; original Fig5 was visually inspected.
