## Sources actually checked

- [IUPAC cage effect C00771](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/C00771), encounter E02086 and cage C00769 distinguish repeated collisions from an encounter. The geometric collision/escape model is an authored illustration with exact probabilities, not a molecular-dynamics measurement.
- [University of Illinois PHYS498, Rates of Diffusion-Controlled Reactions](https://www.ks.uiuc.edu/Services/Class/PHYS498/LectureNotes/chp8.pdf), §§8.1–8.3, eqs.8.12,8.32,8.37–8.38: relative diffusion sum, steady dilute-pair reservoir, radial-potential resistance integral, finite-speed radiation boundary and perfect absorption. The entire six-page chapter was retrieved. The notes use a surface speed w, not a bimolecular microscopic rate constant. Number-density m³/s is converted to L mol⁻¹ s⁻¹ by1000 NA. The screened pair-potential extension is derived by x=R/r and explicitly identified as illustrative, rather than a concentrated-electrolyte prediction.
- [IUPAC kinetic electrolyte effect K03401](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/K03401) and [Debye–Hückel D01534](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/D01534): primary activity effect versus secondary pre-equilibrium effect; dilute limiting law, molal ionic strength, conventional individual ionic coefficients. Subtraction of the three charge squares derives the Brønsted–Bjerrum slope with **log10**, not ln. Water's A=0.509 is used with normalized molal ionic strength at298.15K. The selected I≤0.01mol/kg is explicitly a limiting-law illustration, not a guaranteed universal accuracy range.
- [IUPAC Brønsted relation B00746](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/B00746): statistical factors are set to unity. The acid-series log coefficient has slope **−α against pKa**. The planned positive-slope wording was corrected. The plotted resistance cap is a separate model with consistent bimolecular units; no empirical acid-series points were invented.
- [IUPAC Marcus equation M03702](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/M03702), intrinsic barrier I03135 and inverted region I03152: molar barrier, intramolecular/encounter-complex first-order rate, electronic transmission factor, and the explicit limitation of classical treatment in the inverted region. The chapter does not mistake a first-order transfer rate for a bimolecular capture coefficient.
- [Marcus's Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/06/marcus-lecture.pdf), indexed official full-text passages around printed pp.78 and84, eqs.5a–6: harmonic barrier, solvent/internal reorganization partition and retrospective experimental history.
