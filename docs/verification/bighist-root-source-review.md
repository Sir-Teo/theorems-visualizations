## Dispositions and corrections

**3.8 closed.** Four statistically exceptional events and the prolonged Devonian caveat replace an unqualified five-event statistical claim. Species, genera and family metrics are distinguished. The logistic recovery target is now90% of the actual pre-kill stock, rather than90% of capacity. A loss≤10% requires zero waiting. The chosen instantaneous pulses are not presented as real event durations; a rate-peak lag is not equated with stock recovery.

**3.9 closed after full-source follow-up.** Complete Hildebrand1991, Renne2013, Morgan2016, Schulte2010 and Harvey2008 originals were obtained and compared by the physics reviewer. URLs, hashes and exact claim locations are in the [complete impact comparison](bighist-impact-primary-sources.md). The previous access failures were resolved with legitimate complete coauthor/institutional copies. The relative date is now5±27kyr at68%confidence, not a universal32kyr hard bound or a latest absolute calibration. Globally distributed ejecta are distinguished from site-specific indicators. Heat/darkness/cooling are qualified as modelled mechanisms with uncertain components and durations; sulfate estimates can extend for years to decades. An unsupported generic12–20km model range and optional10–15km reported range were omitted in favor of the actual chosen-parameter inversion and the separately verified historical Alvarez estimate. Extreme speed/angle/density settings can require an impactor≈44km in this simplified model; the diameter axis/curve/handle now extend to50km so that inverse is visible. No exact atmospheric or biospheric calculation is claimed by the crater display.

**3.10 closed.** Reading the published original exposed substantive corrections. Italy/Denmark's30/160 ratios use acid-insoluble residues, while New Zealand's20 uses whole rock; these heights are not directly comparable concentrations. Negative plutonium alone was not conclusive; the authors also measured iridium isotopes. Their fourth diameter argument was a broad optical consistency check. The60-fold mass is excavated material, with an explicitly uncertain0.22 stratospheric fraction; the display now explains its all-material uniform-layer illustration and its chosen3000kg/m³ density versus the paper's2200. Removed an unchecked1977 family anecdote and an incorrect universal priority claim; the paper itself cites earlier impact proposals. The schematic profile previously overshot its stated peak and jumped at the boundary; its replacement is continuous and reaches exactly the stated ratio. Profiles remain clearly schematic, not reproduced measurements.

**4.4 closed.** Founder sampling is compared with the paper's derivation and geographical study. The near-linear approximation requires small accumulated drift, not merely a small single-step effect. The initial fixed-loci count now counts exactly0/1, not nearly fixed loci; the paper's sampled-population conclusion is not a universal ranking of every American population. An unchecked optional Mayr1942 priority annotation was removed. Actual Bernoulli founder draws are exercised by the numerical test.

**4.7 closed.** The exact Gumbel-maximum distribution verifies the logN increment and threshold. The display's four balanced groups, chosen parameters, ten runs and nonabsorbing zero floor are explicit extensions. Previously rounded group sizes could change the actual total population; groups now contain exactlyN learners. The plotted left score is the best group averaged across runs, not the population mean. IsolatedN/4 groups require about four times the total population for the same threshold. The empirical studies are qualified as a demographic model and a small observational comparison; human biology is not described as having stopped evolving.
