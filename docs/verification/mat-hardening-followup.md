## Corrections and model limits

Removed a universal density-alone claim, recalled real-metal α range, blanket polycrystal factor and historical credit requiring a different original-paper review. A relation of the formτ≈τ₀+αGb√ρ is conditional on the relevant population and structural regime. The demo is a synthetic nearest-image2D ensemble; its fixed plane spacing, annihilation radius, box size, ramp time and displacement clamp introduce extra scales. It neither proves the relation nor must fit exponent1/2.

The previous implementation discarded all glide history belonging to a dislocation when it annihilated, and changed its normalization with the remaining population. The corrected scalar accumulatesΣsᵢΔxᵢ over every ramp step, retaining contributions before annihilation, and normalizes by the fixed prepared populationN₀andℓ₀=1/√N₀. It is signed cumulative glide, not an absolute-displacement mean. The response criterion is explicitlyP≥1/4under a finite1200+1600step protocol atΔt=4×10⁻⁵, with maximum individual step.003. A half-step control doubles both step counts at the same preparation/ramp durations. The preparation force residual is reported; it is often appreciably nonzero, so no equilibrium or static yield is claimed.

Empty prepared populations have no threshold. Uncrossed criteria are right-censored at the maximum tested stress, shown as open points and excluded from fits rather than given a fabricated threshold. The through-origin slope is explicitly imposed. A log exponent is unidentified for a single distinct density; noNaNis displayed. Repeated configuration keys replace points instead of duplicating fit weight. Density sweeps can be restarted/cancelled safely; generation tokens and timer cleanup prevent overlapping old chains. Initial display computes only one ensemble rather than launching an unsolicited large sweep. Plot ranges include points and fitted line; symbols near boundaries have adequate margins.
