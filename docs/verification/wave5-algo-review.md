## Proof/model cautions supplied to author

**Paging.** For a general marking phase with `l` clean pages, symmetry among still-unmarked old pages bounds expected faults by `l*(1+H_k-H_l) ≤ l*H_k`. Adjacent full/current phase sets contain `k+l` distinct pages, so OPT's faults across each pair are at least `l`. Summing counts each OPT phase at most twice; an initial phase costs at mostk. Thus the full general bound is `E MARK ≤ 2H_k OPT+k`, for a request sequence fixed independently of the random eviction choices. Initial-cache and incomplete-final-phase conventions must be explicit.

For LRU, take `Phi=sum_{p in OPT cache} rank_LRU(p)`, with absent pages ranked `k+1`. Process OPT's action first: its fault raisesPhi by at mostk; after OPT contains the current request, LRU's hit does not increasePhi and its fault lowersPhi by at least1. Telescoping gives `LRU ≤ k OPT+Phi_initial-Phi_final`. Identical full initial caches makePhi_initial minimal, so no additive term is needed in that convention; other initializations admit a bounded additive term. The deterministic lower bound requests the missing page amongk+1 pages, giving an online fault every step while a future-aware policy faults at most once perk steps after initialization. This rules out a ratio belowk even with a fixed additive constant.

**Recursive contraction.** With exact base solver for smalln, `t=ceil(1+n/sqrt(2))` strictly reducesn for everyn≥7 and gives fixed-cut survival `t(t−1)/(n(n−1)) ≥1/2`. For two independent contraction/recursive branches, a success lower bound is `p(n) ≥1−(1−p(t)/2)^2`, not an equality for actual success. The rounded additive threshold needs an explicit depth/tree argument for the asymptotics. Positive minimum cut, undirected multiedge sampling and loop removal are essential; disconnected zero-cut inputs need a separate return.

**Bloom.** Under independent insertion/query hash draws, exact false-positive probability is `E[(occupied/m)^k]`; query collisions are included. The marginal-bit expression raised to thekth power is not exact. The small case m2,k2,n1 is5/8, versus9/16 from that independence shortcut. Both this shortcut and `(1−exp(−kn/m))^k` must remain approximations. Deterministic seeded traces do not establish random-function guarantees.

**Count–min.** The proposed finite family on F2^5 is valid: each row uses a uniformly chosen binary `s×5` matrixA, including zero coefficients, and uniform offsetb; `h(x)=Ax+b` maps to width2^s. For distinct fixed keys, `A(x−y)` is uniform, giving collision probability exactly1/width. Sketch rows need independent draws. The proof requires nonnegative updates and a fixed stream/query independent of the family draw; all-query claims need an additional union bound. A finite PRNG-seed illustration is distinguished from the exact conceptual uniform family.

**List update and learning.** All-paid move-to-front has access-plus-swap cost2r−1 and is4-competitive by twice-inversion potential; it must not be compared against a free-forward-exchange offline optimum. The free-exchange2 result is a distinct cost model. Weighted majority with beta1/2 gives `M ≤ (ln N+m_best ln2)/ln(4/3)`, so the factor near2.41 multiplies both `m_best` and `log2 N`. Exact tie handling and positive-weight assumptions remain explicit.

**Maximum load.** A complete elementary lower-bound route was supplied: poissonize the number of balls toPoi(n/2), whose bin loads are independentPoi(1/2). At `L=ceil((1−epsilon)log n/log log n)`, the probability a given load equalsL is at least `exp(−1/2)*(1/(2L))^L`, so some load reachesL with high probability. The probability the Poisson number exceedsn is at most `(sqrt(e)/2)^n`; append balls ton to transfer the lower bound. The union/Chernoff upper bound handles `(1+epsilon)log n/log log n`. No finite equality or two-choice result follows.
