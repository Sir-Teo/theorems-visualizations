## Corrections and scope

Parallax now distinguishes the exact IAU parsec convention from the geometric tangent approximation; precision presets are illustrative. Magnitude presets distinguish solar bolometric4.74 from catalogue visual magnitudes; unsupported named faint-star/instrument-limit claims were removed. Colour uses checked catalogue B−V values and the actual Ballesteros equation; toy filters and hot-end inference limits are explicit, and spectrum bounds cover hot controls.

The LTE slab uses the formal transfer solution and checked air wavelengths, with dynamic spectrum normalization and explicit non-LTE limits. Hydrogen N2/N1 at10000K is2.90e−5, not a few percent; at5772K it is4.98e−9. A new level-cutoff control exposes the isolated-atom partition divergence rather than hiding it behind a fixed truncation. Saha states partition functions generally and the ground-state approximation specifically, including electron spin2 and ion/neutral weights1/2. Plot bounds cover the actual ratios. The spectral-sequence picture is an absorber-population proxy, not a radiative-transfer prediction; pressure changes its peak.

Mass–luminosity now uses all six published Eker branches within their calibrated interval. Points are explicitly simulated, with chosen scatter and mass noise; OLS standard errors are conditional, not errors-in-variables intervals. Branch jumps are preserved. The lifetime is only a fuel/rate proxy, normalized to10Gyr at1 solar mass using the actual fitted solar luminosity0.984011Lsun. Handles remain ordered and rendered lifetime readouts use proper TeX.
