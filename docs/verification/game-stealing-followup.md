## Mathematical and interaction repairs

The Chomp argument now says precisely that the second bite includes the corner, so playing that bite first leaves **the same** position; “the same and more” was misleading. Monotone shrinking of move sets is not offered as a general stealing condition. For square Hex, role exchange uses board transposition/color exchange, extra own stones cannot destroy a connection, and a request for an already occupied spare stone is handled by another legal spare move. These existence arguments are separate from finding an opening or claiming uniqueness. Ordinary no-swap Hex is the displayed game.

All offered Chomp safe moves omit the immediately losing poison action. Only poison remaining is terminal: the next player loses. The solver strictly decreases occupied cells. All35board sizes2…6×2…8 retain their complete winning-opening table. Every remaining square is colored by its exact best-play outcome. The corner toggle shows the actual corner and its winning reply where needed. Poison, absent and out-of-board keyboard selections do nothing.

Both game histories are now registered opaque state, with exact legal replay and rejection of repeats, poison, post-terminal moves, malformed coordinates and histories incompatible with the restored board. Controls restore first; histories restore after them. Switching games retains both histories.
