## Primary evidence and corrections

Sources retrieved 2026-10-17. Course item numbers are not source-book theorem numbers. The [author's ATAP site](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/trefethen/ATAP/) identifies the book's [official executable chapter files](https://github.com/chebfun/ATAP/tree/development).

- **4.11, orthogonal polynomials:** [ATAP chapter 17](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chebfun/ATAP/development/chap17.m), recurrence argument around (17.6)–(17.9), with [NIST DLMF §18.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.2), especially (i), (iv), (vi), (viii), for precise normalization, finite moments, zeros and Favard support. Added finite positive mass, initial conditions, real recurrence coefficients and the distinction between measures on R and support confined to the displayed interval. Corrected the picture's `n+1`/`n` roots to `n`/`n−1`. The off-band numerical residual now refers to the discrete weighted sum rather than certifying the continuous integral. Curves outside the stated ±6 vertical cap are explicitly clipped. The monic coefficient formulas were independently derived with norm denominators; the abbreviated unnormalized display in ATAP was not copied literally.
- **4.13, CF:** [Gutknecht–Trefethen, 1982](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/trefethen/publication/PDF/1982_5.pdf), §§1–3, printed pp. 358–369, plus [ATAP chapter 20](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chebfun/ATAP/development/chap20.m), Theorems 20.1–20.2. Distinguished the exact circle problem from the truncated real polynomial method, supplied real/absolute-convergence conditions and the actual fixed-degree, shrinking-interval smoothness theorem. Removed the implied universal quantitative guarantee from “rapid decay.” Listed the finite Hankel, coefficient and cosine-transform truncations; numerical comparisons at unresolved Remez/roundoff levels omit their ratios. Single-Chebyshev-mode tests prevent a missing factor of two in the real formulation.
- **4.14, Newman:** [Original Newman paper](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~hiptmair/Seminars/RAP_22/NEW64.pdf), p. 11 notation and Theorem A, pp. 12–13 proof; original page rendered and inspected at `/tmp/atlas-newman-original.png`. The paper explicitly assumes `n>4` and notes order `n` when `n` is even. The actual slider now permits only even 6–40. A stable product-ratio evaluation preserves the original function while avoiding cancellation in `P(x)−P(−x)` near zero. [ATAP chapter 25](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chebfun/ATAP/development/chap25.m), Theorem 25.1, supports the separate best-polynomial and best-rational asymptotics. Removed the false suggestion that this explicit Newman curve is close to Stahl's best-rational asymptotic; both remain separately labeled.
- **4.15, Halphen:** ATAP Theorem 25.2, equation (25.18), supplies the limit and sharp asymptotic, with Gonchar–Rakhmanov (1989) and Aptekarev (2002). Corrected the year, removed a speculative explanation of historical motivation and an unsupported fixed relative-accuracy claim. Added a dedicated Remez polish that refines continuous extrema instead of treating a coarse grid as the maximum. The transformed endpoint `t=1` evaluates the rational limit at infinity: it is no longer forcibly set to zero. The readout reports peak count/spread as numerical diagnostics, not a certificate.
- **4.16, Padé:** [ATAP chapter 27](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chebfun/ATAP/development/chap27.m), Theorem 27.1 and (27.5)–(27.8). Added formal-series and defect/order conditions; normalized full-order linear matching is a generic case, not universal. A rank-revealing normalized solver handles consistent underdetermined rational examples and explicitly identifies lower-type fallbacks. An inconsistent normalized system does not imply generalized Padé nonexistence. Corrected a claim of being the only rational method without iteration, the Stieltjes example (`log(1+z)/z`), universal Taylor/Padé behavior and degree/root labels. Evaluation-point drags stay within the real function domain.
- **4.17, doublets:** [Gonnet–Güttel–Trefethen, 2013](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/trefethen/gonnet_guettel_trefethen.pdf), introduction, §§2–4 and Algorithm 2, plus ATAP chapter 27. Exact Padé theory also has doublets; removing noise does not exclude them. The page now identifies its simplified SVD rank-truncation heuristic, uses an absolute-noise-based threshold and promises neither universal cleanup nor recovery of all four poles with inadequate degree. Distance under 0.08 counts candidate pairs only, not proven small residues. Root marks match their legend; off-window roots are clipped and counted against the actual equal-aspect viewport.
- **4.18, acceleration:** [ATAP chapter 28](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chebfun/ATAP/development/chap28.m), Aitken/epsilon discussion following (28.7), and Euler equations (28.11)–(28.13). Euler's radius-zero asymptotic series is assigned the displayed Stieltjes/Borel integral, not a Taylor germ. Analyticity along a path alone is not a Padé convergence guarantee. Replaced artificial `1e300` reciprocal substitutions with explicit breakdown detection and a lower available epsilon entry. Tracked the even-column level and actual number of partial sums used. For arctan, skipped zero Taylor terms and used Padé in `w=z²` for `atan(z)/z`; the pole panel displays the corresponding actual type. Improved the Euler reference integral with adaptive quadrature and conservatively capped displayed digits near 13.
