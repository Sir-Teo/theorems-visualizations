## Corrections

3.1's old90-versus130km error is resolved from original **Table1 row2**:500/500fm, Niger Delta/Walvis Ridge included, pole42.9°N30.1°W,57.1° rotation,0.95° RMS,1.7% of rotation and **90km**. Row5 excludes those features and gives a different pole/angle/80km; row9 underlies Fig5 and gives44°N30.6°W57°/88km. The **130km** belongs to Table2 row4's poorer combined-block fit, not the chosen South America–Africa row. The source converts angular residuals at the middle of each fitted contour, with uncertainty and correlated/selected-point caveats. No modern shoreline score is identified with that source statistic.

Unsupported memory-placed basalt/fold/fossil coordinates were replaced with explicitly constructed marker pairs generated by the reference rotation. Their closure is a geometric identity, never independent geological evidence. Simplified current shorelines are clearly separated from original914.4m contours; arbitrary pole, zero/full closure and reference reset remain interactive. The former coarse outer-ring overlap percentage was removed because it supplied no original fit diagnostic. Historical unsupported acceptance/priority/mechanism assertions removed.

3.13 no longer asserts that unimodal heights follow from one material, that two peaks alone prove drift, or that a tuned modern histogram reproduces originalFig3. A transparent chosen mixture uses component centres0.7/−4.3km, weights0.3/0.7 and spreads0.5/1km; these are not measured global area fractions. Its matched single Gaussian has analytically calculated moments, checked independently by quadrature. The infinite tails and finite plotted window are explicit. This density is separate from the column model, with no forced depth/peak agreement.

The modern chosen Airy columns conserve hydrostatic mass at a level below both Mohos. If the formal wet solution would require negative depth, the correct exposed-floor balance uses zero water and a nonnegative exposed floor. Equal-columns mode changes effective thickness/density without assigning out-of-range values to sliders. Equal density by itself does not erase thickness relief. Dynamic compensation depth/full floor range avoid inverted/clipped mantle layers and out-of-view predicted depths. No modern empirical fit or substitute for Wegener's historical sal/sima scheme is claimed.
