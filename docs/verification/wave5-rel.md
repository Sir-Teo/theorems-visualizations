## Conventions, hypotheses and computational scope

Throughout: x^0=ct; eta=diag(1,-1,-1,-1); passive observer boost x'=gamma(x-vt); F^{0i}=-E_i/c; F^{ij}=-epsilon_ijk B_k. Contravariant rank-two tensors transform as Lambda T Lambda^T. Field/current derivative indices transform too. E·B is a pseudoscalar: its invariant claim is restricted to proper Lorentz transformations, with orientation reversal tested separately. SI electric, magnetic, charge, current, pressure and flux units are kept explicit. The source Maxwell law is a physical postulate; Lorentz symmetry alone does not derive it. Constants are c=299792458 m/s, e=1.602176634e-19 C, epsilon0=8.8541878188e-12 F/m and proton mass=1.67262192595e-27 kg; the latter two are finite-precision numerical values, not exact SI definitions. Their 2022 CODATA values were checked directly in the NIST [proton mass](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?mp) and [vacuum permittivity](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?ep0) entries.

- **5.1:** four-parameter slice E=(Ex,Ey,0), cB=(cBx,0,cBz), with cF/E* matrix and recomputed invariants. Does not pretend that a tensor definition enforces field equations.
- **5.2:** ideal infinite charged line, away from axis. Actual transformed fields agree with transformed charge/current. Arrow scaling has a declared reference, distinct from the independent radial probe.
- **5.3:** stationary ions and relativistic moving carriers have equal/opposite **lab** densities. The separate current four-vectors determine primed densities. The rest electric force equals gamma times the transverse lab three-force; it is the transverse four-force that agrees directly. Artificially large carrier speeds are stated model parameters.
- **5.4:** analytic manufactured smooth Maxwell field, exact derivative transformation and source/current comparison. A local infinite idealization, not a finite-energy isolated source. Normalized derivatives and SI current scales are explicit.
- **5.5:** exactly advected Gaussian current. Separate 256-panel Simpson spatial and temporal integrals show nontrivial charge exchange through a fixed box. The profile is a continuity example, not self-consistent charged matter; quadrature residual is not a rigorous bound.
- **5.6:** exact circular motion in the magnetic frame transformed in both position and time. E<cB and E perpendicular B are required. Lab drift is E cross B/B²; the initial magnetic-frame speed is 0.35c. No radiation reaction or fictitious Newtonian addition of a drift. This is generally a trochoid, not an exact Newtonian cycloid.
- **5.7:** six actual cold-stream distributions with independently transformed densities and momenta. A deterministic local kinetic state, not random samples or a thermal gas. Anisotropic stress is distinguished from perfect fluid.
- **5.8:** perfect-fluid rest isotropy gives (e+p)UU/c²-p eta. Dust p=0; pressure/energy ratio alone is not a complete EOS or stability proof. Passive observer boosts give material velocity -v.
- **5.9:** conservation is conditional on equations of motion for the total closed system, not tensor symmetry. An exact smooth isobaric contact/entropy profile supplies a nontrivial finite-volume example, with analytic stored energy/momentum and independently integrated boundary flux. No pressure-step/shock simulation is claimed. The frozen picture description is corrected accordingly.
- **5.10:** explicit incident-wave EM tensor and average pressure of a stationary opaque, specularly reflecting/absorbing surface. Instantaneous fields versus cycle averages are separated. The pressure curve is the response factor 1+R, continued at zero intensity; the unit wave shape remains defined there while the normalized matrix is defined zero. For strictly positive amplitudes the normalized matrix cancels the amplitude scale analytically, including subnormal saved amplitudes. No moving mirror recoil, transmission or boundary-superposition field is invented. The frozen picture description is corrected accordingly.
