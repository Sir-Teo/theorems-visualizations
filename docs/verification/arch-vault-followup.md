## Independent derivation and chosen model

Let the bay be `[-R,R]^2`, `f(s)=sqrt(R²−s²)`. The groin is `z=max(f(x),f(y))`; the old minimum has zero height all around the perimeter and represents a different vault geometry. On each perimeter edge the corrected surface is a semicircle. On a diagonal `(s,±s)`, plane coordinate `X=√2s` satisfies `X²/(2R²)+z²/R²=1`, so the diagonal span is `2√2R`, riseR.

Thickness is an **areal weight parameter**: `w=γt`, not an exact offset solid with finite-thickness intersections. One web family has area `4R²∫₀^(π/2)(1−sinθ)dθ`; there are two families. Thus `Ag=4(π−2)R²`, whereas `Ab=2πR²`. AtR3,t0.3,γ22: `Wg=271.2424144929348 kN`, `Wb=373.22120724646743 kN`; one groin receives `Wg/2`, one bay-corner `Wg/4=67.8106036232337 kN` **under the imposed allocation**. For n adjacent bays, interior corner piers receive two shares and the total remains nWg.

A short web arch between groins at strip coordinate s has arc length `2R asin(|s|/R)`. Splitting its vertical weight between its two ends and adding the adjacent family gives `q(s)=2γtR asin(|s|/R)` per unit s along a full diagonal: the public text specifies point(s,±s) and `dW=q(s)ds`, so this density is not silently interpreted per unit of diagonal plan length or curved arc length. This is zero at the crown and largest at the corners. Horizontal reactions of those short web arches are **omitted**, so the allocation plus vertical-only rib calculation is not a complete 3D force network.

For a three-hinged plane candidate, use the support-to-load lever `(R−s)`, not s. The vertical-only diagonal reaction is `Hd=√2 γtR²(3π/4−2)`; the barrel-strip reaction per unit nave length is `Hb=γtR(π/2−1)`. These are conditional candidates, not a containment/safety result for either vault. The orange plan vectors use these directions and one common scale per plan; interior longitudinal components cancel and transverse components add. The opening slider changes the wall geometry and marks the interrupted support line; the accounting never invents an alternative lintel or load redistribution.

For24 equal s bins, positive normalized endpoint u has primitives:

- `F(u)=u asin(u)+sqrt(1−u²)−1` for the mass integral;
- `J(u)=((2u²−1)asin(u)+u sqrt(1−u²))/4` for its first moment.

A positive bin has `P=2γtR² ΔF`, load coordinate `X=√2R ΔJ/ΔF`; reflected negative bins preserve exact symmetry. All24 records remain when webs are off, with zero loads and finite coordinates. No endpoint-singular midpoint quadrature is used.

The rib geometry uses the existing `archFromLine` polygon builder at25 mean points, normal joints and constant depth; its24 polygon weights are `γ×0.3×area`, applied at the polygon centroids. An independent triangle-fan centroid calculation checks this builder. Symmetry gives `V=W/2`. For left-half loads and half-span a, the candidate is fixed by `H R=Σleft P(a+X)`, hence passes through the mean crown and springings. Each joint's moment is affine along that joint; solving for zero moment gives its crossing fraction and force. Containment tolerance is `1e−8` in the dimensionless joint fraction, with positive compression required. This is a **joint-resultant polyline** for the stated discretization; it is not a continuous shell stress field. An outside crossing rejects only this candidate. The code never searches all equilibria or reports a minimum depth/collapse condition.

The continuous depth interval also has a closed-form nonsingularity check, rather than an inference from grid samples. Parameterize each block as `r(s,u)=p₀+sΔp+u(n₀+sΔn)`, with s in[0,1] and u in[−d/2,d/2]. The Jacobian is affine in(s,u), area is `d A₀`, and centroid `cx=C₀+C₂d²`. Evaluating the depth endpoints bounds every left-block centroid strictly between the support and crown for all d in[0.1,1]: minimum support lever0.1692843540m and crown distance0.1767806036m. The minimum area coefficient0.3535520283 and map Jacobian0.3239324903 remain positive. These are floating-point evaluations of explicit coefficients, with large margins, not interval-arithmetic certification. Positive loads and levers give H>0. Symmetry gives nonnegative shear on the ascending half and nonpositive shear on the descending half, matching tangent-y signs; tangent-x is strictly positive. Hence force·tangent>0 and no joint moment-intersection denominator vanishes in the continuous parameter domain. The seventh independent test verifies these coefficient bounds and off-grid agreement with the polygon builder.
