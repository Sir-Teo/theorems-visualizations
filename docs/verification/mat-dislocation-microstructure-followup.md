## Corrections and mathematical basis

**3.11:** Removed the false claims that every deformation twin is a mirror, that twinning needs no dislocation source, that uniform shear alone maps the hcp basis, and that a geometric zero implies easy physical twinning. Removed recalled material c/a markers and universal temperature/rate claims. The full fcc variant is explicit:n=[111]/√3,ℓ=[11−2]/√6,s=1/√2. The projected row period isP=√6a/4, distinct from the true in-plane repeat√6a/2; the per-layer twinning shift isa/√6, or2P/3. Earlier prose confused those periods.

For integer fcc coordinates(u,v,w)/2 withu+v+w=2m, the projectedx=(2m−3w)/(2√6) has phasema/√6 moduloP. At full shear its phase differs from reflected row2m₀−m by2(m−m₀)P, exactly an integer period. An independent full3D shear/reflection calculation maps primitive generators by an integer matrix of determinant−1, so this correspondence has an algebraic lattice basis argument; finite enumeration is only a regression. Visible points and hollow sites are generated analytically per row, eliminating the earlier missing-parent-row failure at lower−6/thickness9. The view follows the selected band, and the exact phase tolerance replaces the loose.02a coincidence threshold. The c/a=√3 button reaches the actual geometric zero; its readout states the physical limitation.
