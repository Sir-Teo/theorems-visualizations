## Corrections

**4.2:** Added circular, no-slip and long-tube hypotheses to the statement and precise author derivation. Removed unread detailed Poiseuille/Hagen priority dates and unsupported blood/plasma viscosity measurements; the default viscosity and density are identified as chosen model parameters. Calculates formal Reynolds number and length/diameter ratio, with an explicit high-Re warning and a reminder that low Re does not validate the remaining assumptions. The controls deliberately include formal ideal-model extrapolations; these are not predictions of actual blood flow. The profile is evaluated only within the tube; eliminated intentional NaNs outside it. Dynamic vertical scaling now contains even the tiny-velocity corner's peak marker; tube walls have frame margins. Closed Q and independent numerical profile integration remain visible.

**4.5:** Added the equal positive maintenance coefficient and viscosity assumptions needed to combine separately optimized vessels into a cubic tree with common shear. States uniqueness of the minimum and its cost ratio. Removed unchecked real-tree exponent ranges and the unsupported turbulent-exponent aside. Original pp. 212–213 limitations replace those claims. Previously the cost graph displayed only 0–2.4 times its minimum while the narrow-radius curve and draggable marker rose dozens of times above that, silently clipping them. It now shows dimensionless radius and normalized component/total costs on a logarithmic vertical axis; the full domain and gold marker fit. Relative schematic widths are disclosed. Readout still gives absolute optimum/chosen radius and power per length for the chosen Q,m. Flow slider now uses a linear 0.1 step so its true 50 mL/s endpoint is natively reachable (the prior logarithmic browser endpoint yielded 49 after rounding).

**4.6:** The previous finite bisection ceiling fabricated a pressure near 4000 mmHg when requested volume exceeded the saturating model's capacity, and its prose always ordered artery above vein even where its own slopes reverse. Replaced inversion with an analytical pressure increment `−p∞ log(1−ΔV/headroom)`, conditional on `ΔV < headroom`; otherwise returns an explicit no-finite-solution state. No pressure bar is drawn for a nonexistent solution. Finite pressure axes scale to the computed results. Tangents are limited to the positive-pressure display domain and markers have margins. Same-pressure comparison and nonlinear constitutive law are explicitly a toy; the source's linear compliance law is distinguished. Removed unverified whole-body blood-volume percentages and the false always-greater pressure-rise claim. Compliance ordering reversal is now disclosed and tested.

**4.10:** Pins the exact area-column provenance; computes every velocity from Q/A rather than suggesting all textbook numbers form a measured dataset. Replaces individual-red-cell transit claims with a uniform bulk-flow estimate and names omitted cell/bulk differences. Dynamic logarithmic upper limits now contain high-output aortic velocity **166.67 cm/s**, recruited capillary area **7500 cm²**, data labels and all markers; the previous 100 cm/s axis clipped the former. Recruitment is a controlled model change at fixed output, not an asserted complete exercise response.
