Rendered all source pages and read the full apparatus/procedure, chromatography, identification and limitation paragraphs. The initial dry gases were200mmHgCH4,200mmHgNH3 and100mmHgH2, with200mLwater and one week’s discharge. The original identifies glycine and α/β-alanine, but explicitly calls aspartic and α-aminobutyric acid **less certain** because of weak spots. The page now preserves that distinction; the original yield is described as the milligram range rather than a precisely reconstructed time series.

Climate reviewer acquired and read the full original Johnson et al.2008 *Science*322:404 plus supporting methods: https://science.gsfc.nasa.gov/sed/content/uploadFiles/publication_files/Johnsonetal2008.pdf. Original p404 is PDFp1, followed by SOM. It reports22 amino acids and5 amines in11 archived volcanic-apparatus vials; inspected original Fig1 and analytical methods. Supporting results warn that absolute experiment yields cannot be recovered because the saved volume is unknown; relative abundances were normalized to glycine. The chart does not represent those relative abundances as absolute carbon yields.

The exact Schlesinger–Miller1983 *J.Mol.Evol.*19:376–382, DOI10.1007/BF02101642, full article was sought through Springer, SemanticScholar, ADS and NASA but has not been acquired. Its complete indexed abstract supports the quoted1.2–4.7% methane band, about2% atH2/carbon2–4 and10^(−3)% atH2/CO2=0. An earlier original conference abstract uses1.5–4.7%, demonstrating why it is not a replacement for the journal’s full experimental report. **The full-source flag remains** for exact endpoint/protocol/condition alignment; the abstract is not falsely called a completed original-paper review.

Two independent computational defects are corrected despite that retained source flag. First,2.4% is an **illustrative value within the band**, not its arithmetic midpoint. Second, Y·(1−exp(−t/2)) did not reach the reported final Y at day7; the explicitly assumed shape is now Y·(1−exp(−t/2))/(1−exp(−7/2)). Both the day7 readout and curve reach the chosen endpoint. The guide’s logarithmic slope is computed exactly from its0.001-to2 endpoints, removing its tiny jump at ratio2. All intermediate/time-course values remain labelled assumptions, not measured observations. Removed an unsupported claim about what “most models now” say about the actual atmosphere.

**3.4 Great Oxidation: closed.** Full Philippot et al.2018 *Nature Communications*9:2245 DOI10.1038/s41467-018-04621-x, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5993798/, complete Introduction and Results/Discussion read, including the full argument and Figs1–5. Its actual conclusion directly contradicts the old readout “MIF-S gone by2.31Ga”: Western Australian MIF-S persists **beyond2.31Ga**, and recycling of old sulfur can outlast atmospheric MIF production. The disappearance is asynchronous between basins, so it cannot be used as one universal atmosphere-switch date. Corrected statement, caption, plot highlight and readout. Oxygen bands are now explicitly **editorial schematics**, not measured confidence intervals attributed to this paper.

Corrected buried organic carbon’s sign: **burial preserves net oxygen**, while oxidation of old buried organic carbon consumes it. The stoichiometric argument CO2+H2O⇌CH2O+O2 makes the distinction explicit. The model is a sequence of **equilibria**, not a numerical time integration of a real flux history. Its equation is S−x/(x+ε)−kx=0, ε=10^(−5), x inPAL; the saturating sink makes a rapid **continuous** crossover, not an exact discontinuity. Solving kx²+(1+kε−S)x−Sε=0 gives the stable positive root implemented in the separate helper. It agrees with an independent bisection across subthreshold, crossover and superthreshold regimes; statements about S≤1 are correctly restricted to the maximum fast-reductant sink, not an assertion of zero oxygen.

**3.5 endosymbiosis: closed with consistent counts and scope.** Full original Andersson et al.1998 *Nature*396:133–140 DOI10.1038/24094, complete PDF https://www.nature.com/articles/24094.pdf. Read abstract, *General features*, full *Reductive evolution* and *Mitochondrial affinities* sections, and the phylogeny/operon Figs4–6. The paper reports834 **protein-coding** Rickettsia genes, with separate RNA counts; it explicitly states that reduction in mitochondria and Rickettsia likely occurred **independently**. It also discusses nuclear proteins secondarily recruited from genomes unrelated to the mitochondrial ancestor. These passages refute the old ladder and the claim that every absent ancestral gene moved into a nucleus.

Full Rath et al.2021 *Nucleic Acids Research*49:D1541–D1547 DOI10.1093/nar/gkaa1011, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7778944/, official full XML https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC7778944/fullTextXML. The right schematic shows **coding origin**, with nuclear-encoded proteins imported into mitochondria. Dots are rounded blocks, not a claim about ancient gene-retention fractions or the physical distribution of finished proteins.
