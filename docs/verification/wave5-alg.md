## Source and mathematical scope

Read the actual [Judson Annual Edition 2023 PDF](https://judsonbooks.org/aata-files/aata-20230811-print.pdf), printed pp.97–115, §§8.1–8.4. The book's item numbering is preserved. Statements and proof ideas are newly written. All original chapter passages were available; no unsupported original claim was substituted from a secondary summary.

- 8.3–8.7: parity, repetition, likelihood and the memoryless binary channel. Controls label **q as flip probability**; the book instead uses p for correct transmission. Likelihood is nearest-distance only for q<1/2, all candidates tie at exactly 1/2, and farthest-distance wins above 1/2. Winners are selected using exact integer-distance ordering, including saved q values arbitrarily close to 1/2. Impossible endpoint events are distinguished from floating-point underflow at positive interior probabilities. Readout includes the complete q string. Seeded empirical frequencies and their Monte Carlo standard errors are separated from exact probabilities.
- 8.8a–8.21a: injectivity, binary Hamming metric, detection versus correction, nonempty XOR-closed groups and kernels. Minimum distance/weight statements explicitly exclude singleton minima. Binary rank/elimination is computed in GF(2), rather than using real-valued matrix routines. Dependent/zero generators and check rows remain available cases.
- 8.22a–8.28: **column-message convention**, H=(A|I), G=(I;A), matching dimensions and characteristic two are explicit. Every information word has one forced parity extension. Kernel enumeration and generator images are computed separately. Initial A matches Example8.23 and reproduces Table8.24 exactly.
- 8.30–8.37: zero columns collide with no error; duplicate columns collide with each other. The correction guarantee includes no error and requires at most one actual flip. Arbitrary-error stress mode exposes wrong recovery and missing syndrome columns instead of claiming universal decoding.
- 8.38a–8.43: all minimum-weight leaders are calculated and ties have an explicit capped-index rule. Maximum-likelihood interpretation states 0<q<1/2. The source's Example8.39 calls four length-five codewords a (5,3) code; their actual dimension is **2**, explicitly corrected in the item's scope and independently tested. Coset/syndrome equivalence includes rank-deficient H and unattainable check tags.

All numerical displays are exact finite examples except explicitly seeded channel experiments and floating-point channel likelihoods. Rectangular word arrays do not claim Euclidean Hamming geometry. Native integer sliders encode discrete words/matrix rows; probability controls use `step:'any'` and preserve actual continuous saved values. No opaque unsaved state is used.
