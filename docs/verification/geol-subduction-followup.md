## Corrections and construction

Removed “never deeper than700”, the universal300km minimum/600km peak, an exact returned-dip promise and the assertion that ordinary brittle failure plus one phase transition accounts for deep earthquakes. The retained phenomenon is geometric, with uncertainty and regional variation explicit. The model's age×rate proxy,90+0.11phi cutoff/cap and density are chosen teaching parameters, not data or a calibrated thermal law.

The old scatter changed displayed depths while the histogram/regression used hidden unperturbed depths. The revised construction samples depth from an explicit1024-bin midpoint CDF and adds6km-standard-deviation horizontal Gaussian scatter. Every displayed hypocentre, histogram bin and regression now uses the same pair of coordinates. It generates exactly the requested event count, with no rejection-loop truncation. Histogram heights are fractions of all events and sum to one. The slab is integrated until800km at every permitted dip and interpolated to event/cutoff depth; low-dip deep geometry is no longer truncated at a fixed2400km arc length. Dynamic horizontal bounds include the full path, events and fitted segment.

The fitted object is an OLS line x(depth), not a plane. Its dip is a summary of the chosen bent geometry and sampling, not necessarily the asymptotic control. Fewer than six events beyond100km yields an explicit unavailable result, not NaN. The dashed800km continuation is a geometric construction, not an observed seismic population.
