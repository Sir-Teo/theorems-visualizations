## Corrections

The local subcritical Hopf cycle is unstable; the stable firing branch is global, not “born stable at a subcritical Hopf”. A single recovery time does not establish an exact minimum frequency. Multiplying gate rates by3^((T−6.3)/10) leaves the voltage equation unscaled, so the old claim of a universal3.5-fold firing-rate increase with unchanged curve shape was unsupported. Parameters and units are now explicit.

The curves are sequential300ms **constant-current steps**, not continuous slow ramps. The descending history now begins at the actual final state of the ascending protocol. Independent-rest runs remain separate. Their rates use the actual last retained interval after100ms and require at least three events. Unresolved points remain gaps instead of fabricated frequency-zero data. The first resolved grid samples are reported as finite observations, not exact rheobases, proof of an asymptotic bistable interval or certified depolarization block. The nonuniform input grid is specified.

The old “spiking initial state” trace then turned input off for5ms before the selected step, so it did not directly compare the selected constant current from the claimed initialization. The trace now applies the exact selected current from time0, initialized at rest or at the final state of a separately specified100ms I20 warm run. Its own150ms count and finite rate after50ms are displayed. The nearest plotted grid current is **labelled with its actual I**, rather than presented as a calculation at an arbitrary dragged input. All three rate series determine the rate view, and both trace extrema determine its voltage view. A last-quarter voltage range accompanies the crossing detector: loss of0mV events alone does not prove that every oscillation has vanished. The full warm trace and sweep use temperature-consistent RK4 steps (.04/.025ms); existing shared integrators unchanged.
