## Editorial supplement13.39

Rudin’s chapter ends at13.38. The retained13.39 URL is explicitly labelled an editorial supplement and cites Engel–Nagel, *One-Parameter Semigroups for Linear Evolution Equations*, II.3.13–3.15 pp.82–84 and II.3.23 p.88. Complete definitions, propositions and proofs were read in the [author-hosted complete book](https://www.math.uni-tuebingen.de/de/forschung/agfa/members/engel-nagel_one-parameter-semigroups.pdf), SHA-256 `a48ba290280c718cbf6a65a6b09b4c3c59f622102619fe5b227d446c921a56ff`. The Hilbert dissipativity inequality, continuation from one positive resolvent to all positive resolvents, closedness from the lower bound/surjective range, and contraction generation were checked. A dissipative finite matrix has surjective I−A by injectivity; arbitrary matrices need not. The plot uses actual complex singular values of the triangular exponential and labels its241-point, finite-time peak honestly. A logarithmic ordinate avoids clipping growing examples.
