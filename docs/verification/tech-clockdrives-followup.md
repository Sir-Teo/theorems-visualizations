## 5.4: threshold-switched driven foliot

The old `−τ sign(angle)` law was a conservative V-shaped-potential oscillator. Calling that a driven escapement and universally applying its τ⁻¹/² period scaling conflated different models. The actual author paper has **hysteretic threshold switches**: drive reverses at +φ0 on the outward positive crossing and at−φ0 on the negative crossing. Viscous friction is β·angular velocity. The foliot overshoots both switch angles before turning.

The new helper reproduces source Appendix A. With a=β/I, μ=τ/I and x=μ/(a²φ0), the strictly increasing equation `atanhλ−λ=1/x` has one root0<λ<1. Its derivative is λ²/(1−λ²)>0, with endpoints0 and∞. The offered domain gives approximately0.372≤x≤957, keeping the numerical root representable. Period is `4(λ+1/x)/a`; peak time is `log1pλ/a`; amplitude is `φ0[1+x(λ−log1pλ)]`. The analytic half-cycle and its sign-reversed second half have continuous position and velocity, with opposite velocities at the two switches. The periodic model supplies exactly4τφ0 work per cycle; independent midpoint integration of βω² agrees with it.

An independent oracle solves **half-period y directly**, using `y−2tanh(y/2)=2/x`, rather than repeating the production λ root. This is strictly increasing for y>0 and has bracket[0,2/x+2]. It checks periods at all physical parameter corners plus an off-grid case. Independent finite differences verify the actual ODE and continuity, and direct dissipated-energy integration supplies a separate physical check.

Five continuous controls: weight position0.3…1.2, torque1…6, viscous coefficient0.5…2, switch angle5…30° and stationary phase0…1. The authored inertia is0.6+2xw². The full phase curve includes481 regular samples plus both exact turning points and the half-cycle switch. Phase changes the actual analytic angle, bar and weights; no fake wheel-contact trajectory is drawn. Ten-percent stronger-drive gain is1440(P/Pstrong−1) relative minutes per calibrated reference day; the outward-weight comparison recomputes the full model, not a universal scaling. These comparisons are explicitly not measured accuracy.

An extra exact-event check found that the original draft's `(time%P+P)%P` could round a positive half-period to the preceding side of the torque switch. The final helper only adds P for a negative remainder. Exact0/half/full-cycle torque conventions are now asserted, along with continuous angle/velocity. Final foliot native matrices were repeated after that repair.

## 5.5: one common torque normalization

The old plain-drum mode used radius1/0.7 in its profile but divided that factor away only in delivered torque. Thus the diagram and torque curve described different transmissions. The new authored model sets the same barrel radius and full-wind pulley radius to1 in both modes. For run-down u, ideal torque is M0=1−0.6u; real torque is `M=M0(1+0.6g sinπu)`. Fusee radius is1/M0 and plain-drum radius1. Both use the same `D=Mr/Rb` with Rb=1.

For g=0, fusee output is identically1 and plain output M0. In particular at u=0.5 they give1 and0.7 under the **same** reference. With a departure the compensating output is exactly1+0.6g sinπu. Every501-sample spring/radius/output curve, actual selected contact/chain segment, profile and readout uses these values. The readout says largest/smallest **sampled** output; it does not claim an exact global extremum. The profile's vertical coordinate is explicitly an authored mapping of state, not manufacturing pitch, elapsed time or solved winding kinematics. No clock rate is inferred.

Two continuous controls cover u0…1 and g−0.5…0.5; an actual visible switch changes modes. Both states retain their controls and exact reload. Positive spring torque throughout the domain is checked independently.
