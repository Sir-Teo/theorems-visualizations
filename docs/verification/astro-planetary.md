## Substantive fixes

Locking had a factor-two mismatch with the stated torque/Q convention and negative arrival times for slow spins. It now uses2I|ω0−n|/(3k2GM²R5/(Qa6)), approaches synchronism from either side and clamps there; real rheology is explicitly outside the toy. The locking plots now include short arrival times and resolve spin-up before those arrivals, instead of clipping the handle below a100-year floor or starting its history after locking was complete. A final focused locking light/dark pass also passed (/tmp/atlas-astro-planetary-browser-locking-final.log). The lunar positive-phase-lag branch stops showing states after formal contact or synchronism, and curve bounds include its displayed day lengths.

Mercury now states nonzero Fourier coefficients and torque balance as conditions, rather than claiming every commensurability is stable. Coefficients need not grow monotonically with e. Narrow spin range alone no longer diagnoses libration/chaos. Unsupported7%/55% capture numbers were removed; the40-spin counts use a disclosed finite-time mean tolerance and are explicitly toy outcomes.

The old Hill encounter used absolute boundaries30/60 Hill radii for every separation, producing a distant-kick coefficient about44 rather than the independent asymptotic30.09 and nonmonotonic errors. Boundaries now scale as100|Δ|/200|Δ|, with extrapolation and a table extending to16; the far continuation matches the independent linear impulse/Jacobi coefficient within0.4%. Close-encounter removal at2.6 is a chosen rule, not an integrated scattering prediction. Empty-survivor statistics are handled, and control changes now reset and rerun the same seed rather than retaining history from older parameters.

Io panels rescale to contain the high-power corner; the forcing toggle imposes fixed e or exponential damping and does not solve a coupled resonance. Jeans shading is a speed-population fraction, not the directional, speed-weighted flux. Its column drain time is explicitly local and formal, not atmospheric age. Log-domain times avoid heavy-gas exponential overflow; bars cap visibly at10^24yr and low-λ cases are marked outside the classical regime.
