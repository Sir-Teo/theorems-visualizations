Corrected the misleading implication that Wright and Guild both performed their experiments directly with the eventual common CIE primaries; their data were transformed to that system.

Corrections and mathematical validation:

- Deuteranopia incorrectly chose a projection plane using S/M, which depends on the absent M response. Brettel’s rule is S/L. The replacement implementation uses S/M for protanopia, S/L for deuteranopia, M/L for tritanopia, with branch comparisons avoiding divisions at black. Changing the absent response now leaves the simulated output invariant, and projections preserve neutral and anchor stimuli.
- Dichromatic reduced information is a two-dimensional coordinate space; the selected representative stimuli need two half-planes, rather than necessarily one plane. Confusion lines in chromaticity specify proportional surviving signals; displayed swatches are separately normalized to compare chromaticity, not claimed to have identical unnormalized brightness.
- Two primaries fail to span the full trichromatic space, but can exactly match tests already in their span. Revised blanket “two never match” claims. The matching controls retain the actual computed residual.
- Proportionality/additivity alone do not prove dimension three; the Grassmann card now states the additional trichromacy assumption. Peak-normalized cone curves do not show that one cone class is absolutely “much less sensitive”; removed that claim and identified tiny negative short-wave approximation artifacts.
- Maxwell’s old 630/518/465 nm values came from confusing physiological corners with experimental standards. Replaced them with the explicitly approximate experimental recreation above and removed the unsupported exact historical-unit conversion claim. Removed “first” priority wording. Grassmann’s Balance button now explains its intensity limits and residual rather than promising a white match outside the controls’ range.
