## Source audit and declared models

- 5.1: Asmundson and Oner, [IMF, Money: At the Center of Transactions](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/fandd/issues/series/back-to-basics/money), core functions and barter discussion. Sequential cycle allocation is an explicitly chosen coordination exercise, not a historical claim or proof of money emergence. Each good and token is conserved; no bilateral pair satisfies both wants for n ≥ 3. Inflation erosion is a separate prescribed-price-path calculation.
- 5.2: McLeay, Radia and Thomas, [Bank of England Q1 2014](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy), opening passage and linked article distinguish loan/deposit creation from mechanically multiplying reserves. Both demonstrations are accounting exercises. The geometric model assumes fixed reserves, full redepositing and no cash leakage; the loan ledger fixes reserves and creates equal loan/deposit entries. Its ratio is an illustrative target, never a current regulation. Subsequent funding and settlement constraints are stated.
- 5.3: Humphrey, [Richmond Fed WP00-7](https://www.richmondfed.org/-/media/richmondfedorg/publications/research/working_papers/2000/pdf/wp00-7.pdf), printed p.8 / PDF p.10, explicitly distinguishes an accounting identity from a theory requiring additional behavioral knowledge. Fixed continuous growth rates are chosen scenarios, not fitted data. Effective annual inflation is separately exp(pi)−1.
- 5.4–5.5: Blanchard, [MIT 14.452 Topic 6 (2007)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-452-macroeconomic-theory-ii-spring-2007/674c807c0213618b99592671bf2b002c_slides06.pdf), slides 10 and 13–17: exact gross nominal/real return relation, competitive wages, fixed labour, and neutral permanent money-level shock in a benchmark model. The visual's fixed-capital Cobb–Douglas closure is explicitly its own static example, not that course's complete intertemporal model. Point forecast is not equated with an expected random real return. No empirical adjustment horizon is inferred.
- 5.6: Nachega, [IMF WP05/221](https://www.imf.org/-/media/websites/imf/imported-full-text-pdf/external/pubs/ft/wp/2005/_wp05221.pdf), printed p.11 / PDF p.13, defines gross real money finance and inflation tax on existing balances; printed p.6 also distinguishes their difference as changing real cash demand. Our continuous derivative S=dot(m)+pi*m is derived explicitly. The exponential steady-demand curve and its peak are chosen, uncalibrated parameters. A nonzero dot(m) is only an instantaneous accounting perturbation, not another static equilibrium.
- 5.7: Sargent and Stachurski, [QuantEcon adaptive Cagan](https://intro.quantecon.org/cagan_adaptive.html), equations 33.1–33.4, supply the negative relation between real balances and expected inflation and adaptive expectations. Their discrete exogenous-money-growth model is not presented as our code. Our continuous constant-real-deficit closure is derived from m=100exp(−ae), e'=b(pi−e), and M'/P=d, yielding pi=(d/m−ab*e)/(1−ab). With a=1 year, b=.4/year, stability derivative at a steady rate e is b(ae−1)/(1−ab). RK4 uses maximum dt=.002 year and shrinks steps so estimated state changes do not exceed .005. Truncation at e>6 or |logP|>15 is clearly identified, not called a measured hyperinflation or a proof of singularity.
- 5.8: Hanke and Krus, [original compiled Hyperinflation Table](https://www.cato.org/sites/cato.org/files/pubs/pdf/hanke-krus-hyperinflation-table.pdf), Germany and Zimbabwe rows and footnote *. Germany October1923: 29,500% monthly, wholesale index, 3.70-day reported doubling. Zimbabwe mid-November2008: 7.96×10^10% monthly, exchange-rate-implied estimate, 24.7-hour reported doubling. Footnote identifies dual-market Old Mutual share-price parity. Intermediate graph points are explicitly hypothetical constant-rate 30-day conversions, never fabricated historical observations. Different indices remain explicit. The common-month conversion need not reproduce rounded reported doubling times exactly.
- 5.9: [San Francisco Fed (2006)](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/publications/doctor-econ/2006/03/inflation/), expected-inflation cost item1 / Example1, unexpected-inflation item1 / Example4. Chosen cash-inventory formula bY/C+iC/2 is independently minimized, with fixed chosen spending and withdrawal cost. Opportunity costs and zero-sum debt transfers are not relabeled total social welfare losses. All rates in this item are continuous; one-year nominal repayment is 100exp(.02+expected inflation).
- 5.10: Caballero, [MIT14.02 Spring2023 lecture4 transcript](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-02-principles-of-macroeconomics-spring-2023/1sWJWc4iYESuo4fu0vAqT8aS-KuSosPpq_transcript.pdf), money demand, income and opportunity return discussion. The semilog specification and set-valued zero-rate satiation extension are explicitly chosen. The code never claims that the exponential formula alone has a lower-bound equilibrium for arbitrarily large money supply. No modern central-bank operational estimate is inferred.

These sources were opened and their relevant text read online. Historical table entries are the only externally reported numerical observations in the chapter. Parameter values in all other demonstrations are chosen and labeled.
