## Corrections and decisions

**2.14 — Fourier.** The three heat **sources** in the original are Sun, surrounding space and internal heat; atmospheric properties modify heat exchange. Corrected the previous enumeration and removed an unsupported “first” claim and a modern-energy-budget narrative attributed to Fourier. The original explicitly discusses suppression of air exchange as well as radiation; the box and atmosphere are now presented as an analogy. The modern numerical hot box had a substantive defect: partial-opacity panes did not transmit downwelling infrared radiation from other panes/ambient space, and an evacuated transparent pane made Newton's system singular. The solver now propagates upward/downward grey radiative fluxes through every pane. A transparent isolated pane's irrelevant temperature is fixed to ambient. Independent tests check exact opaque-layer and fully transparent limits and global radiative/conductive energy conservation across 24 parameter combinations. Pane count can reduce transmitted sunlight, so the readout no longer promises every pane raises the temperature. The model is explicitly modern and illustrative, with its coefficients and separate planet-panel 240 W/m² forcing stated.

**3.8 — Pinatubo.** Corrected the lower-tropospheric reference timing from about one year to the paper's nearly 18 months. The 0.15 optical-depth peak, 0.42-year rise and one-year decay are chosen pulse parameters, not measured/fitted aerosol data. The 0.4–0.6 K band is a ±0.1 K guide around the approximately 0.5 K reference, **not** an observational confidence interval. The AR6 forcing efficiency is −20 ± 5 W/m² per SAOD. The comparison is between a toy ocean-surface response and a lower-tropospheric observation, which the scope now distinguishes. Forcing and response axes now include input extremes. Independent convolution tests compare the underlying integrator with the analytic response to an exponential forcing pulse when deep-ocean exchange is disabled.

**3.9 — evidence synthesis.** Read and matched Table 7.13, including the **one-sided** instrumental and paleoclimate limits. Fixed the missing open upper arrow on the paleoclimate very-likely lower limit. The Gaussian toy is distinguished from AR6's expert synthesis, with the flat prior on positive restoring feedback explicit. Reused the integrating reviewer's `sensitivityDistribution` to condition consistently on positive restoring feedback: densities integrate to one on that branch, quantiles use the same normalization, the displayed median is conditional, and Gaussian mass outside the branch is reported separately. The reciprocal of the unconstrained normal mean was previously labelled as the ECS point estimate, and negative-restoring mass silently disappeared. Very broad process-only cases now give a finite conditional 95th percentile and the correct conditional median (example after native handle drag: λ=0.56±0.80 gives median 4.87 K, 5–95% interval about 2.0–42 K, excluded Gaussian mass 24.14%). The numerical toy does not claim to reproduce published likelihoods or exactly regenerate assessed bounds.

**3.10 — Twomey.** Confirmed fixed-water droplet/optical-depth scaling in the complete original. The displayed two-stream albedo is a stated approximation, and the Monte Carlo uses a nonabsorbing Henyey–Greenstein slab, not the paper's absorbing-pollution calculation. The final AR6 Table 7.8 aerosol–cloud ERF is correctly −0.84 [−1.45, −0.25] W/m² for **2019** relative to 1750; this must not be replaced by the −1.0±0.7 value appearing earlier in the component assessment. The simulation separates unfinished photon paths from transmission and reports the count; no unfinished paths occur in tested extremes. The old 4000-scattering cap could silently count unfinished paths as transmitted. An exact cache key replaces optical-depth rounding, and the schematic albedo map now uses a full 0–1 scale so thick-cloud changes remain visible. Droplet-number controls can be inverted to demonstrate a sign reversal. Historical Conover assertions were removed; the generated track is explicitly schematic.
