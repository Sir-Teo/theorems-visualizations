## Independent model derivation

Set a=span/2=22.75m, R=k·span and c=a−R for k∈[.5,1]. The authored circular arc is r=c+Rcosu, z=Rsinu, with u=0 at the base and uₜ=acos(−c/R) at r=0. Its rise is Rsin uₜ. Define A(u)=∫ᵤᵘᵗ r(v)dv. For uniform surface weight w, W=2πwRA is the vertical weight above that parallel. Vertical equilibrium gives Nφ/w=−RA/(r cosu). With meridional curvature1/R and hoop curvature cosu/r, normal equilibrium gives Nθ/w=A sec²u−r tan u. The hemisphere's apex limit is−R/2 for both normalized resultants; a pointed apex has limit0. The zero-radius endpoint is set exactly rather than evaluated using cancellation-prone divisions.

Because A′=−r, Nθ/w=(A tan u)′. Consequently a signed band integral with ds=Rdu is R[A tan u] evaluated at the band endpoints. The primitive vanishes at both the base and the crown, including the hemisphere's limiting endpoint: the full signed integral is zero. Positive allocation is instead clipped to the base-side tensile interval before evaluating the antiderivative. Floating-point cancellation near the crown is reduced by expressing A using δ=uₜ−u as R[sin uₜ·2sin²(δ/2)+cos uₜ(sinδ−δ)], with a local series for sinδ−δ.

There is exactly one interior hoop zero. Let F=A−r sinu cosu; its sign agrees with Nθ inside the arc. Then F′=cosu[R(1−3cos²u)−2c cosu]. Writing y=cosu shows that this derivative changes sign exactly once, at y=(−c+√(c²+3R²))/(3R). Thus F decreases to one minimum and then increases to0 at the crown. Its base value is positive and its near-crown value is negative. Bisection on the strictly decreasing interval isolates the unique interior zero, without scanning for or omitting additional roots.

Two chosen height dividers are sorted and define three bands. Equal or reversed divider values remain supported; equal bands have zero width. Their nonnegative allocations sum to the same exact positive integral for every partition. Multiplication by chosen w=40kN/m² gives diagnostic force units, never surveyed or real chain loads. Span is the only borrowed dimensional context; the actual octagonal double shell is not the model.

Separately, an isolated block on a bed whose normal is the meridian has gravitational components mg cosu normal and mg sinu tangential. Coulomb friction alone suffices precisely when tan u≤μ.
