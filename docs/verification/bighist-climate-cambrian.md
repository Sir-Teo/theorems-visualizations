## 3.6 — Snowball Earth

- [North, Cahalan & Coakley1981, full NASA manuscript](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19810008165/downloads/19810008165.pdf): annual-mean insolation section gives **S₂=−.477**; outgoing-radiation fit gives A203.3 and B2.09; diffusive-transport section, printed pp17–20, Eqs20–24 defines flux, divergence, boundary conditions and equilibrium. Eq22 was inspected as an image: −∂x[D(1−x²)∂xT]+A+BT=QS a, where a denotes absorptivity. The source’s D=.649 is empirically fitted; ice albedos .32/.62 and −10°C ice criterion appear in the albedo section. Corrected −.482 to −.477. The demonstration labels its chosen Q0=340.25, 2K smooth transition, 24 cells and pseudo-time relaxation instead of claiming exact replication of the source. The diffusive boundary terms telescope in the independently checked energy budget.
- [Gough1981 original14-page scan](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1981SoPh...74...21G): full §6, printed p28, Eq1 inspected visually after OCR. Coefficient is **2/5** and exponent −1; the text describes a standard-model approximation and its limits. The demonstration explicitly chooses present solar age4.57Gyr; this is not Gough’s older4.7Gyr calibration. At700Ma the chosen law gives L/L0=.9423, approximately6% fainter.
- [Myhre et al.1998, complete four-page paper](https://sealevel.info/Myhre1998_98GL01908.pdf), §5/Table3:5.35ln(C/C0), concentration in ppm and forcing in W/m². The source itself discusses poorer fits at large changes. The control range is deliberately labelled extrapolated; its upper endpoint is not a physically valid atmosphere generated by this logarithmic approximation.
- [Kirschvink1992 original two-page chapter](https://courses.seas.harvard.edu/climate/eli/Courses/EPS281r/Sources/Snowball/kirschvink-1992.pdf), §§2.3.1–2.3.3, both scanned pages read: low-latitude glacial evidence, albedo mechanism, volcanic greenhouse escape, possible meltwater refugia, and BIF test. These are hypothesis/evidence claims, not a unique proof of global ocean sealing.
- [Hoffman et al.1998 complete original](https://www.snowballearth.org/pdf/Hoffman_Science1998.pdf), pp1342–1346, especially Namibian lithology/isotopic argument and p1343→1344 paragraph: the .12bar number explicitly applies **at present**. Ref25 also discusses uncertain CO2-cloud effects. Current copy removes the universal30° instability claim and the unqualified Cryogenian .1bar thaw threshold.
- [Caldeira & Kasting1992 author original](https://ciunit.org/publications/caldeira-1992-irreversible-glaciation.html), [full PDF](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1oLFitA-eCJxRLWo7_HOzN091Z9AdtODW), all three original pages read, including p227 Box1 inspected visually. Box1 Eqs1–6 specify the nonlinear radiative/CO2 polynomial model, .477 insolation shape, zero boundary flux and cloud assumptions. The .12bar/<30Myr calculation concerns today’s Sun. Their CO2-cloud reflection was discussed qualitatively rather than included in the numerical radiative calculation. Our hybrid EBM is not that model.
- [ICS2026/06 complete official chart](https://stratigraphy.org/ICSchart/ChronostratChart2026-06.pdf), full one-page chart inspected: Cryogenian approximately720–635Ma.

Numerical fixes: sampled CO2 sweeps now span exactly .3–30000, matching controls; branch continuation uses200 rather than40 relaxation steps and remains explicitly approximate; temperature axes expand for allowed high-forcing states. The readout distinguishes sampled branch transitions from measured climate thresholds. Added the actual used prerequisite `climate-4.4` (temperature-dependent albedo feedback), mirrored in syllabus. `climate-5.1` remains an explanatory link to the planned diffusive model.

## 3.7 — Cambrian explosion

- [Marshall2006 complete author review](https://cs.brynmawr.edu/Courses/cs361/spring2008/Readings/Marshall2006.pdf), pp355–370: fossil/anatomical uncertainties, preservation vs lineage origin, environmental/developmental/ecological explanations. The review does not establish the demo’s oxygen800–540, predation550–500 or toolkit850–600 endpoints; these are now explicitly **chosen illustrative intervals** in caption, controls and readout. The toolkit interval preceding the displayed window receives an actual offscreen arrow rather than a zero-width bar. The539–515 highlight is a display convention, not a unique universal explosion duration.
- [Matthews et al.2020 complete accepted manuscript](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/7ec82bc9-e734-4e84-93ca-1e44355235f0/content), §§5–8, especially DRK-10 and MP-14 sample interpretations on manuscript pp12–13 and age-model comparisons: **Drook574.17±.19/.24/.66Ma**, **Mistaken Point E surface565.00±.16/.22/.64Ma**; uncertainties include progressively analytical, tracer and decay contributions. Corrected the574Ma label from Mistaken Point to Drook; scope reports total uncertainties and the separate E-surface date. Source records dating/stratigraphic disagreement; numerical precision does not imply every Ediacaran horizon is equally well constrained.
- [Yang et al.2018 complete author manuscript](https://nora.nerc.ac.uk/id/eprint/521412/1/2018-JGS-Chuan%20Yang%20et%20al.pdf), Results/Discussion, manuscript lines197–284: youngest reliable detrital zircon518.03±.69/.71Ma gives **maximum depositional age**, not a direct exact fossil date. The authors discuss reworking and possible residual Pb loss. That qualification is now in the point label, scope and readout. Lines304–338 note diachronous first-trilobite occurrences and520.93/519.30 regional constraints.
- [Paterson et al.2019 full primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6410820/), introductory fossil constraints, analysis and duration discussion: oldest trilobite body fossils approximately521Ma; uncertainty in inferred pre-fossil roots; their analysis finds normalised trilobite morphological rates by519Ma. This supports the rounded observation, not a universal ending date for every animal radiation. The paper’s older541Ma boundary is superseded only by the separately cited current ICS chart.
- [ICS2026/06 chart](https://stratigraphy.org/ICSchart/ChronostratChart2026-06.pdf), inspected full chart: Cambrian base538.8±.6Ma; Stage3 base approximately521Ma.
- [Royal Ontario Museum, complete relevant Burgess dating explanation](https://burgess-shale.rom.on.ca/science/the-burgess-shale/geological-background/how-old-is-the-burgess-shale/): no direct radioactive date from the Walcott Quarry, fossil correlation gives its stated505–510Ma estimate. Scope explicitly identifies508 as a rounded representative within **that estimate**; its obsolete542Ma Cambrian boundary is not used. A future current primary calibration would improve this illustration.

**Retained gap:** Grotzinger et al.1995, Science270:598–604, DOI10.1126/science.270.5236.598, full original regional bio-/geochronologic tables and subsequent calibration needed to justify Cloudina’s displayed representative546Ma placement. Search metadata/abstracts were available; the complete relevant original was not. The scope says the placement remains unresolved. This is reviewed-blocked, not an untouched item.

Model correction: both drawn curve and readout solve dN/dt=r g(t)N(1−N) from N(−600)=10⁻⁴ exactly using integrated smoothstep exposure; they previously used inconsistent Euler steps(.25 vs.05Myr). For u=(t−τ+w/2)/w, exposure is0 before the gate, w(u³−u⁴/2) during it, and t−τ after it. Production bisection finds10%/90% crossings of this same solution. Independent tests integrate the gate with Simpson quadrature and compare the production curve; monotonicity, positivity, endpoint behaviours and no-complete-rise states are tested.
