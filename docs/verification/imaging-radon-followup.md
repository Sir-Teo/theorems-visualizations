## Corrections

**3.4 slice theorem.** The continuous L¹ identity is distinct from direct sums over ideal point samples. Ellipse edges are not bandlimited, so aliasing can occur below detector Nyquist and even at DC. The old claim of exact sampled agreement up to Nyquist and equal DC mass was false. The readout reports exact mass, sampled DC, the actual discrepancy, and the displayed frequency interval0–min(14,Nyquist); a larger Nyquist is explicitly outside the view. The sampled35°/12-detector phantom curve exceeds1.075 after mass normalization, formerly clipped by the fixed upper bound1. All displayed real/imaginary curves/dots now determine the view. Removed a caption instruction to enlarge an object for which no size control exists.

**3.5 uniqueness.** The former claim that a missing angular wedge destroys uniqueness contradicted its own compact-support hypothesis. A continuous nonempty open angular interval, with all offsets, determines the compact-support Fourier transform on an open cone; real analyticity gives injectivity, with severe instability. The classical integral formula is now stated for smooth compact support; arbitrary L¹ inversion needs an appropriate regularized interpretation. Finite directions are distinguished from a continuous interval. The numerical image performs interpolation and zero fill, not analytic continuation, and therefore cannot demonstrate failure of that uniqueness theorem.

The scoped Fourier reconstruction also repairs a negative-kx-axis conjugacy bug: atan2(0,negative)=π had been reduced to0 without conjugating the imaginary part. For the asymmetric chosen phantom this changed reconstructed pixels by up to.018726. Full-angle interpolation wraps through the conjugate first spoke; limited-angle interpolation stops at the last actual measured view, removing an unreported half-view angular extension. Full-precision grids retain row/physical pixel orientation and Fourier phases. Titles/readouts label interpolated coefficients rather than pretending all between-spoke values were measured. The fixed radial cutoff15.75 and finite64² grid remain explicit.
