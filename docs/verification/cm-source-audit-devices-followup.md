## Corrections and scope

**4.8.** Source-supported mobilities replace values quoted from memory; room-temperature values are deliberately held constant, without pretending to model temperature/doping scattering. Uses the declared carrier subset's DOS/gaps. Very low-doped Ge at400K exposed a negative built-in potential from the majority-only log formula: the full Boltzmann bulk-neutrality asinh expression now remains positive, and an explicit warning labels failure of the current law's majority-dopant approximation. The optional ideality-two extension now declares constant equilibrium width and depletion lifetime2tau; the prior hidden3% width floor and2% barrier floor are removed. Depletion geometry is omitted when Vj>=Vbi, with an explicit invalid-model label; the current law there is an extrapolation. Real reverse depletion width fits the dynamic spatial view. The series equation is solved monotonically over the physically bracketing interval, rather than an arbitrary[-4,V+.2] range. Full current values fit the dynamic logarithmic range; equilibrium current is honestly zero with only its marker at the labeled log floor. Ideality is reported only in positive forward current, not inferred from reverse absolute-current slopes. The Fn−Fp junction separation and bulk energy reference are consistent; compressed relaxation curves remain a schematic.

**4.9.** Removes remembered silicon radiative coefficients, universal direct/indirect ratios, invention chronology and unreviewed alloy-composition presets. Presets select nominal photon energies and reset to custom after a manual energy change. The assumed ABC rates conserve injected charge and give an analytic maximum at n=sqrt(A/C), eta=B/(B+2sqrt(AC)); with the chosen indirect coefficient the maximum is below0.2%. The small deterministic glyph ensemble is explicitly schematic, not Bernoulli sampling or accurate rare-event statistics. A larger UV view contains the whole photon packet. Invisible emission uses neutral false colour. Removed the false inference that1−IQE is the fraction of electrical power turned into heat and the claim that a photon gap is a strict minimum forward voltage. Photon yield, extraction/injection losses and wall-plug energy efficiency are distinguished. Cubic loss causes droop in this declared model; it does not establish the cause of real LED droop.

**4.10.** The old code subtracted nearly equal charge roots in weak inversion, clipped negative square-root arguments, forced zero current for Vg<=0 and displayed a flat depletion profile even for negative surface potential. A dedicated signed Boltzmann–Poisson first integral now handles both accumulation and inversion using stable expm1 remainders. Surface potential is solved numerically and the signed profile integrates its field; excess electron sheet charge is independently integrated from the minority density. This capacitor is an equilibrium source-end cross-section. Its threshold is the stated depletion approximation, not falsely the exact numerical crossing. The current plot is now an explicitly disclosed smooth interpolation, not an “exact Brews” solution: it has Hu's body-factor square-law limit and the weak-inversion exponential/drain limit, independently tested. Current and capacitor assumptions are separated. Full selected output currents and deep-subthreshold densities remain visible. The statement includes the body factor and makes no universal100mV upper bound on swing. Oxide continuations and the midgap energy approximation are declared schematic.

**4.11.** Replaces coarse energy scanning with one monotone phase root on each allowed parity interval, including shallow states near the barrier. Wavefunctions use analytic normalization on the **whole real line**, including infinite exponential tails, instead of a rectangular finite-window sum. The displayed wave amplitude is explicitly arbitrary. The right panel shows positive confinement shift on log axes: full finite and infinite well curves now fit, without clamping the transition marker to a false energy. All-space matching/current continuity, bound-state counts and infinite-well upper bounds are independently checked. The AlGaAs-inspired gap/mass/offset values are declared illustrative and do not recreate an unreviewed early experiment. Exciton/strain/band-mixing and very narrow envelope limitations remain explicit.

**4.12.** Corrects the S-versus-P selection rule and finite-n strength factor. Cu2O radius uses the actual primary paper's quoted1.11nm length and **integer n**, without an unsupported defect substitution into the hydrogen radius. The mean separation at25 is1039.515nm; twice it is the paper's roughly2µm full extent. P states start at2; no negative/invalid P1 radius can be selected. Removed unsupported remembered Cu2O dielectric/lattice inputs and universal GaAs three-state assertions. Lorentzian peaks are sampled at actual centers and nearby linewidth offsets, fixing a uniform grid that missed narrow peaks; dynamic axes contain their true heights. The line sum's unit reference is not falsely called a calculated continuum. The input is an assumed effective Lorentzian FWHM, **not instrument resolution**: a5neV laser can probe actual few-µeV lines, so its bandwidth cannot be inserted as their physical linewidth. Spacing>2Gamma is explicitly a heuristic, not a prediction of observed n. Hypothetical width buttons and historical actual instrument results are separated. Real asymmetry, inhomogeneity and power-dependent blockade are disclosed.
