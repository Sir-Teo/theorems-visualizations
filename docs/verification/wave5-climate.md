## Model choices and numerical checks

5.1 uses a conservative finite-volume operator on x=sin(latitude), exact cell integrals of quadratic sunlight, no-flux endpoint faces and a cut-cell ice fraction. The steady linear system is solved by Thomas elimination. Every cell balance and the area-mean energy identity are checked independently; uniform-cover profiles converge quadratically to the exact constant+P₂ solution. Temperature and poleward flux derive from the same solve.

5.2–5.4 use an explicitly stated slow ice-line law after thermal equilibration. A361-point response table, computed from960-cell solves, supplies the same piecewise-linear closure to diagrams, root isolation, scalar basins and quasistatic protocols. Snowball and ice-free endpoints satisfy one-sided inequalities. The default has five equilibria, with three attracting scalar states; no generic three-root S-curve is imposed. A coarse240-cell grid generated a spurious small turning wiggle near the small-cap fold. Refinement to960 cells removes that wiggle; default roots agree with a1920-cell/721-point refinement within0.03°. Exact model thresholds are not inferred from finite tabulation. The cooling/warming protocol has180 forcing increments per leg and no implied calendar time.

5.5 uses the exact carbon budget c'=V−Wc with an explicit dimensionless inventory threshold. The forcing is an authored linear map to the EBM's equatorial thaw condition; the modern logarithmic fit is explicitly not extrapolated into Snowball conditions. Exponential-integral/log1p ratio forms preserve zero and subnormal removal rates instead of overflowing V/W. A finite crossing is reported only when V>Wc*. Equality is asymptotic, not a finite escape. No ppm, millions of years or calibrated greenhouse destination is asserted.

5.6 integrates the literal two-species ODE with RK4≤0.05 and checks positivity/simplex errors without clipping or immigration. Empty populations remain exactly empty. Radiative area averaging holds algebraically; half-step refinements agree. Final derivatives distinguish finite trajectories from equilibria. Contrast zero gives the bare-planet temperature identically.

5.7 samples exact fixed-step transitions of the stationary OU linearization, seeded deterministically. Finite-record estimates are compared with population moments rather than required to match them. The fluctuation scale relative to the distance to the unstable fold point is displayed. The OU record has no nonlinear escape boundary and is never called a noisy nonlinear tipping simulation.

5.8 isolates both sign-branch cubics on a finite physical flow bound, splitting at derivative zeros to retain possible tangencies. Original-equation residuals and finite-difference Jacobians check every selected parameter family. The source's roots are approximately−1.067909798918,−0.307026886054,+0.219090341761, with stable-node/saddle/stable-spiral linearizations. Zero flow is nonsmooth: no invented trace/determinant is reported. Freshwater-flux and sverdrup calibrations are absent.

5.9 derives the height fold from h⁸−ch−a=0 and labels the dynamic closure as an authored scalar relaxation. Negative ground balance can leave an attracting ice-free boundary plus two positive branches. At exactly a=0, zero height is repelling for c>0; it is not mislabeled attracting. The environmental lapse is prescribed and not identified with the dry parcel adiabat. No Greenland threshold or melt-time prediction is made.

5.10 uses Budyko's original nonlocal mean closure and units. Quadrature of the separately icy/ice-free temperatures reproduces the mean balance. The albedo step permits a local temperature jump, with explicit boundary albedo0.50. A computed reference at72° defines the trial edge condition; the original geographical inputs and1.6% report are not silently recreated with new parameters.
