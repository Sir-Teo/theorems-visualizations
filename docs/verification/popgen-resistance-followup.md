## Corrections and exact model

The previous statement described resistance as usually pre-existing, universally costly and exactly piecewise linear in log-odds even while mutation and immigration were simulated. It also falsely said withdrawal could not cause decline without immigrants and treated combination treatment/refuges as simply reducing one scalar advantage. Those unsupported generalizations were removed.

The actual chosen generation is selection, sensitive replacement at rate m, then forward mutation mu. The equation shown now matches that composition. Its d=0 equilibrium solves `c*p²−B*p+mu=0`, `B=c+mu+(1−mu)m(1−c)`, with stable small root `2mu/(B+sqrt(B²−4c mu))`. The old40000-step initialization was replaced by that exact root; `mu/(c+m)` remains explicitly a weak-rate approximation. The recurrent-mutation toggle only changes generations after the initial seed and its consequence is stated.

Only selection alone has exact odds multiplier `(1−c)/(1−d)` and withdrawal multiplier `(1−c)`. With mutation/immigration present, the selection-only reference starts from the **actual state at application** and is clearly conditional. Its half-frequency time is compared with an actual full-map crossing during exposure and identified as beyond the exposure when appropriate. A reversed application/withdrawal interval now means no exposure, rather than silently forcing a generation of treatment.

The full model is evolved in log-odds using stable log probabilities and log-sum-exp transfers. The plot uses actual log10 frequencies on an ordinary vertical axis: extremely rare sensitive types below10^−400 remain represented without a fabricated floor or numerical extinction. Log-frequency is correctly distinguished from log-odds; only the rare-resistance part is approximately linear. Actual zero immigration is accessible. Scientific formatting prevents a positive mutation rate from appearing as0.0000. Screenshot inspection also caught a clipped exposure label at the bottom of a wide log range; its relative placement was corrected and the native run repeated.
