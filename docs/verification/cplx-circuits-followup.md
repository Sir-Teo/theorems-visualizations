## Corrections and mathematical scope

- **4.3:** Kept Lupanov’s supported `(1+o(1))2^n/n` theorem. The much simpler displayed block construction proves only the big-O order. Its proof now accounts for shared decoders, column groups and row combinations: `O(2^(n−k)+2^k)`, `O((2^k/s)2^(n−k))`, `O(2^k2^s)`. Choosing `k=ceil(2log₂n)`, `s=floor(n−3log₂n)` suffices asymptotically; the previous choice did not justify a leading constant1. Replaced the estimated DNF and multiplexer counts with actual generated AND/OR/NOT circuits and shared-input-NOT counts. The block helper’s independently reconstructed raw gate count and ordinary truth-table evaluation agree. Automatic search is explicitly finite; sampled ratios need not be monotone. Manual bounds now follow the input width; zero-gate constants have an explicitly disclosed floor on logarithmic plots. Removed an unverified Shannon–Muller co-credit instead of treating it as established.
- **4.6:** Over the binary domain, `C_n=a_n AND all_i x_i`, rather than a constant-advice circuit that would accept nonunary strings. Preserved the unary noncomputability/uncountability argument. Pink finite-simulation cells mean “not observed within the bound”, with no nonhalting conclusion. The displayed64-bit string is explicitly a bounded sample, indexed from the sampled machine0, rather than the uncomputable advice. The finite sample supports no claim about how often general machines halt.
- **4.8:** The proof now reduces a general polynomial witness predicate to SAT and bundles query lengths (or pads); it guesses a polynomial witness circuit and checks the original predicate. No unproved claim that a guessed SAT decider is correct is needed. The hash threshold is not called a certified global error fraction, and the DPLL-based oracle is explicitly a simulation. The final direct assignment check, not sampled statistics, supplies the soundness argument. Original Sipser credit is retained with a precise original reference.
- **4.9:** Fixed the direction and force of the conclusion: equality to the second level, and the original conditional corollary `P≠NP`, with no unconditional lower-bound assertion. Filled in polynomial-length symbol-bit addresses, a deterministic single-tape machine, fixed boundaries, halted-output padding, invalid-address guards and a designated final acceptance check. The CA model has periodic boundaries, illustrates complete local consistency, and does not supply a polynomial-size succinct certificate or check EXP acceptance. Counted initial-row constraints as well as transition windows. A random single window need not detect a lie; the full set does.
- **4.10:** Chose one precise uniformity convention and distinguished it from Cook’s stronger NC¹ convention. NC⁰ exists, with constant input support; logarithmic-depth necessity applies to functions depending on every input. Expanded every displayed XOR into **four actual AND/OR/NOT gates, depth3**. Chain/tree curves, sizes, selected markers and plot bounds now agree. Flat parity DNF has two AND/OR layers **plus the input NOT layer**, total depth3, and size `n+2^(n−1)+1`; negative literals are not free. Corrected the parity lower-bound wording to the precise supported class separation PARITY∉AC⁰, rather than an unqualified exponential-in-n claim; a quoted DNF construction does not itself prove that separation.
- **4.11:** Corrected the pictured left/right direction and explained associative composition of Boolean carry maps. One prefix-combine cell contains three gates; combine rounds are not single Boolean levels. The presentation states unrestricted fan-out, low-n-bit input reduction, initialization/final-XOR overhead and displays the final carry-out. The linear-size claim belongs to the more careful Ladner–Fischer construction; the displayed recursive-doubling networks retain their actual counts.
- **4.13:** Replaced the false “d+1 bits” claim and address-at-every-level stack with an executable branch-path evaluator that recomputes gate addresses. The readout budgets branch bits, saved values, phase/value bits, counters and a temporary address separately, excluding animation history and separately noting generator workspace. Used the direct configuration-graph construction for every NL machine, avoiding an unsupported closure shortcut through NL-completeness. Boolean products have an AND layer plus a **fan-in-2** OR tree. Identity edges, simple-path length `N−1`, and the displayed matrix’s derived-versus-original edge behavior are explicit.

### A defect in a primary printed proof

Karp–Lipton p.207’s displayed quantifier sentence for Meyer’s Theorem6.6 appears to omit a requirement that the guessed characteristic function accept the particular input `x`, and its displayed base-set disjunct does not enforce all terminal values. Taken literally, the recurrence condition alone is not a membership test for `x`. The theorem and attribution are separately supported, including the complete Arora–Barak tableau proof. The atlas uses that complete construction, explicitly checking the initial row, transitions, boundaries and **final acceptance**, rather than copying the defective printed display. This is a source-level correction, not a claim that finite CA tests prove the theorem.
