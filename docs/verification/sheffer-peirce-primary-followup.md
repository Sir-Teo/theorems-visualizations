# Sheffer and Peirce source comparison

Sheffer's [complete original 1913 paper](https://archive.org/download/jstor-1988701/1988701.pdf)
was read, printed pp. 481–488 (PDF pp. 2–9 after JSTOR's public-access
cover). SHA256:
`7636eddef1ea4eefd03eb5b131d5455d148ca5d881bf7891ef24f3c0eeda6154`.
The formal development in §1 and the complete §2 were checked; rendered
pp. 487–488 were inspected to resolve the connectives and final footnote.
The publisher's PDF endpoint returned 403; this public Early Journal
Content scan contains the full original article.

Theorem 1 in §2 derives negation and disjunction from NOR. The final
footnote on p. 488 explicitly supplies the dual interpretation as NAND.
The credit now distinguishes these, rather than suggesting that the main
1913 presentation uses the atlas's modern NAND notation. The page's DNF
argument and displayed gate networks are modern explanations of the same
functional completeness, not a transcription of Sheffer's postulate proof.

Peirce's complete *A Boolian Algebra with One Constant*, [Collected Papers
4.12–20](https://isidore.co/misc/Res%20pro%20Deo/Peirce/Done/4_local.html#cp4.fn25t.tm),
was read in the electronic edition, including the editorial heading and
notes. Downloaded volume HTML SHA256:
`914791579a6e82ecd6a89cdef4e9e534e93d0c7d2d7af12beb51533516f2b456`.
Paragraph 13 defines paired propositions as jointly denied; paragraph 20
and its table express Boolean operations with that primitive. This supports
an earlier NOR construction. The approximate c. 1880 date is editorial,
and the credit now says so. This evidence is a published edition of the
original text, not an inspection of the autograph manuscript. No claim
of a precise day, year of first publication, or direct influence is made.
The edition's corrections to elimination examples are not used to support
the atlas's independently stated Boolean identities.
