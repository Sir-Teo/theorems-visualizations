## Proof and model contracts

- Differential entropy is a signed Lebesgue integral when defined, with negative and infinite values distinguished. Histograms use exact cell masses, full support, analytic entropy and moments, including empty cells. Translation does not alter entropy; scaling changes it by log₂ of the scale.
- Quantized entropy convergence is proved for bounded, compactly supported Riemann-integrable densities, allowing moving grid offsets. Exact bin overlaps yield the cell-average density, corrected entropy and divergence identity. Dominated convergence proves the limit. Finite entropy alone and a universal quadratic convergence rate are not asserted.
- Gaussian and exponential-family maximum-entropy statements use continuous relative entropy with support, normalization, moments and integrability stated. The whole-real-line mean-only constraint has no maximizer. Analytic densities retain their full tails in calculations; finite plot windows do not truncate those calculations.
- Independent histogram convolution uses exact overlap lengths and analytic integrals of each linear-density segment. A stable series handles nearly equal segment endpoints. EPI's numerical example illustrates the inequality; its proof is the separate scalar transport argument with an explicit general-extension boundary.
- The real AWGN theorem imposes a per-codeword power bound. The converse uses Fano, entropy subadditivity and concavity. Achievability supplies a continuous information-density threshold argument, Gaussian power slack, removal of excessive-power codewords and maximal-error expurgation. The earlier finite-DMC theorem alone does not establish this result. Zero power is handled directly.
- The editable two-use code normalizes every codeword to its power budget, generates256 reproducible Box–Muller noise samples and performs nearest-neighbor decoding with lowest-index tie breaking. Its error count is empirical, not an exact probability or asymptotic capacity proof.
- Shannon–Hartley uses two-sided PSD N₀/2 and2W real orthonormal coordinates per unit time. Sampling follows from Fourier-series completeness, Plancherel and a Cauchy–Schwarz pointwise bound. The exact coordinate-block proof and the long-time physical-window equivalence are distinguished. The finite editable sinc expansion has full-time energy equal to the sum of squared coefficients and infinite tails; it is not a capacity-achieving code.
- Energy per information bit uses stable `expm1` evaluation, with ln2 as a zero-efficiency limit. The comparison parameter is a fraction of ideal capacity, not an unsupported claim about a named modulation scheme.
- Water filling computes sorted noise-floor crossings and actual intermediate allocations. Strict concavity and marginal-gain inequalities prove uniqueness and optimality. Ties activate together; zero power is explicit. The base floor and final rise remain separate, preserving tiny positive allocations that rounded-level subtraction would lose.
