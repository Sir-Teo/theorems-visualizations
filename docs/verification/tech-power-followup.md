## Explicit authored wheel model

The new helper keeps its uncalibrated hydraulic assumptions separate from the source tables. For jet speed v=sqrt(2gH), the undershot rule is rho Q×0.75×max(0,v−u)u. Its peak is exactly0.375 of rho gQH at u=v/2; the corresponding uncaptured version is0.5. These are properties of this particular one-direction momentum rule, not all impulse machinery. An independent polynomial derivation checks the result.

For buckets, fill=min(1,1/(1.5u)), with full fill at u=0; theta=(125+55(1−fill)) degrees. Radius is0.45H for overshot and schematic undershot phase,0.4H for breastshot. Carried flow is Q×min(1,1.5u)×[1−min(0.5,u²/(gR))]. Overshot power is the nonnegative part of carried-flow×rho×[gR(1−cos(theta))−u²/2]. Breastshot substitutes fall R[cos(pi/3)−cos(theta)] and adds0.6×rho Q×0.75×max(0,v_in−u)u, where v_in=sqrt(2g(H−1.5R)). Zero speed returns zero power. Every coefficient is authored; none is a fitted historical efficiency or general best-speed prediction. The original shared helpers remain unchanged for other unreviewed items.

The comparison uses exactly601 speeds0,0.025,…,15m/s. Hollow markers and readout maxima are explicitly largest grid samples. The selected control is never clamped invisibly to jet speed. The separate wheel diagram has a normalized drawing radius, while phase uses signed u×time/physical radius. It is a schematic rather than dimensional ancient or Smeaton apparatus. Independent tests check phase, flow scaling, nonnegative powers and the energy ceiling throughout the finite speed grid and offered heads.
