## Corrections

**4.14:** Removed the incorrect claim that Re alone determines dynamic similarity, the universal arterial transition boundary and the expectation of a murmur above it. Geometry and boundary conditions must match; pulsatility introduces additional parameters. Vessel-scale points and density/viscosity are clearly chosen model inputs. Synthetic pink noise is labelled as a teaching sound, not an actual stethoscope recording or a clinical prediction. Iso-Re curves now terminate at the actual positive log-map domain; labels move with viscosity. The view has margins around all native handle extremes. Particle markers remain inside the cartoon. Tiny positive Re values now use significant/scientific notation instead of falsely displaying zero.

**4.18:** Fixed the scaling claim: halving throat area quadruples the throat kinetic term, while the exact pressure difference also subtracts the unchanged upstream term. The simplified rule's discrepancy is now explicitly decomposed into the omitted proximal term and rounding of the coefficient **3.9753379** to 4. This distinction is visible even with zero narrowing: the exact drop is zero but the simplified expression remains positive. The recovery fraction is explicitly prescribed, without pretending to solve jet turbulence. Viscous loss is an assumption, not a guarantee for all short throats. Arrow-length capping is disclosed.
