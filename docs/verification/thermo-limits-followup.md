## Corrections and scope

- **5.11:** corrected the isobar degeneracy temperature from h^(4/5) to **h^(6/5)**. Solving Pλ³/(kT)=1 independently verifies the exponent. nλ³=1 marks loss of classical dilution; negative extrapolated entropy starts only at nλ³>exp(5/2). Added large-N/continuum/internal-state qualifications and removed unsupported historical measurement and data-provenance assertions. Entropy unit now is k per atom, not Nk per atom. Axes fit all five gas curves, h rescalings and selected-state markers.
- **5.19:** equal-temperature zero drift applies only at zero load; positive load produces backward drift. Carnot is a limiting engine ratio, with zero net work and heat at exact stall. Changes in physical parameters reset both simulation state and animation time, avoiding mixed histories. Rate and history plots include large negative drift. Prefactors, tight coupling and omitted heat leakage remain explicit model assumptions.
- **6.12:** added canonical Cartesian coordinates, flat measure, integrability and the actual vanishing boundary term. A separable quadratic coordinate contributes half kT; general power a|ξ|^s contributes kT/s under the full-line assumptions. Hard-wall position and periodic-angle counterexamples are tested. Simulations remain finite Monte Carlo illustrations; physical changes restart them and their clock.
- **6.14:** removed the uniform rotor-gap and “always classical translation” claims. Separates approximate plateau regimes from an infinite-temperature molecular claim. All retained model vibrational levels on the explicitly chosen increasing branch are included (18 H₂ /82 N₂), replacing unexplained 14/40 cutoffs. Rotor uses E/kT>55 and is checked against100 and independent J≤500 sums. Fixed normal-H₂ parity populations and equilibrium populations stay distinct. B_e use, omitted coupling/distortion/dissociation/electronic excitation and lack of a physical error bound are explicit; JANAF is a comparison, not a model fit or validation guarantee.
- **6.16:** distinguishes finite classical partition counting from leading-Stirling F,S; the N=1000 bar computation keeps lnN!. Corrected reference-dot colors. Removed silent clamping of the selected free-energy state; axes extend to its actual log(nλ³). Entropy curves fit all pressure/gas/temperature extremes.
- **6.17:** replaces the universal short-range/O(1/N) assertion with source-supported limiting hypotheses and distinctions between thermodynamic equivalence, concentration and coexistence. Finite model uses explicitly a backward difference, N≥2, genuine finite temperatures and an honest A(u)/N asymptote. Its entropy-density discrepancy is O(logN/N). Plot now uses energy per oscillator on a common horizontal scale as N changes; bins show unconditional probability divided by bin width, and retained mass is disclosed.
- **6.19:** states finite Z/weak bath coupling/fixed N. Zero-temperature degeneracy yields Π₀/g₀, not necessarily a pure state. Infinite-temperature I/d is finite-dimensional. Coherences *may* appear in another basis. Dragging the field through the origin keeps the previous unit direction, avoiding a zero Hamiltonian with nonzero analytic spin polarization. Removed the invented error floor in the diagonalization readout.
