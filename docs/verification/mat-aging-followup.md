## Corrections and independent reasoning

**4.9.** The spacing is explicitly a chosen regular-array surrogate. Cutting is an assumed square-root branch, not a universal law for all chemistry. With tensile branches `A sqrt(r f)` and `B(f)/r`, their unique crossing is obtained algebraically as `(B/(A sqrt(f)))^(2/3)`; its coarsening time follows directly from the cubic law. The UI now uses that exact crossing instead of a sampled maximum or unbracketed bisection. It distinguishes a crossing outside a requested window and the actual visible endpoint maximum. All current parameter combinations have their crossing inside the standard displayed window; explicit narrower-window regression cases exercise both outside states.

Temperature changes only the time scale at fixed fraction in this chosen model: its crossing height cannot fall merely from raising temperature. Both inactive branches are fully drawn on a logarithmic strength axis; no 900 MPa clipping remains. Radius limits include the maximum modeled radius. Only the three gaps between four visible particles are bowed, so no hidden neighboring arc leaves the view. The cut-half displacement is visibly schematic and exaggerated rather than falsely labelled one Burgers vector. Al-like constants and the 190 °C clock calibration are synthetic, with no alloy fit or historical priority claim.

**4.10.** The displayed Hamiltonian now matches the exact implementation: unlike undirected eight-neighbor pairs counted once, plus stored energy on deformed sites. Copying an orientation also copies its deformation state. Only nonpositive local energy changes are accepted; the two directions of state conversion remain possible, as small strain-free embryos can disappear. A theorem about energy decrease does **not** imply monotonic strain-free fraction or a universal numerical threshold.

Grain regions now use the same eight-neighbor connectivity as the dynamics, including periodic corner adjacency, rather than silently changing to four-neighbor connectivity. Initial samples are included. Readouts use the actual current state; plots sample every four sweeps and show current-state markers. Axes follow manually advanced and animated histories. A real advance-40 button makes the protocol inspectable. The alleged 60–140 HV calibration and claim that the simulation verifies a half-power growth exponent were removed. The optional decay acts concurrently and is labelled as an assumed bulk-energy function, while its square-root strength proxy is dimensionless. With zero embryo attempts the UI correctly says neighbor copying cannot create the strain-free state.
