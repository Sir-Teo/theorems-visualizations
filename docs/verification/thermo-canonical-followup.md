## Corrections

- **6.1:** Added weak-coupling, reservoir smoothness and partition-normalizability hypotheses. The leading finite-bath exponent correction is controlled by `S_R''=-1/(T²C_R)`, rather than a universal relative probability errorO(E/U_R). The finite Einstein-bath marginal is now normalized over **all**n=0,…,q using an exact binomial-ratio recurrence. Formerly, it was renormalized over the displayed0–12 states, and q<12 indexed undefined entries. Full means and total variation now include undisplayed states and the infinite geometric tail. The reference temperature is identified as a discrete first-ratio match. The Metropolis chain samples a correlated **marginal**; it does not traverse the full combined microstate space. Logarithmic panels omit zero probabilities explicitly.
- **6.2:** Defined the isolated-ground-level asymptotic as a ratio, required a finite partition function, and restricted the finite hot-state-count limit to a finite state space. Energy-zero dependence is explicit. Actual dragged levels, all three degeneracy lists, Boltzmann weights and stacked totals agree.
- **6.4:** Stated fixedV,N, a temperature-independent Hamiltonian and differentiability. RelativeN⁻½ scaling requires an extensive nonzero chosen energy reference and extensive response; an energy shift changes the relative width, and critical/coexistence response can invalidate that scaling. The histogram Gaussian is explicitly approximate, especially for a cold single oscillator. Bins retain unconditional mass and disclose omitted samples. Both full histograms and width curves/markers fit the views, including widths above3. The chosen oscillator slider now reaches every integer1–500: Chromium's logarithmic native-range grid ended at492, so this one scoped control uses linear integers. The mole-width readout is calculated at the actual temperature.
- **6.5:** DerivedF=−kTlnZ directly from Gibbs entropy andU−TS. The previous claim thatF→E₀ fixes the integration constant was false: a termcT has the same zero-temperature limit. Fixed-variable, temperature-independent-Hamiltonian and finite-N chemical-potential qualifications are explicit. Stable oscillator free energy replaces the sinh expression; local tangents and full curves fit the panels. Direct2400-state sums are independently checked against closed forms, with a geometric tail bound below3×10⁻³² at every slider corner. Removed the unqualified finite-solid chemical-potential readout. Free energy/energy, entropy and generalized-pressure units are separately labeled.
- **6.6:** Added the0ln0 convention and discrete/reference-measure distinction; removed the false assertion that Boltzmann macrostate entropy is meaningful only for a uniform fine-grained ensemble. The constrained maximum-entropy reference handles pure endpoints exactly and admits negative inverse temperatures for population-inverted bounded spectra. Dragging out of a pure state preserves normalization.
- **6.9:** Unequal-degeneracy **level** populations at highT are proportional to degeneracies; individual microstates become equally likely. TheC/T integral is the entropy **increase**, not the total entropy in the presence of ground degeneracy. Replaced a sampled peak search with the unique stationary equation `y tanh[(y−ln g)/2]=2`, proved from the displayed finite partition sum. Removed an overbroad claim about every unbounded spectrum. This is a smooth finite-system peak, not a phase transition.
- **6.10:** RestrictedJ to positive half-integer/integer quantum numbers and stated the independent isotropic-moment model. Curie accuracy requiresx=gμ_BJB/kT≪1; adjacent spacing alone is insufficient for largeJ. The plotted marker previously clamped actualx to[.01,6], while populations/readout used actualx reaching about336. The actual point now remains visible across the full slider range; the Curie tangent ends at the visible saturation range without flattening into an artificial horizontal segment. A stable finite-state calculation agrees with the coth Brillouin formula, normalized populations and spin½tanh; the Langevin limit holds at fixedx.

The new `content/thermo/canonical-source-audit.shared.js` uses stable shifted weights, centered energy variance, degeneracy-aware Gibbs entropy, exact finite-bath recurrence, oscillator closed forms, constrained maximum entropy and finite-spin sums. It is used only in the reviewed items; existing helpers and neighboring item blocks are preserved.
