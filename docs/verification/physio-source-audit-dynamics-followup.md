## Sources actually read

- Keener and Sneyd, [author-hosted circulation manuscript](https://www.math.utah.edu/~keener/books/control.pdf), complete §11.8.2 and §11.8.3, PDF/printed pp. **522–523**, and adjoining pp. **524–525** for the nonlinear distinction. Equation **11.161** is the compliance-chamber balance; **11.162–166** derive the linear wave speed and forward/backward solutions. This exact author artifact is cited without inferring its edition/year. Constant compliance and the half-sine input are explicit simplifying choices, not the manuscript's numerical data. Thin-wall conversion follows independently by differentiating area and hoop strain: dA/dP = Ad/(Eh).
- Womersley (1955), [original paper](https://pdodds.w3.uvm.edu/files/papers/others/1955/womersley1955a.pdf), *J Physiol* **127**, 553–563, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.1955.sp005276). Read complete pp. **553–556**, particularly momentum equation **4**, solution **9**, velocity-phase discussion **10–17**, and cross-sectional flow integration **18–24**. Read complete limitations pp. **562–563**: rigid wall and spatially uniform gradient are the two strong approximations; finite wave travel and arterial expansion require a broader theory. Original scans 554 and 555 support the equation where OCR is imperfect. The phase drawn here is *centre-line velocity*, not the paper's volume-flow phase. Its slight overshoot past 90 degrees is verified separately.
- Westerhof, Lankhaar and Westerhof (2009), [The arterial Windkessel](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11517-008-0359-2), §§1–2, pp. **131–134**, read as a **secondary review**, supporting the interpretation of the two/three-element circuits and their inability to resolve spatial wave reflection. The mean-resistance convention is derived independently for the actual series circuit used here.

## Corrections and follow-up

The inherited implementation evaluates periodic Windkessel pressure analytically rather than assuming a finite warm-up reached equilibrium; handles true cycle minima separately from the end-cycle pressure; distinguishes distal R from total R+Zc in adjacent 4.8; contains complete pressure/flow curves at extreme settings; constrains the observation site upstream of the wave reflector; computes exact piecewise pulse overlap maxima; and identifies anatomical radius presets as chosen examples. The Womersley follow-up narrows the quasi-steady claim from alpha <= 1 to **alpha much less than 1**, because the exact solution at alpha=1 already has a nonzero lag and is not an exactly synchronous parabola.
