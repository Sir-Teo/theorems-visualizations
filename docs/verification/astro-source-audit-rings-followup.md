## Source correction

The previous text says repulsive satellite kicks overcome an independent outward diffusion to explain physical confinement. The author treatment specifically explains the flaw in that picture. §IV Eq.(11) discusses diffusion in an unperturbed ring. §V.C relates satellite energy and angular momentum transfer using the Jacobi invariant, and shows the conflict with an unperturbed viscous prescription. Nonaxisymmetric perturbed flows can transport angular momentum inward during dissipation. Figures6–7 and the complete discussion distinguish locating a ring where net torque vanishes from actually confining it. The final statement and idea preserve that distinction. The visualization is explicitly a kick-plus-diffusion comparison, not a collisional model, a proof of confinement, or a historical lifetime estimate. Its moons do not exchange reciprocal momentum and it does not model collision stress or epicyclic energy.

## Independent mathematics

Expanding Newtonian planet gravity about the circular moon orbit and using n⁻¹ and RH=a(m/3M)¹ᐟ³ gives x¨−2y˙−3x=−3x/r³, y¨+2x˙=−3y/r³. With no moon, xg=2(y˙+2x) is constant. The conserved encounter energy is E=(x˙²+y˙²)/2−3x²/2−3/r. Far from the moon an epicycle of amplitudeA about guiding centreD has E=−3D²/8+A²/2. For a weak encounter, forcing evaluated on x=D,y=−3Dt/2 scales asD⁻² with a D-independent time profile, so the linear epicyclic amplitude isO(D⁻²). The first-order net guiding-centre change vanishes by the odd tangential impulse. Energy conservation then gives ΔD≈2A²/(3D), hence the signedD⁻⁵ leading scaling. This weak, passing, initially cold encounter argument does not define a universal strong-scattering boundary.

The2.6RH retirement rule is labelled a chosen prescription. Particles crossing the explicitly open0.8–1.2 radial box are retired at its boundary. Retired markers denote rule endpoints, not flight trajectories. Radial drawing stretch is reduced to3 so every positive boxed radius maps to a positive drawn radius. Histograms include the full box, are fraction of the **original** population per unit radius and lose area when particles retire; no survivor renormalization hides loss. Survivor statistics return null for no particles and readouts explicitly say undefined rather than dividing by zero. Mass, diffusion and presence controls reset the seeded paired experiment; both samples receive identical stochastic increments. No-moon test/reference samples remain exactly equal.
