## Sources actually inspected

- [Trefethen–Bau, Numerical Linear Algebra](https://www.stat.uchicago.edu/~lekheng/courses/309/books/Trefethen-Bau.pdf), Lecture 25, printed 191–192: companion matrix (25.3) and Theorem 25.1. PDF text encoding is defective, so the complete relevant printed page 192 was rendered and visually read (`/tmp/wave5-nla-tb-202.png`). The operation model expressly allows rational arithmetic and integer-order roots; the obstruction is exact and general for dimensions at least five. The widget makes no solvability claim about each individual quintic parameter and does not use numerical iteration to establish impossibility.
- [Bindel, Cornell CS6210, Oct. 21, 2009](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~bindel/class/cs6210-f09/lec23.pdf), pp. 1–2: invariant subspaces, Schur basis, algebraic/geometric multiplicities and diagonalizability. [LAPACK Users’ Guide](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node50.html) was also read from its actual HTML, including mathematical ALT text: complex triangular Schur form versus real quasi-triangular blocks and Hessenberg similarity reduction.
- [Bindel, Oct. 26, 2009](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~bindel/class/cs6210-f09/lec25.pdf), pp. 1–3: Gershgorin component counting and perturbation bounds. This source's global eigenbasis-condition bound is in the induced **1-norm**; the item explicitly labels that variant and supplies its own resolvent proof of the stated 2-norm version. Row discs are used consistently in the model and proof.
- [Bindel, Oct. 22, 2025](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs6210/2025fa/lec/2025-10-22.html), multiple-eigenvalue perturbation discussion: fractional-power sensitivity. The specific cyclic Jordan perturbation is derived directly by its characteristic polynomial.
- [Trefethen, Computation of Pseudospectra (1999)](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/trefethen/publication/PDF/1999_83.pdf), §3, pp. 6–7, equations (3.1)–(3.3): closed resolvent, perturbation and smallest-singular-value definitions. The complete pages were rendered and read (`/tmp/wave5-nla-ps-06.png`, `-07.png`) because the PDF's text encoding is unusable. The 2001 Wright–Trefethen Oxford repository excerpt originally found through search was not retained as the final reference after the full-page fetch returned 403.
- [Oxford EigTool, Transients menu](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/pseudospectra/eigtool/documentation/menus/transients.html): operator gain versus spectral decay. The widget's finite matrix exponential and gain are calculated directly; it makes no fluid-flow or nonlinear stability inference.
- [Bindel, Nov. 9, 2009](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~bindel/class/cs6210-f09/lec30.pdf), pp. 3–4: min–max theorem, dimension-count proof and compression. [Nov. 11 notes](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~bindel/class/cs6210-f09/lec31.pdf), p. 1: compression interlacing and ordered Weyl bound. The author supplies a direct spectral-basis intersection proof of Weyl so it does not secretly depend on the later min–max item.
- [LAPACK CHETD2 documentation](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/de/d79/group__hetd2_ga6fd9e9254a0b3a365541f2e36af51646.html): Hermitian-to-tridiagonal unitary reduction. The implementation retains computed small entries instead of overwriting them to fabricate zeros.

All parameters and plotted quantities are dimensionless. General eigenvalues use the atlas eigensolver; symmetric spectra and norms use the atlas Jacobi/SVD routines. Exact structural facts are never inferred from a floating-point rank threshold.
