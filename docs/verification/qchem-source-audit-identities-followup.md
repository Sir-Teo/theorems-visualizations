## Corrections

4.8 previously asserted a nonstationary trial state cannot obey the identity and used a frozen-width Gaussian as the counterexample. This is false: a parameter-independent state has zero state derivative, so its **own** energy derivative always equals its Hamiltonian-derivative expectation. The display now says exactly that; a different derivative from the reference measures approximation error. It distinguishes stationarity as sufficient, gives the general residual term, preserves the moving-basis/Pulay qualification and requires a differentiable branch/domain. Every reference is described as finite difference; endpoint secant residuals are shown, not declared exactly zero. All three energy curves, their tangents and all density peaks fit at extreme widths. Numerical density is normalized on the full box, displayed only in the declared window. Gaussian optimum is an analytic cubic root, not an opaque bounded minimization.

4.9 had **the opposite nuclear-force sign**. With physical F=−∇E, the correct relation is2T+V=ΣR·F. It also incorrectly said a zero scalar virial implies equilibrium. Both are corrected. The log potential is separate from degree-zero homogeneous potentials. The new local finite-grid solver uses one declared Dirichlet box for energies and states; its kinetic form includes **both** boundary differences. It no longer mixes Richardson-extrapolated energies with fine-grid expectations while implying exact equality. The readout reports energy-sum and virial residuals, mesh/box and tail probability. Potential drawing stops at the actual viewport boundary rather than carrying out-of-view path data. Numerical agreement illustrates the infinite-space theorem and does not eliminate boundary/domain hypotheses.

4.10 now limits codimension/conical shape to transverse independent conditions, allows tuned one-parameter crossings, distinguishes complex codimension3, and corrects the claim that Born–Oppenheimer energy curves cease to have meaning. The approximation can fail; eigenvalues remain defined. Exact zero coupling, not a tolerance, triggers the true-crossing text. Diabatic slopes match the actual0.9factor. After native camera rotation inspection exposed cone clipping, the 3D range was enlarged to contain the whole cone and its projection was checked numerically.
