The old simulation quietly changed the gene-copy count to 2Ne and rounded its frequencies to a displayed 32-copy grid. The new neutral illustration keeps exactly 32 integer census copies and uses a beta-binomial draw with concentration 32(2Ne−1)/(32−2Ne), giving conditional mean p and variance p(1−p)/(2Ne). At Ne=16 it reduces to binomial sampling. This is explicitly a moment-matched illustration, not Buri's sex-specific breeding protocol. A series control loads the appropriate initial line count and approximate effective size.

Corrected the right panel's units: it plots normalized allele-frequency diversity H(t)/H(0), H=mean[2p(1−p)], rather than the recorded fraction of heterozygous flies. Original founders were all heterozygotes (100%), whereas allele-frequency diversity is .5 at p=.5. Buri's actual genotype-heterozygosity recurrence also differs from the simple neutral diversity curve.

The diversity item 3.12 now states the assumptions needed to extrapolate census size to diversity, removes an exclusive “only if” explanation, and acknowledges ecological correlations. The observed band and comparisons are explicitly the 2012 compilation. The adjustable mutation rate and effective-size ceiling are teaching assumptions, and the ceiling need not put the curve within the band. Expanded the axis to show low-rate/small-census predictions.

The Kimura item 3.14 previously omitted the original's synonymous correction and used two-year generations. The default rate now follows 1.2/(28 million years × 300 nucleotide pairs) with four-year generations, giving one genome-wide substitution every 1.75 years, rounded to 1.8 in the original.
