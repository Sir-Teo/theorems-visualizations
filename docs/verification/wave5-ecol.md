## Sources, evidence and corrections

Sources accessed2026-10-21; statements/proof explanations are original. All simulation constants are explicitly authored, not estimated ecological parameters.

- **5.1/5.12 Holling:** C.S.Holling(1959), *Some Characteristics of Simple Types of Predation and Parasitism*, Canadian Entomologist91(7):385–398, [doi10.4039/Ent91385-7](https://doi.org/10.4039/Ent91385-7). Bibliographic metadata and indexed abstract confirmed; full publisher text was inaccessible in this run. No original numerical catches, fitted original search rates or experimental parameter estimates are asserted. The renewal/depletion simulator is explicitly authored. The long-run constant-density disc response is derived directly from its stated search/handling budget.
- **5.2 Charnov:** [author’s institutional repository](https://digitalrepository.unm.edu/biol_fsp/9/), Theoretical Population Biology9:129–136(1976). Item states the special repeated-identical-patch average-rate problem with a differentiable concave gain; calculus proves the criterion independently. The exponential gain is an atlas choice, not a Charnov data curve.
- **5.3 diet choice:** [Eric Pianka’s account](https://www.bio.utexas.edu/faculty/pianka/foraging.html), with [MacArthur–Pianka1966](https://doi.org/10.1086/282454). Two-prey criterion is independently derived from the explicit return quotient. Inferior-prey abundance independence requires a positive encounter rate; at zero abundance all acceptance probabilities tie.
- **5.4 hare/lynx:** [Krebs et al.1995 author-hosted paper](https://www.zoology.ubc.ca/~krebs/papers/122.pdf) supports interacting food and predation influences. Actual114 annual lynx-trapping counts1821–1934 are embedded, copied from the [Rdatasets CSV mirror](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vincentarelbundock/Rdatasets/master/csv/datasets/lynx.csv), with [official R dataset documentation](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/datasets/html/lynx.html), Brockwell–Davis1991 seriesG p557/Campbell–Walker1977 provenance. These are trapping returns, not paired hare/lynx census measurements. The separately scaled toy model is uncalibrated; no fit or causal inference is claimed.
- **5.5 ratio dependence:** [Arditi–Ginzburg1989 original article](https://sequoian.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Coupling-in-Predator-Prey-Dynamics-Arditi-and-Ginzburg-1989.pdf), J.Theoretical Biology139:311–326. Proportional equilibrium response to enrichment is specific to the displayed feasible ratio-dependent model. Stability is independently classified by its Jacobian; no universal stability advantage is asserted.
- **5.6 Huffaker:** [UC ANR publisher repository abstract](https://my.ucanr.edu/repository/view.cfm?article=152469&groupid=47&i=i&mailer=yes), Hilgardia27:343–383(1958). The primary abstract explicitly says the most complex arena ended after three oscillation waves. Its full scanned PDF exceeded the web reader’s size limit; numerical/time-series reconstructions of the original experiment are therefore not asserted. The integer patch simulator is explicitly a different authored mechanism illustration.
- **5.7 apparent competition:** [Holt1977 original publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0040580977900429), Theoretical Population Biology12:197–229. Actual model equilibrium and invasion boundaries are derived directly; positive effects under some alternative response/timescale assumptions are acknowledged.
- **5.8 non-consumptive effects:** Beckerman, Uriarte & Schmitz1997, PNAS94:10735–10738, [PubMed primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11038581/), doi10.1073/pnas.94.20.10735. Author-hosted indexed fulltext confirms the experimental risk-only spider manipulation; no experiment counts are invented. The multinomial allocation model is authored and keeps200 prey alive.
- **5.9 regulation:** [Anderson–May1978 original article](https://www.blackwellpublishing.co.uk/pdf/47-1-pp-219-247.pdf), J.Animal Ecology47:219–247. Their paper considers multiple parasite regulation mechanisms; our demographic SI system is explicitly an authored extension, not falsely identified as their exact macroparasite equations. Virulence need not monotonically increase regulation: sufficiently fast disease mortality can prevent invasion. Infection-specific fecundity reduction is not included in this particular model and is explicitly excluded from its scope.
- **5.10 aggregation:** [Hassell–May1973 original article](https://www.blackwellpublishing.com/pdf/42-3-pp-693-726.pdf), J.Animal Ecology42:693–726. The displayed gamma-risk/negative-binomial escape model is a transparent reduction; Jacobian unit-circle criteria are computed for that exact map. Aggregation can stabilize yet increase the host fixed point for the same other parameters. Stable dynamics alone are not proof of field control.
- **5.11 nestedness:** Bascompte et al.2003, PNAS100:9383–9387, [doi10.1073/pnas.1633576100](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1633576100), [author publication listing](https://www.bascompte.net/listpublications). Author direct publisher/PMC fulltext access was blocked; the independent reviewer subsequently retrieved the original5-page article from the WSL-hosted PDF and verified its temperature metric and Methods/null models. [Original paper via WSL](https://www.wsl.ch/lud/biodiversity_events/papers/Bascompte%202003%20The%20nested%20assembly%20of%20plant%E2%80%93animal%20mutualistic%20networks.pdf). No original observed network matrix is reproduced. The exact toy overlap score is defined explicitly and labelled NODF-style, distinct from their temperature statistic. Fixed-link null models do not preserve degrees; no robustness/stability conclusion is inferred.
