## Model and retained functionality

All four previous literary proxies remain with both type/token modes. They share4.13's declared NFC/limited Latin alphabet/token rules and letter lengths excluding apostrophes; syllables remain explicitly authored vowel-group estimates with English final-e/ed/es adjustments. Every1–7 group needs≥5 types/tokens, and larger estimated counts are omitted. Within-group bars are population SD of this orthographic proxy, not confidence intervals or phonetic measurements. Existing accepted Gutenberg provenance from the wordstats/language-model reports applies; the underlying strings remain byte-identical.

Three original-table choices are added with their actual populations/units and optional printed predictions. Unknown per-group source counts/SD are not invented; count mode is hidden/preserved for original rows. The toggle is likewise hidden/preserved for proxies. All hidden saved controls still validate. No input, numeric handle/slider, random or opaque history exists.

Equal weight per displayed group fits ln y=ln A+b ln x−cx with centered normal equations. Both coefficients are signed. Power/exponential special cases respectively fix c=0/b=0. Derivative signs are evaluated over the actual observed x interval; endpoints and any interior stationary point enter the sampled curve and bounds. The app reports strictly signed slope where certified by those derivative endpoints, or that its slope is not one strict sign. It does not falsely infer a universal trend from coefficient names. The green curve refits rounded observed means; gold points are the original printed rounded predictions, not a claim of byte-exact reproduction of original unrounded computations. The right panel's x·mean total and constant-first-group comparison are arithmetic, without a proved speaker-compression counterfactual. Complete SD, curve extrema, measured points and the whole comparison line fit the actual bounds.
