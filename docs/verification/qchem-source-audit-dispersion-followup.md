## Corrections

4.11 corrects the factor-two error in the isotropic sum, states nonoverlap/isotropic ground-state/continuum/retardation qualifications and separates the one-frequency approximation from its additional ionization-energy choice. Removed unsupported universal accuracy and unexamined benchmark claims. The original `sqrt(1+k)+sqrt(1-k)-2` loses tiny shifts by cancellation; an algebraically rationalized expression now retains negative shifts even at k1e−10. Energy log bounds include all selected curves/markers at all slider corners. Cloud bounds include all500samples and ellipse; unstable coupling shows no ground state. Analytic versus sample correlation and schematic oscillator coordinates are explicit.

4.12 removes contradictory claims equating a fitted model tail with the true atom coefficient, and the unsupported causal claim that fitting necessarily makes C6larger. The119.8K/3.405Ånumbers are declared chosen illustration parameters. The old B2tail correction was **positive** for attraction; it should be negative. We replaced the finite40Åcutoff/coreapproximation/tail correction entirely with compactified **infinite-range** Simpson integration using expm1. No overflow is silently converted into hard repulsion. All27model/temperature reference cases agree with independent log-radius quadrature plus a correctly negative remote-tail term.

The exp-6 integral is explicitly for a **hard-core modified** model. The unmodified attractive origin diverges and has no finite classical B2. Its inner maximum is solved by the scalar stationary equation, rather than a0.01Åscan ending at0.4Å: at steepness24the actual core is0.140626Å, which the old scan missed and instead excluded essentially the entire well. Native steepness endpoints now retain the right well and integral. All variants change their tail coefficients as well as walls; no sensitivity attribution conceals this. The reference σline is labelled12–6, not falsely the selected model's zero. Boyle temperature uses a bracketed integral zero instead of linear interpolation between sparse temperature samples. Potential drawing is explicitly limited to its finite energy viewport and B2axes contain the full temperature curves.
