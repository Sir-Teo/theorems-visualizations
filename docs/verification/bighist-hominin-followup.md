## Findings and corrections

The former eight recalled species age/capacity boxes and regression through their invented midpoints were not an actual fossil dataset. They have been replaced with four literal historical summaries from printed p208, retaining their different meanings: an approximate 400–500 cm³ interval; H. habilis/rudolfensis mean 654, SD 96.2, n=8; an approximate 850 for earliest African H. ergaster; and an approximate 1400 modern-human summary. A one-SD whisker is not presented as a confidence interval or full range. The first interval's copy value 450 is explicitly a chosen midpoint, not a measured mean. No fitted evolutionary trend is drawn.

The original Table 1's reported reference human is distinct from its Eq. 3 allometric reference. Table 1 uses a 65 kg male, brain mass 1.3 kg, mass-specific brain power 11.2 W/kg, rounded brain power 14.6 W and total basal power 90.6 W; its reported brain share is 16.1%. Directly dividing the rounded numbers gives 16.1147902869757%. Multiplying the mass and coefficient gives 14.56 W before rounding. Eq. 3 separately uses `3.39 M^.75` watts; at 65 kg this gives approximately 77.6041 W, not 90.6 W. The former 3.4 coefficient and implicit 0.0119 W/cm³ conversion are removed.

The authored accounting calculator now states its conversion: volume V times density proxy rho divided by 1000 gives assumed kilograms, multiplied by 11.2 W/kg. Its second reference bar uses Eq. 3. The volume, body mass and density ranges are illustrative inputs, not fossil physiology. Cranial capacity does not measure living brain mass or basal metabolism. The source itself cautions against interpreting the comparative organ analysis at the individual level; its proposed energetic mechanism does not prove that diet uniquely caused brain enlargement. These limits are stated in the item and summary. The shared chapter/syllabus opening clause no longer certifies an unsourced universal tripling chronology.

The source selector and the three continuous inputs are saved independently. Changing a source entry does not silently overwrite a chosen calculator volume; only the explicit copy button does so. Continuous saved inputs require finite values in the stated domain, at most 15 significant digits and exact physical range-input roundtrip. There is no hidden random/history/camera/audio state. Credit contains no modern publication year that the timeline parser could mistake for an evolutionary date.
