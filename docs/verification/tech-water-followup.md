## Primary evidence

**4.2 recipes.** Read Vitruvius, *De architectura* II.5.1, II.6 and V.12.2, comparing the [Gwilt 1826 translation](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Vitruvius/2*.html), its [Book V](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Vitruvius/5*.html), and the actual [Morgan 1914 edition scan](https://www.chenarch.com/images/arch-texts/0000-Vitruvius-50BC-Ten-Books-of-Architecture.pdf). Morgan printed p.45 (PDF67) specifies three parts **pit sand**, or two parts river/sea sand, to one lime. II.6, pp.46–47, describes the Bay of Naples powder and underwater binding; V.12.2, p.162 (PDF198), specifies two to one for the harbour powder. Inspected actual page pixels for pp.45, 46 and162. The former universal 3:1 pozzolana recipe was unsupported.

[Jackson et al. 2013](https://open.metu.edu.tr/bitstream/handle/11511/39621/index.pdf), *American Mineralogist* 98:1669–1687, DOI10.2138/am.2013.4484: retained the actual 19-page article, read abstract/materials and mineralogical descriptions plus the thermal-model appendix. The Baianus Sinus sample supports C-A-S-H binder and Al-tobermorite in relict lime clasts. The appendix uses particular material and thermal boundary assumptions; it is not an ambient-temperature universal durability model. Inspected actual pp.1669 and1686. No measured phase fraction, ancient strength or source thermal history is replayed.

[Seymour et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.add1602), *Science Advances* 9:eadd1602: retained the actual PDF and complete [primary XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9821858/fullTextXML). Modern Mix1 contains OPC, fly ash, sand, water and added quicklime, rather than being an untouched ancient recipe. Fractured/re-mated specimens and their clast-free controls were monitored through 30 days. These experiments support a proposed mechanism; they do not measure every Roman structure. The item makes the distinction explicitly.

**4.3 historical text.** Read [Vitruvius VIII.6.1–9, Gwilt](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Vitruvius/8*.html), and Morgan actual pp.244–246 (PDF282–284). ## Models and corrections

**Mortar.** The capacity, moisture factors, rates, logistic carbonation front, two-face wall and ash ratio are explicitly authored proxies. Each displayed strip has exactly60 cells; the curve and readout independently average those same cell centers. Conversion is a+(1−a)b, so the carbonation fraction applies to remaining capacity, rather than ignoring the hydraulic reaction's use of it. The submerged comparison sets b=0 as an assumption without asserting dissolved CO2 is absent. The entire logarithmic age family is plotted. Endpoint roundoff from exponentiating a logarithm is bounded back to the declared age interval.

**Aqueduct.** Source, city, both reservoir heights and the complete comparison polyline are fixed by the chosen terrain/gradient. Cumulative Euclidean length includes the vertical tank connections. Available head determines ideal capacity through Darcy–Weisbach; requested discharge has a separately computed loss. Unsupported demand no longer pushes the outlet far below the plot. The complete capacity grade gives the plotted gauge-pressure profile, and the readout separates arrival height, discharge capacity and nominal membrane stress. Dynamic bounds contain every route and pressure sample. The finite midpoint classifications and their counts are labelled approximations.

**Clock.** The small-orifice, quasi-steady model integrates A(h)/sqrt(h) for A proportional to h^(2p), with fixed final head0.15, using stable log1p/expm1 formulas. Its p=1/4 equal-level case is exact. All specified runs are normalized to12 equal illustrative hours; this differs from the Karnak seasonal scale. The error plot contains the entire241-point sampled curve and independent selected state, including the cone error that exceeded the former fixed±120 minute range. Its reported largest sampled error is not called an exact continuous maximum. Constant-head mode now draws a supplied source **and separate constant-area receiver**; the receiver level indicates time. Varying source profile cannot falsely change that assumed linear receiver scale.
