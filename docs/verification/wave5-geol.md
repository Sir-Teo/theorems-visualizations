Slab subtraction and exact model terrain subtraction remain distinct.

Airy and Pratt: constant-g column pressures and exact mass balance, with positive density contrasts and common compensation depth. Density ratios are computed, not universally fixed at6–7. Unsupported/uncompensated comparison states disclose the missing balance.

Flexure: homogeneous linear thin plate, buoyancy foundation and no horizontal compression; symmetric line load and sinusoidal forcing have analytic exact profiles. Edge flexure uses general decaying cosine/sine solutions, derives zero/bulge positions and a synthetic thickness inversion without claiming observational fit.

Rebound: homogeneous incompressible Newtonian half-space, small surface slope, sinusoidal modes, no elastic lithosphere; derive τ=2ηk/(ρg). Instantaneous versus finite-ramp unloading and two-mode mixtures expose single-exponential limits. Synthetic values are not Scandinavian observations.
