## Corrections and scope

- **3.3 DCEL:** A face can require multiple boundary components, rather than one `next` orbit in general. Isolated vertices and the unbounded face's null outer pointer are explicit. The demo is restricted to connected straight-line plane graphs with at least one edge. It rejects coincident vertices, edge-interior vertices, overlaps, crossings and nonincident touches before constructing pointers. The half-edge click selection promised by the caption had no handler; it now works. Trees have an explicitly identified unbounded face, and bridges can appear twice in one boundary walk. The scoped construction uses exact cross-product angular order: a valid four-vertex fixture with three almost identical ray angles previously produced one face instead of three when `atan2` rounded the angles together.
- **3.5 triangulation:** Replaced the circular angle-sum justification with diagonal splitting, `m₁+m₂=n+2`, and the weak-dual tree/two-leaves proof. The diagonal witness includes vertices **on** the candidate `uw`; a closed-triangle emptiness test is required. Simplicity, convexity, segment intersections and the selected diagonal's midpoint-interior check use exact predicates for stored binary coordinates. Valid flat boundary vertices work; self-contact and adjacent overlapping edges are rejected. A failed ear search reports failure instead of certifying a completed triangulation. The final step now reports the completed triangles separately from the number actually clipped and highlights the existing final-output pseudocode line.
- **4.4 backwards analysis:** Stated bounded feasibility and a consistent canonical lexicographic optimum. The probability counts constraints whose removal **actually changes** the selected optimum; counting every tight constraint was false with redundancies. Exact rational vertex enumeration independently compares every possible deletion. Fixed box sides are not randomly deleted. The fixed-prefix deletion probability and the separate4000-random-order frequency are distinguished; finite frequencies can exceed a theoretical probability bound. The sampled total is a mean, not a certified expected bound. Plots include probability1 and full bounded-box optima/regions. The verification enumerator is not presented as Seidel's expected-linear algorithm.
- **4.5 enclosing disk:** Restricted unique-center existence to nonempty inputs; supports may have one, two or three distinct points. The rebuild's linear cost is **expected**, not deterministic: the two-boundary routine is deterministic linear, but its parent needs its own backwards-analysis expectation. Explained the nested conditional2/j bound, uniform preceding order, and the fixed-input/random-permutation scope. Replaced fixed-epsilon containment and near-collinear fallbacks with exact diameter/incircle predicates and exact binary-input support calculations. Duplicates, collinearity, right triangles and cocircularities are checked. Every animation prefix has a separate exact-containment diagnostic, excluded from plotted algorithm test counts. Curves are five finite shuffle runs versus one deterministic distance order, not a proof of an asymptotic growth rate. Current/previous disks and all points fit the view; a nonfinite drawing failure is reported honestly. Theorems use the usual arithmetic-operation model; exact BigInt costs and diagnostic checks are not claimed to run in real linear wall-clock time.
- **4.7 arrangements:** Removed the hidden60-unit bounding-square cutoff. Sign sequences along entire lines enumerate whole affine cells, edges/rays and incidences; a rectangle clips **only shading**. Actual arrangementV/E/F and query-face counts agree with their general-position formulas when applicable. Parallel/concurrent configurations have separately computed counts; coincident lines and collapsed queries are rejected. The before-insertion zone counts boundary-edge incidences with multiplicity. Its demonstrated10m upper bound comes from the full textbook induction. The classical9.5m+O(1) result instead counts after inserting the horizon. For genericm-line inputs, splitting old crossed edges adds2m incidences and the new horizon adds2(m+1), so the after-count equals the before-count plus**4m+2**. The plot uses the applicable10(i−1) bound and discloses that its bars are update-work proxies; the sign-vector enumerator is not a timed quadratic DCEL implementation. Full curves, points and viewport shading fit the actual panel.
