## Scoped method and behavior

The score is a transparent plug-in independent-Poisson log-likelihood contrast. Factorials and exposure powers cancel; rates use the declared authored smoothing(count+.5)/(training words+1), with equal candidate prior odds. This does not implement the article's uncertainty-adjusted Bayesian method. Zero is explicitly a tie. The16-word set is a fixed teaching choice, and leave-one-out only refits rates; it is not independent validation of feature selection. Known selected essays reuse themselves in the displayed full-training score and are prominently labeled; their leave-one-out results are separate. Joint/Jay/disputed essays never train either candidate.

All85 essay rate points,12 score bars and seven choices on each axis remain, with all three existing word sets. A new exact integer essay selector and real scatter click select an essay and expose its per-word score contributions; no custom input/random/opaque/drag/audio state. Every numeric raw saved selector value is validated locally before constraints. Full path/marker/bar bounds include zero-rate points, selected gold rings and both signs of the complete scores. No arbitrary vertical score cap remains. Example55 is Hamilton under the three-word illustration and Madison under16 words, consistent with the intended ability to inspect sensitivity rather than promise uniform classification.
