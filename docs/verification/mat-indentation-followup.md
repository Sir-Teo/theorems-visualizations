## Corrections

Rockwell C is now independent measured differential-depth arithmetic: `100−Delta h/.002 mm`. Both readings are at 10 kgf around the 150 kgf total-force cycle. The UI has a separate differential-depth slider/drag handle and displays its force and datum diagrams. No Vickers depth, fictitious HRC conversion, clamp, or tensile-strength conversion remains. The slider's extended arithmetic range is identified as such.

The spherical representative strain was missing a factor of two: `.2 a/R = .2 d/D`, not `.2 a/D`. For the selected rigid-plastic power law, the ball contact root is obtained algebraically and checked against its hemisphere before any fields are rendered. Unsupported contacts no longer crash with missing diagonal/depth fields or fabricate hardness. The curve plots only its supported exponent interval and actually recomputes radius/representative strain at every exponent.

Vickers depth now follows the correct face-midpoint section: `halfwidth=d/(2 sqrt(2))`, `h=halfwidth/tan68°`. The plane outline and section are distinct. Its sloping-area HV pressure differs from projected-area mean pressure by `sin68°`. Brinell uses spherical-cap area with cancellation-safe cap depth. Both conventional numbers are distinguished from mean pressure in MPa. The entire ball outline is no longer drawn outside the viewport. Exponent zero is accessible, and the full pressure-ratio curve, including values above 18, stays visible.

The reference stress is explicitly defined at plastic strain .002, with chosen C=3 and sharp representative strain .08. These are synthetic model conventions, with material dependence, elastic transition, residual/contact size and omitted recovery stated. Unread steel correlations and unsupported historical dates were removed. Screenshot inspection caught default-left text clipping and nonrounded handle decimals; both were corrected and the native run repeated.
