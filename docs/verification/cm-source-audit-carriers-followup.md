## Corrections

**4.1.** The parabolas, phonon energies, optical prefactors and chosen Si valley location are now explicitly illustrative, while the declared band-edge values have actual parameter evidence. Removed calibrated absorption-depth/material-comparison claims. The old indirect-transition arrow clamped the final momentum into the drawn zone and thereby changed its final energy: it now solves the selected absorption or emission energy exactly, and reports an out-of-branch final state instead of changing it. The Ge path scale is Gamma–L, sqrt(3)pi/a, rather than Gamma–X; tick labels update with the chosen crystal. Zero absorption has an explicitly labeled marker floor on the log plot; small positive coefficients remain nonzero in the readout. Direct square-root and indirect quadratic edge powers require allowed transitions, 3D parabolic bands and slowly varying matrix elements.

**4.2.** Switching gap shrinkage off previously fixed the Boltzmann gap but left the numerical Fermi–Dirac calculation with a temperature-dependent gap. Both now use the same selected gap. Stable hole occupancy avoids cancellation from 1−f in the deep valence tail. The local Arrhenius apparent gap is Eg−T Eg′+3kBT; the old interpretation incorrectly used 1.5kBT. A pair costs Eg while each carrier density has an Eg/2 activation exponent. DOS values are a coherent Hu Table 1–4 set, not conductivity masses substituted into DOS formulas or a blend of incompatible compilations. Very small positive intrinsic densities are no longer floored to one carrier/cm³.

**4.4.** Removes remembered measured donor binding energies and unsupported central-cell comparisons. Rounded host-inspired scalar parameters are declared approximate. The left panel is a slice of the quantum ground-state envelope, not a classical orbit; only 1−5exp(−2), about32.3%, of the radial probability lies inside the drawn Bohr radius. Custom parameters with a radius comparable to lattice spacing explicitly fail the envelope assumption, rather than being presented as valid physical donors. The a*/a<5 warning is a disclosed visual heuristic, not a theorem. The dynamic ladder includes all allowed binding/thermal energies; top annotations are separated after screenshot inspection. Ed/kBT alone does not determine thermal ionization, which also depends on chemical potential and dopant density.

**4.5.** Strong compensation previously made a hole-dominated material incorrectly read “intrinsic.” Intrinsic-like now requires comparable n,p; acceptor domination has its own label. Both electron and hole curves are drawn at actual positive computed values, with a dynamic log range that includes the minority tail; a negative net-donor reference is omitted rather than plotted as a positive floor. Stable ionized-donor occupation and an adaptive neutrality bracket cover the declared30–1000K domain. The FD evaluation is numerical, not exact; the donor model assumes fully ionized compensating acceptors and neglects heavy-doping physics. The simple three-regime account now explicitly requires negligible compensation and moderate doping.
