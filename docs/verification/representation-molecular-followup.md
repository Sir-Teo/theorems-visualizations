## Corrections

Its scope now specifies an isolated non-linear molecule at stable equilibrium in the harmonic approximation, with symmetry-preserving masses. The explanation distinguishes the five rigid degrees of freedom for linear molecules. The Hessian statement now uses mass weighting. Multiplicity counts copies of an irrep; each copy contributes its dimension in normal coordinates, so the two E copies in ammonia account for four coordinates.

The picture previously projected arbitrary displacement vectors without removing rigid motions.
