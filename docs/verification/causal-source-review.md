Corrections beyond source metadata:

- An arbitrary joint structural-noise law does not imply the DAG Markov property: independent disturbances or explicit common causes are needed. Re-estimated finite-sample regressions can shift when parent distributions change even if their population mechanism stays fixed.
- Chain/fork/collider statements now concern isolated triples or paths whose other connections have been checked. Open paths permit dependence rather than forcing it. SWIG prose and readout distinguish a graphical guarantee from accidental independence.
- The back-door explanation formerly claimed that outcome and treatment become conditionally independent despite a surviving causal path. It now uses the intervention/observation exchange argument, and states required positivity.
- Noisy-OR zero-probability conditions previously produced invalid divisions. Impossible alarms now show an undefined conditional law explicitly; zero posterior columns are omitted and conditional bars marked undefined. Removed the false “AND-like effects imply positive dependence” rule: deterministic AND=true fixes both causes.
- Equalizing stratum mixes removes the weighting difference, but is not the unique point where a reversal disappears. Berkeley favors women in4of6 departments, not every department. Neither adjusted nor pooled observational contrasts automatically identify a causal effect.
- M-bias adjustment claims allow zero/special coefficients; removing confounding in the extended graph requires checking the entire graph, not either hidden cause in isolation. Mediator adjustment needs its own confounding assumptions to identify a direct effect. A proxy does not d-separate the original back-door path.
- A failed sufficient front-door criterion removes the guarantee; it does not force numeric failure in every model. Lord’s effect bars now adapt to actual estimates/error bars rather than clipping valid24kg settings at8kg.
