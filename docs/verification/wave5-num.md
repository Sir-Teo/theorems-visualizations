## Primary sources and mathematical scope

- [de Boor, piecewise cubic interpolation notes](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~deboor/412/pc.pdf), all three pages read: cubic Hermite construction, C² matching, complete/natural/not-a-knot end conditions, complete-spline error bound (4), and the explicit warning about incompatible natural endpoint curvature. The chapter uses the 5/384 fourth-derivative bound for exact-slope complete interpolation. It does not assert that general C4 data have global fourth-order natural interpolation. Uniform x² exhibits a nonzero first-cell midpoint error divided by h² even though f⁽⁴⁾=0. Natural sin(pi x) has compatible zero endpoint curvature.
- [de Boor–Lynch, On splines and their minimum properties (1966)](https://ftp.cs.wisc.edu/Approx/lynch.pdf), §1 and its projection argument: the admissible class has continuously differentiable g, absolutely continuous g′ and square-integrable g″. The natural cubic interpolant uniquely minimizes the squared second-derivative functional. A local integration-by-parts proof is paraphrased; this functional is distinguished from geometric curvature.
- [de Boor, CS515 spline lecture notes](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~amos/515/spline.pdf), pp.4–6: Cox–de Boor recurrence, support, nonnegativity and partition of unity. The new cubic basis uses an open knot vector, repeated endpoint knots, simple interior knots and explicit right-endpoint limits. Local coefficient support is distinguished from the generally global effect of refitting one interpolation datum.
- Driscoll–Braun, Fundamentals of Numerical Computation: actual [piecewise linear](https://fncbook.com/pwlin/), [cubic spline](https://fncbook.com/splines/), [finite difference](https://fncbook.com/finitediffs/) and [finite-difference convergence](https://fncbook.com/fd-converge/) passages. Counting of Taylor moments and amplification of absolute function-value errors are explicit. Finite stencils are computed by differentiating cardinal polynomials, not merely displayed from a lookup table.
- [Per-Olof Persson, Math128A Chapter 4](https://persson.berkeley.edu/math128a/lecture_notes/math128a_lecture_notes_chapter_4.html), §4.2, both integer-power and even-power Richardson expansions. The base formula selects the matching factors 2,4,8 or 4,16,64. Repeated cancellation needs a sufficiently long asymptotic expansion and can amplify rounding.
- [Trefethen, Spectral Methods in MATLAB official author page](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/trefethen/spectral.html), official sample Chapter 6 and cheb.m listing in the [sample PDF](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/trefethen/book.pdf). The matrix differentiates the Chebyshev interpolant at the nodes; its diagonal cancels the row sum. Analyticity supports geometric convergence with possible algebraic prefactors. Nonanalytic |x|³ and explicit alternating sample noise expose limits. All displayed derivative errors are nodal maxima; centered differences use a distinct interior uniform node set.

All statements and proof ideas are original paraphrases or explicit finite derivations. Numerical tests and sampled curves are not offered as universal proofs. Log zeros are displayed at a stated 1e−17 floor, with space below the floor so the curve remains visible.

## Models actually computed

The helper solves natural and clamped curvature systems using Thomas elimination, including the endpoint slope equations. Not-a-knot uses a small pivoted dense solve of the actual two third-derivative matching equations. Each cell stores four local power coefficients; function and both derivative panels use them, with interior mismatches and boundary values reported. The operation count is the measured 8N−7 scalar operations in the Thomas solver, excluding assembly/rendering.

Bending energy is integrated exactly from linear second derivatives. The competitor is a global polynomial vanishing at all five data knots. Five-point Gaussian integration is exact for the displayed per-cell polynomial energy/cross integrands, apart from floating arithmetic. The minimum-energy graph uses the independently computed orthogonal decomposition, while the selected competitor energy is integrated directly.

The B-spline demo evaluates the full recurrence on its editable knot vector. Its selected coefficient change vanishes exactly outside support. The locality demo separately refits a cardinal natural spline, evaluates the full global cardinal polynomial and modifies one B-spline coefficient. The complete spline convergence demo samples 32 substeps in every cell; it labels these maxima as sampled. Its signed errors are divided by a displayed power-of-ten scale for readable ticks; the readout retains unscaled errors.

Difference weights come from explicit cardinal-polynomial differentiation. Richardson tables contain actual function evaluations and recursive cancellations. Chebyshev D uses the chosen endpoint barycentric factors; application sums off-diagonal differences to annihilate a constant input exactly. Noise is added only to the spectral sample vector and is explicitly described; the centered-difference comparison uses the exact reference function.
