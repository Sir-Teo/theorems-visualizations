## Model and proof contracts

- Broadcast agreement and honest-sender validity are separate from strong-unanimity consensus. Byzantine decisions are excluded from guarantees. The three-process oral-message exception for two-party broadcast is explicit.
- Hexagon outputs are conditional logical obligations of an assumed correct protocol. They are not fabricated outputs of an executable universal consensus solver.
- EIG records all distinct-ID paths, including paths containing the inspecting observer; self-delivery is a local copy. Correct-ending nodes preserve their raw values and form a common path cover. Scalar deliveries and stored entries are counted for this declared redundant representation.
- Phase king executes all three rounds: proposal thresholds, two-bit endorsements, provisional preference and king adoption. At least one of f+1 distinct kings is honest. Unsupported n/f settings are labeled separately from successful individual runs.
- Graph routes are maximally packed by exact internal-vertex-mask dynamic programming. Global connectivity is found by exhaustive cuts. Correct endpoints and fixed predecessor-checked routes are premises; selected-terminal success is not global consensus. The general cut reduction is identified as an explanatory outline.
- Signed broadcast uses an ideal issuance ledger with nested chain/session binding, distinct signers and round-matched lengths. Freshness, forgery, old-session rejection and deliberately unsafe late/early variants are visible. The all-but-one guarantee is broadcast; a separate partition argument explains the honest-majority necessity for strong-unanimity consensus.
- Quorum intersection uses distinct identities and an explicit honest-voting rule. Intersection alone does not supply a view-change protocol or liveness.
- PBFT uses n=4,f=1, actual backup prepare certificates, prepared-plus-three-commit local decisions and three view-change reports carrying actual evidence. It models one slot and one change to view1, with no higher-slot holes. Timer behavior, checkpoint transfer, client retries, pipelining and full recovery are excluded. A rejected replacement creates no new commits. Certificates are ideal and verifiable by every receiving replica; a pairwise MAC tag alone is not a transferable signature.
- The block tree stores actual parents, unit work, explicit publication and a keep-current-tip tie rule. Fixed deficit, finite horizon, Poisson confirmation approximation and exact stopping-time confirmation risk are separate quantities. A tie counts as catch-up, not guaranteed reversal. Collision resistance does not imply the independent mining-trial assumption.
- The Paper preserves its bibliography and `introduces: ['dist-6.1']`. Its newly drawn OM(1) reconstruction distinguishes counterexamples to a selected rule from the general impossibility theorem.
