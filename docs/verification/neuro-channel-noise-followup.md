## Corrections

- Open-channel variance uses pNa=m³h/pK=n⁴. Absolute SD and coefficient of variation are distinguished. Shared voltage couples the freely evolving channels; the finite-N nonlinear expectation is not asserted to satisfy deterministic HH.
- Replaced the former approximate Poisson/Gaussian binomial sampler and at-most-one-jump updates with a new scoped implementation. Under fixed voltage, each independent two-state subunit has its exact exponential transition matrix. Convolution gives the full K-count matrix; the m-count/h product gives the Na matrix. Multinomial count draws use floating-point binomial inversion, without Gaussian/Poisson substitution. All integer populations are conserved. A subsequent exponential voltage update with frozen new conductances gives a splitting approximation of the coupled system. It is explicitly not a continuous-time Gillespie simulation, not the algorithm used for the source's plots, and not experimentally fitted.
- Deterministic event counts are actually computed: input6 can produce transient spikes, contradicting the old universal “stays at rest” claim. Seeded finite samples need not vary monotonically with area. Channel density/20pS assumptions and actual rounded populations are explicit.
- Pulse trials are independent of the constant-current slider, use9µA/cm² on[2,3)ms, and observe until12ms. The complete0–10ms latency range is included, including its endpoint. Bars sum to the observed-crossing fraction out of all40 trials. Empty/singleton summaries are unavailable rather than NaN. No observed crossing is right-censoring, not proof of a missed evoked response; a post-onset crossing may be spontaneous.
- Voltage extents include all displayed trace values; rasters use integration-resolution interpolated events. The two actual step selectors expose .025/.0125ms. Halving the step does not create an exact paired stochastic trajectory.
