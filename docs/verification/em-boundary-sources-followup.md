## Independent derivations used for the corrected illustrations

For the slot, direct integration gives the four coefficient families. All320 coefficients were independently integrated by128-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature split at every profile break; quadrature never crosses a jump. Laplacian finite differences test interior points and the side/decay conditions. A2000-term boundary calculation reproduces1.178979744 near a constant-slot edge.

For the sphere, integrate each Legendre polynomial exactly using (Pℓ₊₁−Pℓ₋₁)/(2ℓ+1). The normalization is the spherical area average. Parseval gives the squared tail as ⟨V₀²⟩−ΣAℓ²/(2ℓ+1). Independently split polynomial quadrature checks all124 coefficients and all124 truncation errors. The meridian harmonicity check uses Vxx+Vyy+Vy/y, the three-dimensional axial Laplacian, rather than incorrectly applying the two-dimensional one. One-sided radial differences check the surface-charge jump.

For the fringing inverse, write Y=|y|,0<v<min(Y,π) and r=(Y−v)/sinv. Then x(v)=1+logr+rcosv. Its derivative is strictly negative, since

`x′(v) = −[((Y−v)+sinv cosv)²+sin⁴v]/[(Y−v)sin²v]`.

The endpoint limits bracket every point off the plate slits. The y=0 case uses the strictly increasing scalar u+1+eᵘ. Numerical residual polishing improves floating-point accuracy in the deep gap; it does not replace the monotonicity proof.

For SOR, the SPD quadratic energy also gives a direct convergence check independent of sampled histories: one coordinate update changes the energy by −ω(2−ω)rᵢ²/(2aᵢᵢ). A full sweep is a strict contraction in the energy norm away from the solution when0<ω<2. This does not imply monotone maximum residual at every sweep. Independent dense elimination solves smaller Dirichlet systems and checks their SOR solutions.

For the ellipse, set z(θ)=ceⁱθ+ct e⁻ⁱθ, c=(a+b)/2,t=(a−b)/(a+b). Factor the logarithmic kernel as

`−log|z(θ)−z(φ)| = −logc −log(2|sin((θ−φ)/2)|) −log|1−t e⁻ⁱ⁽θ⁺φ⁾|`.

With normalized charge per eccentric angle1+Σaₖcoskθ, Fourier integration gives potential amplitude aₖ(1+tᵏ)/(2k), energy difference Σaₖ²(1+tᵏ)/(8k), and RMS potential spread [Σaₖ²(1+tᵏ)²/(8k²)]¹ᐟ². Every factor1+tᵏ is positive. Direct physical-distance log-kernel evaluations verify the factorization; separate singular-subtracted kernel quadrature verifies the boundary potential, and independent energy integration checks the positive difference. The finite48-bar display samples a continuous density; it is not a discrete proof.4096-point perimeter quadrature affects only arc-length normalization.

For harmonics, independent angular integration tests all16 basis functions through degree3, including cross terms. The Legendre addition expansion independently reproduces the potential. All181 integer tilts times361 integer azimuths (65,341 rotations) preserve every degree norm to floating-point precision. This finite sweep supplements the orthogonality proof; it is not offered as a proof for arbitrary rotations.

For delta derivatives, exact polynomial moments fix the signs and coefficients. The cubic dipole error is0.03ε² and its quadrupole difference is exact. Separate sine/Gaussian tests check the asymptotic quartering when ε is halved. For the molecular model, direct three-vector charge summation checks386,082 charge/length/angle combinations, and the unrounded water fit reproduces its chosen reference to floating-point precision.
