## Corrections and derivations

**3.2.** Dated LLR calibration0.03808m/yr. For reduced-mass circular orbital momentum L=μ√G(M+m)a, N=(L/2a)ȧ and Cω+L constant. Thus ȧ∝a⁻¹¹ᐟ² and a¹³ᐟ² linear until the outward-transfer assumptions fail. The demo stops at a chosen10-Earth-radius inner cutoff and at the outer ω=n root; it does not plot a NaN point, extrapolate reverse spin, or call formal contact a physical lunar-history prediction. Zero lag is an actual button and gives zero transfer. Curves adapt to all valid plotted values. The day-lengthening readout is the model's lunar-only prediction, not a falsely equated historical total.

**3.3.** Repaired inconsistent factor2: N=(3/2)(k2/Q)GMp²R⁵/a⁶. Model time is I|ω0−n|/N; for ω0≫n it is2Iω0Qa⁶/(3GMp²k2R⁵). For a fixed orbit ω(t)=n+sgn(ω0−n)max(|ω0−n|−Nt/I,0). Slow spins increase; fast spins decrease; neither overshoots. Positive model curves adapt in both axes; no fabricated negative or zero logarithm is shown. Inputs above a deliberately stated2% satellite/planet mass-ratio cutoff or overlapping bodies show an out-of-domain message and hide the handle/history. A short model time does not establish an actual historical lock. Rigidity labels are illustrative solid response values, not a validated rubble-pile law.

**3.4.** Added Fourier/hypothesis qualifications, corrected circular-orbit claim to remove **all** non1:1 resonances, removed unsupported monotone-with-e and secondary7%/55% claims and unreviewed Colombo priority embellishment. Poincaré and ensemble axes include all samples. Old small spin-range classifier falsely called slowly circulating trajectories librating. New diagnostic uses unwrapped γ=θ−pM over last64orbits; span<π gives a **finite-window candidate**, never permanent-capture certification or a claim of chaos. The40 seeded phases under linear damping are not a measured capture probability.

**3.7.** Made zero-obliquity, no-physical-libration, leading-e quadrupole assumptions explicit; modern formula sourced separately from original1979 prediction. Eccentricity damping is τe=2/(21n)(Q/k2)(m/M)(a/R)⁵. At fixed orbital angular momentum and small e, da/a=2e de, so positive dissipated power is (GMm/a)e²/τe, independently giving21/2. Forced-e toggle imposes a constant eccentricity and does not integrate the Laplace resonance. Replaced unsourced0.65–1.25 band with dated0.93–1.17 benchmark, labelled first-order±3e tide amplitude, and included high-power decay/constant-e histories within the axes.

**3.9.** An isotropic speed tail includes inward velocities. Exactly half of the energetic population is outward, while flux integrates v_r: Φ/n=π∫vesc∞v³[π³ᐟ²v0³]⁻¹exp(−v²/v0²)dv=v0(1+λ)e⁻λ/(2√π). The diagnostic Hn/Φ with local H=r/λ is neither an atmospheric lifetime nor a universal lower bound. It assumes an unchanged flux, temperature and local column, ignores resupply and does not solve an atmospheric profile. Moon mode is hypothetical surface Maxwellian emission, not an observed collisional lunar exobase. Smallλ warns that static Jeans assumptions may fail. Log times are computed without underflow and retain values above1000decades; no cap24 is used. Speed density is perkm/s, its window adapts, and the exact integral extends to infinity beyond the visible portion. On a log density axis, values below the display floor are omitted, not replaced by artificial positive densities.
