The example makes no claims about named astronomical data.

## Independent mathematics

For the periodic source, letβ=10⁻⁴, period1, position `x=−βcos(2πt)/(2π)` and velocity `v=βsin(2πt)` in units `c=1`. The light arrival time minus baselineD is `t−x`; the ballistic one is `t−(x+Dv)/(1+v)`. Its derivative has numerator `1+v−(D−x)v′`. On the half-cycle with positive cosine, minimizing that numerator reduces to

`F(s)=1+βs−2πβD sqrt(1−s²)−β²(1−s²)`.

Its second derivative is positive, so derivative bisection finds the unique minimum. The opposite half-cycle cannot create a negative numerator. This supplies a continuous folding criterion independent of the finite displayed polyline. The quoted threshold1/(2πβ) is explicitly approximate.

Synchronization uses equal outgoing/returning durations, with reflection settingd and echo time2d in `c=1` units. Every already-set stationary clock reads the same coordinate timet; the circular wavefront geometry is checked directly.

For muons, integrate constant proper decay rate: survival is `exp(−Δτ/τμ)` with `Δτ=h/(βγc)`. The contracted-path computation gives the same proper time. The helper preserves evaluated constants and the distinction between the observed population rates and the teaching model. For the constantγ=8.4 preset, the model survival is intentionally not forced to equal the experimental ratio.

For Einstein's pulses, an independent boost gives two null four-momenta; their total energy isγL and momentumγβL/c for every emission angle. The rest-frame balance subtractsL, yielding the required kinetic-energy difference. The stable formula `γ−1=β²γ²/(γ+1)` avoids cancellation near zero. The small-speed limit identifies the coefficientΔm, without assuming the body's exact total-energy law as the initial premise.

For Compton scattering, an independent calculation starts with `E′=E/[1+(E/mc²)(1−cosθ)]`, forms the recoil momentum vector `p=(E−E′cosθ,−E′sinθ)/c`, and checks the electron shell relation `T²+2mc²T=p²c²` and energy conservation. Wavelength conversion is independently checked in SI. At90° the modern shift is0.0242631023538Å; at180° it is0.0485262047076Å. The direct modern energy conversion is `hc=1239.8419843320025keV·pm`.
