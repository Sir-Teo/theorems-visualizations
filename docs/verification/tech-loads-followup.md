## 4.9: exact sequential coupling

The original `Jr+min(Jfull−Jr,Ft)` interpolated target impulse without a coupled momentum derivation. The new authored thought experiment first performs an instantaneous perfectly inelastic rider/target capture, then allows a bounded dissipative internal horse-to-joined-body force. It is explicitly separate from measurements of stirrup effectiveness.

With mr80,mt80,mh450kg, joined mass M=mr+mt begins at u0=mr·v/M. Internal impulse is `I=min(Ft,(v−u0)/(1/mh+1/M))`. Joined/horse speeds are `u=u0+I/M` and `w=v−I/mh`. Target impulse is `mt·u`; **only mt/M of the additional internal impulse becomes target impulse**. The ceiling is exactly the all-masses inelastic value. Independent tests conserve total momentum, derive capture plus relative-motion energy loss, recover equal speeds at saturation and zero-force behavior, and cover every control corner/off-grid time.
