## Numerical and physical scope

1. **Intensity/flux:** a uniformly bright sphere with d>R, unchanged frequency and vacuum propagation. The exact angular radius is asin(R/d), solid angle is 2π[1−sqrt(1−R²/d²)], and detector flux is πI(R/d)². An independent angular integral compares measured and predicted flux. The angular disk is a cone cross-section, not an incorrectly normalized all-sky solid-angle map.
2. **Extinction:** seeded 256-sample exponential first-interaction lengths. Unscattered beam removal includes scattering out of the ray; no multiply scattered light is included. Displayed sample transmission has a binomial SE. Changing the native seed slider is fully shareable and deterministic.
3. **Transfer:** optical path t increases in the ray's propagation direction, giving dI/dt = S−I. This is deliberately distinguished from vertical stellar optical depth increasing inward. The formal constant/linear-source solution is compared with independent 1,024-layer midpoint-source transfer.
4. **Random walk:** 128 actual 3D walks with exponential flight lengths, hence E[r²]=2Nℓ², not Nℓ² (the latter belongs to fixed-length flights). Projection is explicitly labeled, and the measured moment uses all three coordinates. The count is fixed rather than an escape simulation. The separate R_sun-scale estimate assumes a homogeneous sphere in the diffusion regime, explicitly not a calibrated solar transport time. The readout gives ensemble SE.
5. **Grey atmosphere:** static plane-parallel LTE, radiative equilibrium, grey opacity and approximate Eddington closure/boundary. The two-thirds layer is model-specific; it is not introduced as the universal mean path or universal photon-origin depth.
6. **Limb:** exact formal integral for a prescribed linear bolometric source; Eddington law compared with an isothermal no-darkening model. No claim of an observed wavelength-independent 40% ratio. The attenuation kernel has mean τ=μ. The source-weighted contribution mean is separately displayed as μ(a+2bμ)/(a+bμ); these coincide only for the isothermal source. An independent first-moment integral checks this distinction. The image uses a palette ramp and actual values, not fabricated surface textures.
7. **Opacity:** separate SI Kramers components with unit Gaunt factors, X=0.7, variable Z and a declared bound-free guillotine factor 10. Bound population factors are held fixed for the illustration; no exact total Rosseland mean is obtained by incorrectly adding separately averaged components. No invented H-minus hump or self-consistent ionization is claimed.
8. **Curve of growth:** numerical unit-area Voigt convolution, with core evaluated by Fourier quadrature and outer wing by real Gaussian/Lorentz convolution. Integrated optical depth Q is proportional to column only at fixed line strength and width. Equivalent width uses a dense sinh-coordinate quadrature over ±1e5 Doppler units and reports a conservative omitted-tail bound. The line panel is explicitly a much narrower visible window.
9. **Broadening:** actual thermal Gaussian, Lorentzian and Voigt convolution for specified hydrogen mass, wavelength and natural rate. Lorentz sampling clusters near its narrow peak, rather than missing it with a coarse fixed linear grid. The log profile ceiling includes the true Lorentz peak; the floor is disclosed. Perturber pressure uses n k T; the impact cross-section is prescribed and is not a calibrated giant/dwarf or Stark-width inference. Finite-window profile area is labeled as less than the full unit-area integral.
