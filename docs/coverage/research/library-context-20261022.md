## Findings that affect future design

**Library work needs explicit routes.** The selected outlines distinguish libraries and bibliography from general information resources. The archived ZA sample adds a useful boundary: studying access to information differs from studying the information's subject. A topical archive may belong with that topic; using a search interface differs from engineering one. The archive is dated evidence, not authority for present-day item classification. Sources: [outline index](https://www.loc.gov/catdir/cpso/lcco/), [archive notice](https://www.loc.gov/aba/publications/FreeLCC/archivedlcc.html), [2024 edition index](https://www.loc.gov/aba/publications/Archived-LCC2024/freelcc.html), [selected Z schedule](https://www.loc.gov/aba/publications/Archived-LCC2024/LCC_Z2024TEXT.pdf).

**Every classification code needs its scheme and edition.** In the inspected examples, S leads to agriculture in LCC and health in the Xwi7xwa scheme; T also has different meanings. Xwi7xwa describes a local British Columbia implementation and distinguishes shelf arrangement from multi-valued subject headings. Atlas domain links must preserve that institutional context and cannot make the two schemes equivalent. Sources: [LCC S](https://www.loc.gov/aba/cataloging/classification/lcco/lcco_s.pdf), [LCC T](https://www.loc.gov/aba/cataloging/classification/lcco/lcco_t.pdf), [Xwi7xwa context](https://xwi7xwa.library.ubc.ca/collections/indigenous-knowledge-organization/), [historical public scheme](https://xwi7xwa-library-10nov2016.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2021/06/2018-MODIFIED-DEER-Public-version.pdf).

**Community authority belongs to the community.** Local Contexts separates provenance, protocols and permissions. The public Te Whakatohea project record provides a situated example with named community context and activity conditions. It does not supply a license for an unspecified recording or appoint the atlas to issue community labels. No song, icon or template is reproduced here. Sources: [label guidance](https://localcontexts.org/labels/about-the-labels/), [public project metadata](https://localcontextshub.org/projects/68ae4264-20b1-4b33-a334-eaca4e58280b/).

**Practical and everyday domains need more than mathematical analogies.** The selected G, S and T outlines provide discovery prompts for movement, recreation, food production, manufacturing, hand making and household practice. Topic-specific research must establish what a learner can actually investigate or do. The outlines alone do not support practical instructions. Sources: [LCC G](https://www.loc.gov/aba/cataloging/classification/lcco/lcco_g.pdf), [LCC S](https://www.loc.gov/aba/cataloging/classification/lcco/lcco_s.pdf), [LCC T](https://www.loc.gov/aba/cataloging/classification/lcco/lcco_t.pdf).
