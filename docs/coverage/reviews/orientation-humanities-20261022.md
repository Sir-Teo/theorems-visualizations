## Cross-domain conclusions and limits

The broad territory lists are clearly editorial framing; the single-source routes are not falsely presented as exhaustive evidence for each entire domain. All fifteen tasks ask readers to do a concrete small thing with an actual named reading, and source-specific preparation prevents a no-prerequisite introduction from implying competence in the professional/practical field. Where a source is geographically, institutionally or theoretically narrow, that narrowness is acknowledged rather than universalized.

The source set is heavily English-language and US/European-institutional. The representation-gap records honestly identify that limit but do not themselves solve it. Keep additional original-language, regional, Indigenous/community-led and practitioner routes as research work; do not count this orientation layer as completion of those perspectives. The current scope does not require falsely claiming a balanced global syllabus before a bounded navigation page can exist.

No human learner, listener, performer, maker, community contributor or screen-reader session was observed by this reviewer. No source assets are licensed merely by this review.
