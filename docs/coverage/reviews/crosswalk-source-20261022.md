Social/behavioural routes are separate from journalism/information, business from law, life/biochemical from all physical science, environment from all natural science, and mathematics/statistics from other natural sciences. All are useful intake choices, not formal equivalences.

**Sensitive knowledge and culture:** Religion routes do not endorse a belief, describe all adherents or resolve legal classification of a particular religious-law programme. None of the heritage candidates is upgraded from review; their local slugs, indexed-only basis, overlap possibilities and missing community context/permissions remain visible.
