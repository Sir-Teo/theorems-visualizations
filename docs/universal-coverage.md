# Universal atlas: coverage map, version 0.1

**Planning inventory · 2026-10-22 · companion to [Master Plan Revision 3](../MASTER_PLAN.md).**

These 30 domains are an initial coverage lens, not a claim that knowledge has 30 natural divisions. They may overlap or split as research and community review reveal gaps. The existing 13 runtime fields and all collection IDs stay unchanged.

“Existing footholds” lists a **primary planning home** for each of the 85 current collections, exactly once, checked against the registry. It describes subject relevance, not completeness or independent acceptance of every item. Cross-listings will be additional mappings to these same IDs. “Add/deepen” is a candidate scope requiring topic-level syllabus research before authoring.

The lenses correct different omissions. Research classifications alone underrepresent domestic, service, creative and embodied knowledge. Library structures help surface established subject families but encode historical institutional choices. Heritage categories make practices visible without imposing universal boundaries.

Domain-specific vocabulary seeds include **AGROVOC** for D10, **MeSH** for D07/D09, and **Getty vocabularies** for D12/D20/D25. Use them to find omissions, aliases and external concept identifiers—not as evidence that an explanation is correct or that a vocabulary can be copied without checking its terms.

## Cross-cutting coverage requirements

- **Place and community:** local, regional, transregional and global contexts, including diasporas and Indigenous knowledge where appropriate sources and community permissions exist. No single continental example represents an entire tradition.
- **Time:** contemporary practice, historical development, discontinuities, obsolete models, and uncertain/disputed dates.
- **Language:** original names/scripts, aliases, source language and translation provenance. Do not equate an English translation with the sole authoritative version.
- **Method:** proof, observation, experiment, interpretation, source criticism, argument, design, performance, and situated practice.
- **Depth and audience:** foundations, general orientation, introductory study, specialist depth, and practical application.
- **Experience:** visual, textual, auditory and physical modes; accessible equivalents; literacy and bandwidth constraints.
- **Status:** planned, reference-only, authored, independently accepted, redesigned, accessibility-reviewed, evaluated for learning.

Regional, gender, disability, labor and environmental perspectives can appear across many domains. They must not disappear because no single top-level shelf owns them. A coverage-gap entry may propose a new domain if the current map repeatedly hides a subject.

## Choosing the next collections

The first new collections should remedy **missing methods and major empty territories**, rather than simply choose the easiest adjacent quantitative topics. Start with the eight pilots in the master plan; use existing collections for cross-links without claiming they already cover the new field.

After pilots, prioritize a bounded collection when:

1. It fills a documented coverage gap or enables a valuable reader journey.
2. Its learning questions and appropriate exploration method are clear.
3. Sources, lawful media and relevant language/domain reviewers are available.
4. Its syllabus can be independently accepted in a small batch.
5. Its maintenance burden, date/jurisdiction sensitivity and physical-practice limits are understood.

Do not freeze item-level IDs for every proposed subfield now. First create researched overviews and pilot contracts; then freeze new inventories independently of Wave 6. Existing collections can deepen under their current syllabi in parallel.

## Implemented registry for U1

The [version 0.3.0 registry](coverage/registry.json) contains stable planning IDs, names/aliases, scope/exclusions, versioned crosswalks, collection mappings, candidate collections, essential questions, methods, introductory/advanced status, representation gaps, source seeds, proposed stewardship, desk-review dates and review triggers. The registry bibliography remains classification/discovery seeds. The eight bounded pilot topics have topic-level research; a separate [orientation catalog](coverage/overviews.md) now supplies 30 institutional reading routes with exact independent AI review. Six pilots are implemented locally: P01/P02/P03/P05 are locally accepted under bounded AI review, while P07/P08 remain in review. No whole-domain syllabus, accepted production coverage or specialist/human review is implied.

Each collection mapping declares **primary**, **cross-listed**, or **reference-only**. All 85 existing collections have exactly one primary home; eight justified cross-listings add routes without increasing the canonical count. The [validator](../scripts/validate-coverage.mjs) checks references, pinned category inventories, brief files and planning-only states. This file and the JSON registry remain planning artifacts, not runtime manifests or content-acceptance ledgers. Reviewers are proposed roles with no assigned people or approvals.
