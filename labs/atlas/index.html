New formats · 6 pilot explorations

Ways to explore knowledge

Read documents, compare interpretations, build arguments and inspect measurements. These local previews keep their sources and limits in view.

01 · History

What can two documents establish? →

Compare a conditional diplomatic proposal with a public argument. Decide what the documents say, what you infer, and what this pair cannot establish.

Reviewed for local preview.

Screen-reader and learner sessions have not been performed.

02 · Literature

One pond, two translations →

Compare two historical English versions of Bashō’s poem. Locate a difference, distinguish observation from interpretation, and support a reading without declaring one final meaning.

Reviewed for local preview.

Screen-reader and learner sessions have not been performed.

03 · Philosophy and religion

Does reciprocity settle a disagreement? →

Build a reasoned response to a disagreement over music and quiet. Separate the source words from assumptions you add, then test what changes when the roles reverse.

Reviewed for local preview.

Local review covers source fidelity, evidence labeling and bounded prototype reuse. No professional specialist, screen-reader or learner session has been performed.

04 · Agriculture

What does a soil-water point measure? →

Explore 278 laboratory observations from three samples in one plot at Prairie du Sac, Wisconsin. Compare raw points, inspect instrument labels, and try a separate synthetic storage calculation.

Reviewed for local preview.

Local review covers source fidelity, evidence labeling and bounded prototype reuse. No professional specialist, screen-reader or learner session has been performed.

05 · Craft

Read and repair a weave plan →

Explore a six-by-six plain-weave plan, introduce a structural error and repair one complete weft sequence. Use the accompanying packet to prepare a real paper demonstration.

Independent review in progress; local preview only.

Digital/source review is separate from unperformed physical and human checks. No real paper sample, sample photograph, practitioner check, second-person reproduction or learner/accessibility session is claimed.

06 · Performance

One phrase, two durations →

Compare two generated versions of an original eight-beat rhythmic phrase. Keep onset positions fixed, inspect short and sustained tone envelopes, then explore tempo without confusing beats with seconds.

Independent review in progress; local preview only.

Digital/source review is separate from performer/educator review and actual screen-reader/learner sessions, which remain unperformed. Release licensing is proposed, not granted.
