D03 · Orientation

Computing and information systems

Computing connects representations, procedures and systems with the people who use and maintain them. A working demonstration is a starting point for questions about correctness, resources and consequences.

What this field asks

  • What behavior is guaranteed, and under which inputs?
  • Who is affected by a system’s failure or design choices?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Algorithms, programming and data
  • Hardware, operating systems and networks
  • Software engineering, security, interaction and societal context

Ways to explore

  • Trace a small input through a procedure.
  • Compare a specification with a failing test case.
  • Map the boundary between a component, its users and its dependencies.

Boundaries and gaps

  • A curriculum-area list is a navigation map, not proof of software competence or security.

Still to build in this atlas

  • Programming practice, architecture, operating systems, networks, databases, software engineering, security, HCI, AI systems
