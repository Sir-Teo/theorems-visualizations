D01 · Orientation

Mathematics

Mathematics studies structures, quantities, patterns and the consequences of assumptions. Begin by separating a useful example from a statement that has been proved for every case.

What this field asks

  • What has been assumed, and where is it used?
  • Which example would disprove the proposed claim?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Number, algebra and discrete structures
  • Geometry, analysis and change
  • Probability, computation and mathematical models

Ways to explore

  • Construct examples and counterexamples before trying a proof.
  • Compare two proofs to see which hypotheses each needs.
  • Translate a practical question into a model, then identify what the model omits.

Boundaries and gaps

  • A finite picture can explain a proof idea without proving the general statement. The linked course begins after calculus.
