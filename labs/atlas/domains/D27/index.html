D27 · Orientation

Theatre, dance, film and performance

Performance inquiry studies actions unfolding for and with audiences. Theatre, dance and film each combine interpretation with material, temporal and collaborative choices.

What this field asks

  • How does an enacted or edited choice change a reading?
  • Which cultural context is lost when a performance is translated or recorded?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Acting, movement, choreography and dramaturgy
  • Staging, cinematography, editing and production
  • Performance traditions, audiences and histories

Ways to explore

  • Compare a text with a documented staging choice.
  • Annotate timing or movement while naming what the record cannot show.
  • Trace the roles and permissions behind a production.

Boundaries and gaps

  • A selected drama calendar offers one entrance, not mastery of theatre, dance or film. A recording does not reproduce the whole live event.
