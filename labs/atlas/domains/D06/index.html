D06 · Orientation

Earth, climate, oceans and geography

Earth inquiry connects rocks, water, air, oceans, places and long histories. Geography also asks how people organize and experience those places.

What this field asks

  • What changes when the spatial or time scale changes?
  • Which parts of a map or reconstruction are observations and which are choices?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Geology, landforms and Earth history
  • Weather, climate, oceans and water
  • Maps, remote sensing and human geography

Ways to explore

  • Compare observations made at different scales.
  • Read a map’s projection, legend and missing information.
  • Trace water or energy through a system with a stated boundary.

Boundaries and gaps

  • NOAA provides an ocean–atmosphere–water entrance. It does not stand for all geology, human geography or local hazard guidance.

Still to build in this atlas

  • Hydrology, soils, geochemistry, ocean observation, remote sensing, hazards, human geography
Place

Language

Practice
