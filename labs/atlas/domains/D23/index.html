D23 · Orientation

Languages, linguistics and communication

Language inquiry studies how people structure and use meaning. It includes language change, variation, acquisition, communication and the relationships between languages and their communities.

What this field asks

  • What pattern is supported by the examples, and what data are missing?
  • How does context change the interpretation of an utterance?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Sounds and signs, words, grammar and meaning
  • Use, variation, change and acquisition
  • Translation, rhetoric, documentation and language learning

Ways to explore

  • Compare forms while keeping their meanings and contexts visible.
  • Test a proposed rule against counterexamples.
  • Trace what a translation changes, preserves or leaves ambiguous.

Boundaries and gaps

  • The linked syllabus is one theoretical entrance. A description of a language is not a ranking of its speakers or a language-learning course.
