D08 · Orientation

Ecology, conservation and environmental practice

Ecology studies organisms in relation to each other and their environments. Conservation adds decisions about what to protect, for whom and under which changing conditions.

What this field asks

  • Which ecological process and which value judgment enter this decision?
  • Who selected the system boundary and the success measure?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Organisms, populations, communities and ecosystems
  • Landscapes, disturbance and change
  • Conservation, restoration and human-environment relationships

Ways to explore

  • Compare field evidence with a stated model.
  • Trace interactions across organism, population and landscape scales.
  • Separate an intervention’s measured effect from its intended objective.

Boundaries and gaps

  • The inspected 4DEE framework explicitly omits traditional ecological knowledge in its current form. This overview cannot supply that missing participation.

Four-Dimensional Ecology Education: The Framework

Ecological Society of America · May 2025 version · source language: en

The framework joins ecological concepts, practices, human-environment interactions and cross-cutting themes. It explicitly says traditional ecological knowledge is not included in the current framework.

Reading scope: Framework introduction; Core Ecological Concepts; explicit TEK limitation · checked 2026-10-22

Source limit: Use as one curricular perspective. Its stated omission cannot be repaired by relabeling this overview as Indigenous knowledge.
