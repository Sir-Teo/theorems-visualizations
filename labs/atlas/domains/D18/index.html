D18 · Orientation

Psychology, mind and human development

Psychology investigates mind, behavior and development through evidence. It connects individual processes with environments, relationships and life histories.

What this field asks

  • What was measured, and how well does it represent the proposed construct?
  • For whom and under which conditions was the finding observed?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Perception, memory, cognition and emotion
  • Development, personality and social behavior
  • Research methods and applied psychological fields

Ways to explore

  • Compare a research question with its operational measure.
  • Separate group-level evidence from claims about an individual.
  • Trace alternative explanations for a behavioral pattern.

Boundaries and gaps

  • A professional overview does not verify every application. This page offers no diagnosis, assessment or treatment.
