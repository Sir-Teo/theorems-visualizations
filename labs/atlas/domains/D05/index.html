D05 · Orientation

Chemistry and materials

Chemistry relates composition and structure to transformations and properties. Materials inquiry adds processing, scale and performance in use.

What this field asks

  • Which observation supports this proposed composition or structure?
  • How would a different preparation change the result?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Atomic and molecular structure, bonding and reactions
  • Chemical analysis, synthesis and biological chemistry
  • Materials, interfaces, processing and scale

Ways to explore

  • Compare candidate structures against stated evidence.
  • Trace matter and energy through a bounded transformation.
  • Relate material processing to a property while naming uncontrolled factors.

Boundaries and gaps

  • A curricular overview is not a laboratory protocol. Laboratory work requires appropriate equipment, training and supervision.

Still to build in this atlas

  • Organic, inorganic, analytical and environmental chemistry; polymers, electrochemistry, laboratory methods
Place

Practice
