D13 · Orientation

Economics and finance

Economics examines production, exchange, distribution and the institutions shaping choices. Finance studies claims on resources over time and under uncertainty.

What this field asks

  • Which outcome does the model explain, and which does it leave outside?
  • How would a different institutional rule change the comparison?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Households, firms, markets and institutions
  • Growth, employment, distribution and economic history
  • Money, finance, uncertainty and public policy

Ways to explore

  • Separate assumptions, model results and empirical evidence.
  • Compare distribution as well as totals.
  • Inspect how a measure was constructed before treating it as wellbeing.

Boundaries and gaps

  • A model is not a forecast by itself. The linked guide is one introductory perspective and provides no personal investment advice.

Still to build in this atlas

  • Economic history, development, labor, public/institutional economics, household finance, competing schools and methods
Place
Regional economic histories and institutional variation need more than one textbook perspective.
Language
Policy and household-finance terms need jurisdictional and language context.
Practice
Measurement, archival work and institutional case studies remain gaps beside mathematical models.
