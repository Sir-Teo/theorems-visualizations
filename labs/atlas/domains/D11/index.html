D11 · Orientation

Engineering, manufacturing and technical systems

Engineering turns needs into systems that must work within constraints. It includes design, construction, operation, maintenance and learning from failure.

What this field asks

  • What would count as meeting the need rather than merely passing a component test?
  • Which interface or failure mode has been left out?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Requirements, models and design tradeoffs
  • Components, interfaces and manufacture
  • Verification, operation, maintenance and retirement

Ways to explore

  • Trace a requirement to a proposed verification.
  • Compare designs under stated cost, performance and use constraints.
  • Inspect how a local failure can affect the larger system.

Boundaries and gaps

  • NASA’s systems approach is a bounded example. It does not authorize construction or replace branch-specific standards and professional review.

Still to build in this atlas

  • Civil, chemical, environmental, industrial and marine engineering; manufacturing, metrology, maintenance, reliability
Place

Language

Practice
