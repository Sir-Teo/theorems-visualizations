D25 · Orientation

Visual arts, design and cultural heritage

Visual arts and design connect material, form, purpose and interpretation. Cultural-heritage inquiry also asks how objects are preserved, attributed, collected and encountered.

What this field asks

  • What is directly visible, and what requires contextual evidence?
  • How do making, ownership and display affect an object’s meaning?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Drawing, painting, sculpture, photography and visual media
  • Design, materials and processes
  • Art histories, museums, conservation and heritage

Ways to explore

  • Describe formal features before assigning an interpretation.
  • Compare materials and design choices in context.
  • Trace provenance separately from a museum label or reconstruction.

Boundaries and gaps

  • Formal analysis is one method, not a universal code of emotional meanings. This page does not reproduce or clear rights to artworks.
