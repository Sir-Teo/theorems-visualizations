D14 · Orientation

Business, accounting and organizations

Organizations coordinate people and resources through records, decisions and routines. Accounting makes some of those commitments visible, while management asks how action is organized.

What this field asks

  • Which record shows a stock, and which shows a flow over time?
  • Whose work or obligation is missing from the chosen measure?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Accounting, reporting and resource flows
  • Operations, logistics and organizational behavior
  • Entrepreneurship, strategy and relationships with stakeholders

Ways to explore

  • Trace an event into the records it changes.
  • Compare a reported outcome with the process that produced it.
  • Map responsibilities and incentives in a bounded case.

Boundaries and gaps

  • The SEC introduction is a dated corporate-reporting lens. It is not current standards advice or a complete account of public, cooperative or nonprofit organizations.
