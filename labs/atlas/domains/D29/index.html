D29 · Orientation

Crafts, trades and everyday practical knowledge

Practical knowledge is developed through making, maintaining and caring in particular conditions. Explanations are useful when they make choices and observable criteria clear.

What this field asks

  • What can be specified in advance, and what must be judged while doing?
  • Which material or contextual difference changes the result?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Textiles, wood, ceramics and other material crafts
  • Repair, maintenance and trades
  • Cooking, domestic work and everyday practices

Ways to explore

  • Describe a sequence and the evidence that each stage succeeded.
  • Compare a planned structure with an inspected outcome.
  • Diagnose a mismatch without pretending a screen model proves skill.

Boundaries and gaps

  • The ARTEX source concerns archaeological textiles, especially ancient Greece. It does not represent all craft traditions or verify physical making.
