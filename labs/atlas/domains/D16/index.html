D16 · Orientation

Law, justice and legal institutions

Law connects institutional authority with rules, interpretation, procedure and contested facts. A legal question must be located in a jurisdiction and time before a source can be applied.

What this field asks

  • Who issued the rule and where does it have authority?
  • Which fact, date or procedural stage would change the analysis?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Legal sources, interpretation and reasoning
  • Public, private and international institutions
  • Procedure, remedies and access to justice

Ways to explore

  • Distinguish an official legal source from commentary about it.
  • Trace a hypothetical claim through facts, authority and procedure.
  • Compare legal systems without assuming one vocabulary fits all of them.

Boundaries and gaps

  • This is legal-literacy orientation, not advice on a dispute. Wex explains US terminology; it is not the controlling source for the UK pilot or any other case.

Still to build in this atlas
