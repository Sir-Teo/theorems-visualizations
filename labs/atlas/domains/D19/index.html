D19 · Orientation

Education and learning

Education asks what people learn, how learning is supported and how opportunities are organized. Inquiry spans classrooms, families, workplaces and learning throughout life.

What this field asks

  • What evidence would show that a learner can use an idea in a new setting?
  • How does the learner’s context affect the opportunity to learn?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Learning, development and motivation
  • Teaching, curriculum and assessment
  • Institutions, inclusion and lifelong learning

Ways to explore

  • Compare a learning goal with the task used to assess it.
  • Inspect what a teaching claim assumes about learners and context.
  • Distinguish engagement, completion and demonstrated understanding.

Boundaries and gaps

  • The research summary motivates questions; it does not establish that this atlas improves learning.

Still to build in this atlas

  • Pedagogy, curriculum, literacy/numeracy, assessment, special/inclusive education, adult learning, educational institutions
Place
School systems, family practices and adult-learning settings need varied local evidence.
Language
Literacy, numeracy and instructional language should not be treated as interchangeable across contexts.
Practice
Teaching observations, inclusive design and learner feedback require real sessions.
