D02 · Orientation

Statistics, data and research methods

Statistics and research methods connect questions to observations and justified conclusions. Study how evidence was produced before deciding what a pattern means.

What this field asks

  • Who or what could have been observed but was not?
  • Which assumption connects the data to the conclusion?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Measurement, sampling and study design
  • Exploration, estimation and uncertainty
  • Causal, qualitative and mixed-method inquiry

Ways to explore

  • Inspect raw records, units and missingness before summarizing.
  • Compare a graph with an alternative grouping or scale.
  • Distinguish a descriptive pattern from a causal explanation.

Boundaries and gaps

  • Exploratory patterns are not automatically confirmatory evidence. The NIST entry supplies one quantitative lens, not the whole field.
