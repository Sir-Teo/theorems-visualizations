D12 · Orientation

Built environments, architecture and planning

Built environments join spatial form, materials, public choices and everyday use. Study a building or settlement as a place inhabited and changed by people.

What this field asks

  • Whose needs are served by a design decision?
  • How did a place’s present form emerge and who can change it?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Buildings, materials and environmental performance
  • Streets, housing, transport and settlement patterns
  • Planning, landscape, heritage and public participation

Ways to explore

  • Read plans, sections and maps at a stated scale.
  • Compare design alternatives and the values built into them.
  • Trace the difference between intended use and observed use.

Boundaries and gaps

  • The linked 2006 course centers the American metropolis. A design discussion is not a permit, engineering approval or current local planning rule.
