D10 · Orientation

Agriculture, food, forestry, fisheries and veterinary knowledge

Food and land-based livelihoods connect organisms, environments, practices and institutions. A useful account names both the biological conditions and the people making decisions.

What this field asks

  • Which local conditions would change this practice’s outcome?
  • How do production choices affect livelihoods and ecosystems?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Crops, soils, water and plant health
  • Animals, forestry, fisheries and veterinary knowledge
  • Food processing, distribution and livelihoods

Ways to explore

  • Trace a food or material through its production system.
  • Compare measured observations with a separately labeled model.
  • Use related vocabulary to find what a first search missed.

Boundaries and gaps

  • AGROVOC is a discovery vocabulary, not evidence that a practice is effective or suitable at a particular site.

Still to build in this atlas
