D09 · Orientation

Health, medicine, care and wellbeing

Health and care connect knowledge of bodies and populations with people’s experiences, services and support. Reading evidence and understanding its limits are useful starting points.

What this field asks

  • What question can this source answer, and for which population?
  • What information would a professional need before applying it to a person?

Territory to explore

An editorial outline, with overlapping boundaries.

  • Anatomy, physiology and disease processes
  • Population health, evidence and prevention
  • Clinical and everyday care, rehabilitation and wellbeing

Ways to explore

  • Distinguish a general explanation from an individual assessment.
  • Check the purpose and source of a medical-test description.
  • Compare information formats and access needs without ranking people’s experiences.

Boundaries and gaps

  • This is a route into health information, not diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for qualified care. The clinical pilot remains gated.

Still to build in this atlas

  • Anatomy, pathology, clinical reasoning, nursing, rehabilitation, dentistry, mental health, disability, nutrition, social care
Place
Health systems, disability experiences and community care vary by setting and need situated evidence.
Language
The current source offers English and Spanish routes; other languages and communication needs require review.
Practice
Supervised observation, clinical judgment and caring relationships cannot be certified by a browser model.
