An original inquiry matrix

Original explanation

Seed access: which source, occasion and household? Diversity: which varieties, sampling frame and year? Labour: whose time and compared with what? Food preferences: whose account? Decision-making: who could choose and who could not? None of these questions is answered merely by adding a variety count.
